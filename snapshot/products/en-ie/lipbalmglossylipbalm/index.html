Questions on the Lip Balm Glossy product page

What is this product? And what can I use it for?

Lip Balm Glossy is a lip balm or lip salve. This is a wax-like substance applied topically to the lips to moisturize and relieve chapped or dry lips.

Ingredients: Petrolatum, Paraffinum Liquidum, Ozokerite, Polyisobutene, Butyrospermum Parkii Butter, Hydrogenated Microcrystalline Cera, Cera Alba, Hydrogenated Palm Acid, Stearyl Stearate, Tocopheryl Acetate, Parfum, Citric Acid, Methylparaben, Propylparaben, Benzyl Alcohol, Limonene.

This product can be used:

• As a gift

• Filling up goodie bags

• To introduce people to your business

Which production techniques do you use?

Pad printing: the printing is done by pressing a silicone stamp (tampon) to the article. Because of the flexibility of the tampon, the shape of the product has little or no effect on the printing.
