Are the lighters sufficient according to the requirements regarding to child safety?

In 2016 the Europe cal commitee took the descision to implement safety measures to prevent children accidents with lighters. All lighters have to meet the requirements of the Europe cal momission as specified in EN13869. All lighters within the HelloPrint product range are produced according to the Europe cal committee.

How should I formalize the file for a lighter?

The colors used are known as Pantone colors. Pantone is a standardized color scheme and is used worldwide. It is, therefore, important to deliver your logo in Pantone colors so that we can start directly with the printing of the lighters.

If the logo consists of Pantone colors, it must be stored as a vector. Vector is to say that the image does not consist pixels, but is build up out of lines.
