Description

Never run out of power again with this rechargeable, high-performance tactical aluminum flashlight. Its sturdy black aluminum alloy casing gives it a robust, tactical look and resists shocks from drops up to 1 meter. With a belt clip for easy carrying and an IPX4 splashproof rating, this 5W LED flashlight delivers 300 lumens for up to 3 hours. It offers a 200-meter beam range and 5 light modes: high, medium, low, strobe, and SOS. Powered by a 2000 mAh lithium battery. Comes in a recycled cardboard gift box with manual.
