Description

This blue-ink pen is made from recycled aluminium. The barrel is finished with a stylish powder coating. The pen features a sturdy metal clip, a plastic tip, and a push button. By using recycled aluminium, fewer new raw materials are consumed during production, resulting in lower energy consumption and less water usage. An environmentally conscious choice.
