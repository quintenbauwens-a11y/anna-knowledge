Description

HelloPrint's Wheat straw pen provides a truly unique writing experience. It is formed from leftover wheat stalks after the grain has been harvested, giving it not only a remarkable appearance but also a wonderful texture! Choose from a selection of four lovely colours and have the distinctive clip customised with your company's text or logo to transform this pen into a powerful marketing tool.
