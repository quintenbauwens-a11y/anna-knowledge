Description

Introducing our 500ml Ocean Bottle – the epitome of sustainability and functionality.

With vacuum insulation, it maintains your beverage's temperature for up to eight hours hot and eighteen hours cold. It features a dual opening for easy cleaning, an anti-leak lid with a convenient carry loop, and is dishwasher safe and BPA-free.

Entirely carbon neutral, each bottle aids coastal communities by repurposing 1,000 plastic bottles.

Packaged in a responsibly sourced recycled cardboard gift box, this bottle is a commitment to a greener planet.
