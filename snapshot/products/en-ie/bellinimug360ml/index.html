What printing techniques are used for this product?

Ceramic Transfer: Your design is printed onto a special paper which is transferred onto the product. This is then heated to create a more durable and permanent design. Ceramic transfer can usually last up to 500 washes and is dishwasher safe.

Ceramic pad printing is a method mainly used to personalise items like mugs with logos, designs, or text. The process involves transferring ink to the surface of the mug using a silicone pad, which allows for precise and detailed printing, even on curved surfaces like the sides of mugs. One of the key benefits of this method is its durability—mugs printed with ceramic pad printing can last through up to 125 dishwasher cycles, making it a popular choice for long-lasting custom designs.
