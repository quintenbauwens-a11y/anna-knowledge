Description

This Masking Tape 50 mm x 50 m is the alternative packaging solution when your want to avoid plastic! This more eco option can be customised completely. Choose from up to 3 colour printing to make sure you brand your packages completely with tape! The tape is 5cm wide.

For best results we are not able to print light and pastel colours on brown tapes.
