What are Photo mugs?

Photo mugs are mugs made of ceramic or porcelain and are used to hold beverages such as tea and coffee. These high quality mugs are perfect for promoting your brand or keeping your memories close.

This product can be used:

• As a gift for friends or family

  • To thank someone

Are the mugs also microwave and dishwasher?

The printed mugs are both microwave and dishwasher safe.

Which production techniques do you use?

Sublimation: the printing is carried out at a high temperature, thereby the eco-friendly water based ink attaches directly to the material. The ink integrates into the material. The material remains flexible and is waterproof.
