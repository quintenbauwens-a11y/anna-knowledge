What materials can I choose from?

The fabric of the beanbag is polyester. When you choose to include the filling for the beanbag, the filling will be EPS granules.

Which production techniques do you use?

For the production of Bean Bags, we use the following technique(s):

Textile printing. With this technique we print the high quality cloth with water-based pigment inks, that provide crisp prints up to 600dpi with vibrant and dazzling colours.

Which carriers are used for the delivery?

For the delivery of Bean Bags, we use our most reliable carriers which, in most cases, are UPS or DPD.

We also use DHL and our own courier service. However, these carriers are only used for certain products (depending on the size, the turnaround and the quantity).
