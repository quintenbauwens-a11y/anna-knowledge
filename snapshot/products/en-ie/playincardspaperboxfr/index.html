Why do we recommend using a white border for playing cards?

We recommend using a white border for playing cards because it helps the cards stay looking clean and new for longer.

When people play card games, the cards are shuffled, handled, and moved around a lot. Over time, this causes the edges of the cards to wear out — they might get little scratches, lose some colour, or start to look frayed.

If the card design goes all the way to the edge (called a "full-bleed design"), any tiny damage becomes very easy to see. For example, if the edges are black or red, you’ll quickly notice white scratches showing through as the coloured ink wears off.

But with a white border, those small signs of damage are much harder to see because they match the border colour. That way, the cards still look nice and neat even after lots of use.

It also helps make sure all cards look the same during a game. If one card has more worn edges than the others, players might recognise it — and that’s not fair. White borders help prevent that by keeping all edges looking alike.
