Questions on the Shop flags product page

What materials can I choose from?

Normal Flag Material: These flags are printed on classic flag canvas with a slight glossy look. The high-quality 110gsm canvas.

Which production techniques do you use?

For the production of Shop flags, we use the following technique(s):

UV-print: The product is printed with eco-friendly UV ink, which is immediately cured with a UV light. Curing ensures that the wet ink doesn't spread once printed, offering sharper quality prints.

Sublimation: the printing is carried out at a high temperature, thereby the eco-friendly water based ink attaches directly to the material. The ink integrates into the material. The material remains flexible and is waterproof.

What is the lifespan of the flags?

The lifespan of the flags is 3 to 4 months with intensive outdoor use. You can extend the life of the flags by bringing them in from time to time in the cases of bad weather.
