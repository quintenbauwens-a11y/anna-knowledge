Questions on the Bamboo & Wheat Straw Pen product page

What is wheatstraw?

Wheatstraw is the residue of the harvested wheat plant in the form of dry stalks. By incorporating wheatstraw into our products, we are redirecting waste materials and embracing a zero-waste approach. This unique and sustainable raw material creates a distinctive and natural look.

What printing techniques are used for this product?

Pad printing: the printing is done by pressing a silicone stamp (tampon) to the article. Because of the flexibility of the tampon, the shape of the product has little or no effect on the printing.
