What printing methods are used for Russell Ultimate Stretch Shirt?

Full colour print: Digital Transfer (Direct to Film Transfer) printing is a cutting-edge method for clothing and textiles. It involves transferring designs from a special film onto the fabric using heat and pressure. It's great for high-resolution, full colour prints with intricate details and is the most economical option for lower quantities.

Embroidery: This method involves stitching thread directly onto the fabric to create your designs or logos. The stitching leaves a textured and durable finish for a touch of sophistication. The embroidered design can have up to 6 colours.

Screen print: This is a popular method for clothing and textiles where ink is pushed through a mesh screen onto the fabric, creating vibrant and long-lasting prints. It's cost-effective available from a quantity of 25 pieces and allows for up to 4 colours.
