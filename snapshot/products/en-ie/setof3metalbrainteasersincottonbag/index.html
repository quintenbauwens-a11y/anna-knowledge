Description

Challenge your mind with these engaging puzzles. This set includes three unique metal brain teasers, perfect for stimulating your cognitive skills. Each puzzle comes neatly packed in a cotton pouch, making it easy to take your brain workout on the go. Ideal for travel or a quick mental break at work. Unleash the fun anytime, anywhere!

The challenge: separate each pair of interlocked metal pieces without bending or forcing them apart. It’s all about finding the right angle, rotation, and a clever twist—simple in appearance, tricky in execution.
