Description

Looking for a handy cooler bag that can be used for any picnic, party, hikes or other outdoor activity? Then you've come to the right place! Questions on the Cooler Fresco Bag product page

What is this product? And what can I use it for?

This product is a cooler bag, which insulates the contents of the bag from outside temperature. Use the bag to keep your cold items cold, and warm items warm! This product is generally used to bring food and drinks outdoors.

What is the print technique used in Cooler Fresco Bag?

Screen printing: in this technique, products are tensioned and then placed under a screen. A layer of ink will be lowered on top of the screen. The ink only adheres to the part of the screen that is permeable (your imprint).
