Description

This round stress ball made from soft PU foam is a simple and comfortable way to help relieve tension and keep hands occupied. With a diameter of 6 cm, the ball fits comfortably in the hand and is suitable for use in the office, while studying, at home or on the go. Lightweight and easy to carry, this anti-stress ball is available in a range of colours. The stress ball can be customised with laser engraving or 3D digital printing. Laser engraving removes the top layer of the foam to create a precise and permanent design with a slightly recessed finish, without adding colour. 3D digital printing combines high-resolution printing with a transparent resin layer applied over the design, creating a raised, glossy finish with a distinctive three-dimensional effect.

What are the print techniques used for this product?

Laser engraving on foam creates a precise, indented design by removing the top layer of foam material. This process leaves a permanent mark without any added colour, giving the foam a textured and slightly recessed look where the design has been etched.

Digital Print 3D combines high-resolution digital printing with a transparent resin layer applied over the design. The raised, glossy finish creates a striking three-dimensional effect, enhancing colours and details with a premium look and feel.
