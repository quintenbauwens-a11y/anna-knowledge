What printing methods are used for The North Face Antora Jacket?

Vinyl printing on clothing is a technique where a design is applied to the fabric using a thin, flexible vinyl layer that is heat-pressed onto the garment. All vinyl prints on clothing using print colors 1 to 4 are made to stay firmly attached to the fabric and keep their bright look over time. Vinyl is water-resistant, so the print will not come off in rain or windy weather. For the best long-term results, we recommend washing garments inside out at low temperatures and avoiding tumble drying. Using high-quality vinyl materials ensures strong color coverage, durability, and a professional finish.

Full colour print: Digital Transfer (Direct to Film Transfer) printing is a cutting-edge method for clothing and textiles. It involves transferring designs from a special film onto the fabric using heat and pressure. It's great for high-resolution, full colour prints with intricate details and is the most economical option for lower quantities.

Screen print: This is a popular method for clothing and textiles where ink is pushed through a mesh screen onto the fabric, creating vibrant and long-lasting prints. It's cost-effective available from a quantity of 25 pieces and allows for up to 4 colours.
