Description

The Textile Frame Tower is a four-sided display that unveils your message or logo from all angles, creating a captivating visual experience. The fabric is seamlessly integrated using Silicone Edge Graphics (SEG), ensuring vibrant and sharp visuals from all distances. Designed exclusively for indoor use, this tower is a showstopper in public spaces or hospitality settings. Despite being lightweight, it is incredibly sturdy, offering reliable stability. The tower is easy to set up with 36 screws securing the 4 frames and 8 corner joints. Customization is available on request, ensuring your display is tailored to your specific needs. The standard finish is in silver anodized aluminum, with other colours available on request.

File guidelines

Use CMYK as the colour mode.

Prepare your files using CMYK as the colour mode, not RGB. Use colourmode FOGRA39 (ISO Coated v2). If you are unsure of the colour mode of your file, don't worry we can always convert this for you. Keep in mind this can cause the colours to differ from the original file.

Resolution of at least 150 dpi.

Make sure the resolution of the images and graphics in your artwork are at least 150 dpi.

Save your files in uneditable, print-ready file formats.

Save your files in an editable, print-ready file formats (PDF (1.4 or higher), TIFF, EPS, JPG). Deliver your files in one layer: the background layer.

Convert fonts to outlines or embed all the fonts.

Make sure you convert fonts to outlines or embed all the fonts when saving it to PDF.

Questions on the Textile Frame Tower product page

What material is this product made from?

It is made from 100% polyester (PES) woven fabric with a weight of 195 g/m² and a light diffusion coating. The material is B1 fire-rated, making it safe for use at exhibitions and events. It features a dye-sublimation transfer print, where the ink is heat-infused into the fibers for durable, high-quality results. The edges are finished with a sewn silicone strip for easy installation.
