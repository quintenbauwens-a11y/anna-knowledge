What printing techniques are used for this product?

Transfer printing is a method where a design is printed on transfer paper or film, then transferred onto a surface using heat and pressure. It allows for precise, high-quality designs on fabrics, ceramics, and more. This process enables customization and is popular for promotional items, team uniforms, and personalised gifts. Transfer prints are typically highly durable and resistant to fading, cracking, and peeling. The design remains intact even with regular use and exposure to various conditions.
