Description

Replace your traditional wooden pencil with this one-of-a-kind alternative. It boasts a writing length of up to 20,000 metres using a graphite tip to emulate the pencil experience perfectly. Plus, with an eraser on the end, you're free to make mistakes and erase them, just like you would with a regular pencil. It wears down so slowly that it will outlast up to 100 traditional wooden pencils!
