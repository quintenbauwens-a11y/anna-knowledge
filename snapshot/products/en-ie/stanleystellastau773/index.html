What is this product?

Tote bags are very handy bags to carry your shopping or to use as a day to day bag. They can be carried comfortably and easily over your shoulder and also can be personalised with a print of your choice! The fabric is light and 160 gsm. The top edge is double folded for strength and a clean finish look. The long handles are reinforced with a cross-stitch for both durability and comfort.

Which production techniques do you use?

For the production of Stanley/Stella Light Eco Tote Bag, we use the following technique(s):

Digital (also called Direct-to-Garment or DTG): prints ink directly onto the product. This method allows for easy switching between prints for each product. However, it can be a slower process compared to other printing methods. It's best for smaller quantities (up to 50 pieces) and prints in full colour.
