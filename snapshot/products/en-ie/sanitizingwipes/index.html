Questions on the Hand Sanitising Wipes product page

What is this product? And what can I use it for?

Hand sanitiser are used to desinfect your hands when you cannot wash them with soap and water. Washing your hands regularly is one of the most important thing to do during these Covid-19 times. In fact, our hands are a great transporter of bacterias, germs and virus and is a major vector of contamination. Our hand sanitiser helps you keeping clean hands in any circumstances.

How to use this product?

HelloPrint's Hydroalcoholic or hydro-alcoholic solutions wipes (also known by the acronym SHA or SHAL) are skin aseptic solutions and are used for hand hygiene. They act through direct application to the hands then through friction; rubbing your hands together with the wipes, and are used without water. To use them, simply open a the individual wipe packaging, take the wipes and scrub your hands with it. Once you are done, simply through away the wipes and the packaging in a closed bins. It is a perfect solution to protect your team by placing a stock of wipes at the entrance of your building. These hydro-alcoholic solutions focus on killing bactericidal, virucidal and fungicidal germs. They must be applied to dry and unsoiled hands.

What is sanitising alcohol in the Hand Sanitising Wipes?

Our Hand Sanitising Wipes is 70% ethil alcohol. The alcohol is important as a desinfectant and kills 99.9% of bateria.
