Description

Circular in shape and finished in stainless steel, this decorative ornament hangs from a ribbon and comes packaged in a gift envelope that folds completely flat. That flat-fold design makes it easy to post or tuck away in storage without taking up space. It works well as a seasonal decoration or as a small gift for someone special. The envelope presentation adds a nice finishing touch, ready to hand over or send as is. Sturdy and straightforward in its design, it's built to be brought out and enjoyed again each year.
