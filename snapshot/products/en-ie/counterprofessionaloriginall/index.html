Description

The Counter Professional Original L Black is a portable counter that is easy to transport and assemble, ideal for exhibitions and events. Featuring a sleek, curved black table top equipped with a canal structure sheet and hook Velcro system, this counter ensures a seamless graphic attachment with Velcro. The wooden black table top is thoughtfully packed in a padded bag with a sturdy handle, ensuring convenient transport. The plastic roll and graphic are securely rolled and packed in a cylindrical bag with a cardboard tube and handle, guaranteeing safe transport. Known for its stability, the counter provides plenty of space on the graphic to showcase your brand or product. The additional storage space inside, with optional wooden shelves, makes it perfect for remarkable presentations. The counter’s graphic is printed on Solid Flex PVC-free PET material, an eco-friendly alternative to traditional PVC.

Questions on the Counter Professional Original L Black product page

What material is this product made from?

The printed material is made from Solid Flex PET. It is a high-quality and durable PET material with a silver backside and very high opacity and flatness. The material is about 340 microns thick and is designed as an environmentally friendly alternative to PVC. It is scratch- and moisture-resistant and can be used without lamination while remaining very durable. The graphics are printed with latex ink printers, using a water-based latex ink formula that dries quickly and is fully cured when it comes out of the printer.
