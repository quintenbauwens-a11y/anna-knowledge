Description

This durable pencil can write up to 20,000 meters of text! Additionally, as it is made of sustainable bamboo, it reduces your environmental impact. This pencil has a graphite point to write and can be erased like a normal pencil. The tip does not require sharpening and is super sturdy, meaning that this pencil last up to 100 times longer than its traditional wooden counterpart. Print your personal logo or text in the middle of the pencil and order today!
