Description

Crafted from 90% recycled stainless steel, this insulated 350 ml mug keeps your drinks hot for up to 6 hours or cold for up to 12 hours. Its leakproof and compact design means it fits easily in your bag and most cup holders—perfect for commuting or a busy day out. You can use it one-handed, and the 360° drinking edge lets you sip from any side with ease. When you’re finished, it’s simple to clean and dishwasher safe. The mug is BPA-free and made from stainless steel, polypropylene, and silicone.
