Description

The Larq PureVis 2.0 Self-Cleaning Smart Bottle water bottle is a 680 ml all-in-one solution that combines self-cleaning technology, advanced water filtration, and smart hydration tracking. Using a 2-stage filtration system and UV-C purification, it improves water quality for a cleaner, fresher taste. The built-in UV-C LED activates automatically every two hours to keep the bottle clean and odour-free, or can be manually triggered with the press of a button. Powered by a long-lasting battery, it runs up to 2–3 weeks on a single charge via USB-C. The cap includes a detachable soft-touch handle for easy carrying, while smart sensors track your water intake and sync with the LARQ app for personalized insights. For best performance, filters should be replaced regularly. Made from durable stainless steel.
