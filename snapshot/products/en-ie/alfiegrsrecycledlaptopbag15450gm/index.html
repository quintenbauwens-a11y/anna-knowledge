Description

Combining everyday practicality with an unmistakably retro finish, this bag is made from a soft, ribbed corduroy-style fabric that brings back the seventies while feeling completely current. It comes with short handles for carrying by hand and a longer strap for the shoulder. A reinforced compartment, separate from the main section, holds a laptop up to 15 inches safely, while the zip fastening and internal zip pocket keep a charger, cables or phone organised. Fully lined interior, around 20 litres of capacity. Made from recycled corduroy polyester (450 g/m²) with 24% recycled content and GRS certification — a sustainable option for daily trips to the office, school or college.
