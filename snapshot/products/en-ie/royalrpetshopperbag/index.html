Description

This wide RPET non-woven shopping bag with long handles is perfect for grocery shopping or for going to the beach. Made from recycled PET bottles, it is the sustainable choice when selecting a perfect promotional bag for your organisation. It is also wide and spacious and can contain up to 25 litres.

Which production techniques do you use?

For the production of Recycled Shopping Bag, we use the following technique(s):

Screen printing: in this technique, products are tensioned and then placed under a screen. A layer of ink will be lowered on top of the screen. The ink only adheres to the part of the screen that is permeable (your imprint).
