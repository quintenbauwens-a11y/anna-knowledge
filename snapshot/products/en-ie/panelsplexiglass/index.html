Description

Luxurious appearance, strong and versatile: that is the best way to describe our custom acrylic signs! Plexiglass, also known as Acrylate, Polymethyl methacrylate or Perspex, is lighter, stronger and more durable than standard double glazing. In addition, this material is a so-called thermoplastic, which means that it becomes softer when heated. The material has a luxurious and chic look and is provided with UV prints.

Here you can find the customized submission specifications for contour cutting.

If you want your product to be cut to a custom shape, you will have to add it as a vector shape on top of your design. Make sure the width of the stroke is 1 pt, the colour is a 100% magenta spot and named "cutcontour". You only need to add one contour line. If you want us to adjust it for you, please send the custom shape on a separate page in the desired position.

Please note that bleed is still needed for custom shaped designs, meaning your design must go at least 5 mm past the contour cut line.

Use CMYK as the colour mode.

Prepare your files using CMYK as the colour mode, not RGB. Use colourmode FOGRA39 (ISO Coated v2). If you are unsure of the colour mode of your file, don't worry we can always convert this for you. Keep in mind this can cause the colours to differ from the original file.

White will not be printed.

White colors in your design will not be printed. All objects in your design that are white, will have the color of the material on the final result.

Resolution of at least 150 dpi.

Make sure the resolution of the images and graphics in your artwork are at least 150 dpi.

Questions on the Plexiglass signs product page

What is this product? And what can I use it for?

Plexiglass signs are a transparent, colourless and durable substrate that is made from a cast polymer. They are an excellent alternative to breakable glass, an ideal way to advertise in architectural displays, or use on a counter or cash register. Timeless, modern and sturdy, they can even be used in a reception area.

Which production techniques do you use?

For the production of Plexiglass signs, we use the following technique(s):

UV-print: The product is printed with eco-friendly UV ink, which is immediately cured with a UV light. Curing ensures that the wet ink doesn't spread once printed, offering sharper quality prints.

Which carriers are used for the delivery?

For the delivery of Plexiglass signs, we use our most reliable carriers which, in most cases, are UPS or DPD.

We also use DHL and our own courier service. However, these carriers are only used for certain products (depending on the size, the turnaround and the quantity).

Are Plexiglass signs fit for usage outside?

Plexiglas is suitable for both indoor and outdoor usage.

Can my product have holes in it and if so, where are the holes?

4 holes in the corners: Four holes will be drilled in each corner of your clean cut advertising panel. Perfect for wall suspension or hanging!

What is the diameter of the holes in Plexiglass signs?

The standard diameter of the drilled holes is 5mm.

The distance between the center of the hole and the edge of the panel is a standard 15mm. Beware when ordering a very small sign that this could be drilled into the main area of your artwork.

What finishing options are available for Plexiglass signs?

Contour cut: Cutting your design on the contour lines gives your sign a great finish. Virtually any shape can be cut, creating a unique look! Please note: special design formatting required for this finish!

What finishing is possible?

There are 3 finishing options.

Bright - Ideal for wall panel displays.

Blockout - With a blackout foil included it help block out unwanted light.

Opal - Has a milky white appearance. Makes it ideal for breaking and dimming light from lamps and light bulbs. Creates a nice, diffused light.
