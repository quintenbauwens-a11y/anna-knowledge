Questions on the Logo Letters product page

What is this product? And what can I use it for?

Logo Letters are milk chocolate bars in the shape of letters of the alphabet. You can print your logo with edible ink on top of a marzipan plate which sits on top of the chocolate.

Which production techniques do you use?

Print your logo on top of the marzipan plate in full colour using edible ink.

On what positions can I print my Logo Letters?

The print can be horizontal or vertical, depending on how you design it on the template. Please make sure you are happy with the direction of the printing before confirming your artwork.

What brand of chocolate are the letters made from?

Barry Callebaut

Are there any potential allergens in the chocolate ingredients?

This product contains soy, milk, and may contain traces of nuts.

How should the chocolate be stored?

Chocolate should be stored in a cool, dry, odorless, and dark place, between 12 and 20 degrees Celsius. Preferably, it should be kept in its original packaging. Important: Never store chocolate in the refrigerator.
