What printing techniques are used for this product?

Pad printing: the printing is done by pressing a silicone stamp (tampon) to the article. Because of the flexibility of the tampon, the shape of the product has little or no effect on the printing.

Screen printing: in this technique, products are tensioned and then placed under a screen. A layer of ink will be lowered on top of the screen. The ink only adheres to the part of the screen that is permeable (your imprint).

What is RCS and why is it important?

RCS, or the Recycled Claim Standard, is a global certification for tracking recycled raw materials through the supply chain. It verifies the presence and amount of recycled content in a final product. This is crucial for ensuring transparency and authenticity in claims of sustainability, promoting ethical manufacturing practices, and encouraging the use of recycled materials, thereby reducing environmental impact.
