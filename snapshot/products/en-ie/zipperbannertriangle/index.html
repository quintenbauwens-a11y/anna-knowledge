Description

The Zipper-Banner Triangle stands out with its unique triangular shape and impressive size, offering the perfect balance between a flag and a banner. With a height of no less than 2.6 metres, this eye-catching display guarantees maximum visibility and impact, especially when choosing the double-sided option. Whether you prefer single-sided or double-sided printing, your message will always leave a strong impression. The banner is quick and easy to assemble within minutes, thanks to its lightweight tubular aluminium frame and wrinkle-free fabric cover. The integrated zipper on the side allows you to effortlessly slide the graphic over the frame and replace it just as quickly whenever needed. Updating your design is as simple as unzipping and reapplying a new print, making the Zipper-Banner Triangle a flexible and efficient solution for any promotional setting.

File guidelines

Use CMYK as the colour mode.

Prepare your files using CMYK as the colour mode, not RGB. Use colourmode FOGRA39 (ISO Coated v2). If you are unsure of the colour mode of your file, don't worry we can always convert this for you. Keep in mind this can cause the colours to differ from the original file.

Resolution of at least 150 dpi.

Make sure the resolution of the images and graphics in your artwork are at least 150 dpi.

Check your artwork on overprint.

Make sure no elements in your artwork are set on overprint unless you want this effect in your design. You can read more about overprint in our Help Centre.

Save your files in uneditable, print-ready file formats.

Save your files in an editable, print-ready file formats (PDF (1.4 or higher), TIFF, EPS, JPG). Deliver your files in one layer: the background layer.

Questions on the Zipper-Banner Triangle product page

What material is this product made from?

The material is made of 100% polyester (PES). It is a soft, wrinkle-free knitted fabric with a thickness of 230 g/m². The fabric is B1 fire rated, making it safe for exhibitions and events. It is printed using dye sublimation, which gives sharp, high-quality photographic images with strong colors that will not crack or fade.
