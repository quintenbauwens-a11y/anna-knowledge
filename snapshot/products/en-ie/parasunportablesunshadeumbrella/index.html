Description

This convenient umbrella opens up to 150 cm wide and features a UV-coated lining for extra sun protection. Made from durable 210T polyester, it’s lightweight and easy to carry, and comes with a storage pouch. The height is easily adjustable, and the pointed end inserts directly into sand or soil. Perfect for beach days, festivals, picnics, or relaxing in the garden. Quick to set up and simple to use all summer long.

File guidelines

Save your files in uneditable, print-ready file formats.

You can download our guides and templates in the template section. Remember to delete the template layers before exporting your design to PDF.

If you already have a design, no worries; we accept other file formats, including JPEG, PNG, or EPS.

For photographic content, high-resolution JPEGs are recommended, preferably without compression. For graphics or text-based content, we recommend print-ready, vector-based PDFs. For content that is a combination of graphics and photography, we suggest using print-ready PDFs to ensure the best quality.

Submit your file in Vector (1-4 colours or embroidery) or in Pixels (Full colour print)

The type of file you need to upload for this product depends on the number of colours you would like to print the product with. When you choose to print your product with solid colours, we need a vector file to ensure the print quality. When you choose to print your product in full-colour, we need a pixel file. Not sure if you have a Vector or Pixel file? Or do you want to know how to create it? Please read our Pixels vs. Vector article.

Use the right colours (PMS or CMYK).

When you choose to print a product with solid colours, the design must be supplied with Pantone Solid colours. If you choose for a full-colour print, then your file should be supplied in CMYK. You can find out here more about the different colour modes.
