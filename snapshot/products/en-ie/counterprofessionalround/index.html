Description

The Counter Professional Round is a portable compact counter with a playful round shape, ideal for exhibitions and events. Featuring a sleek round black table top equipped with a canal structure sheet and hook Velcro system, this counter ensures a seamless graphic attachment with Velcro. The round wooden black table top is carefully packed in a padded bag with a sturdy handle, ensuring convenient transport. The plastic roll and graphic are securely rolled and packed in a cylindrical bag with a cardboard tube and handle, guaranteeing safe transport. Solid, stylish, and with a 360° graphic, it’s the perfect all-rounder for any occasion. The counter is closed, offering no internal storage space, making it a simple yet attractive attribute for your presentation. The graphic is printed on Solid Flex PVC-free PET material, an eco-friendly alternative to traditional PVC.

File guidelines

Use CMYK as the colour mode.

Prepare your files using CMYK as the colour mode, not RGB. Use colourmode FOGRA39 (ISO Coated v2). If you are unsure of the colour mode of your file, don't worry we can always convert this for you. Keep in mind this can cause the colours to differ from the original file.

Resolution of at least 150 dpi.

Make sure the resolution of the images and graphics in your artwork are at least 150 dpi.

Save your files in uneditable, print-ready file formats.

Save your files in an editable, print-ready file formats (PDF (1.4 or higher), TIFF, EPS, JPG). Deliver your files in one layer: the background layer.

Check your artwork on overprint.

Make sure no elements in your artwork are set on overprint unless you want this effect in your design. You can read more about overprint in our Help Centre.

Questions on the Counter Professional Round product page

What material is this product made from?

The printed material is made from Solid Flex PET. It is a high-quality and durable PET material with a silver backside and very high opacity and flatness. The material is about 340 microns thick and is designed as an environmentally friendly alternative to PVC. It is scratch- and moisture-resistant and can be used without lamination while remaining very durable. The graphics are printed with latex ink printers, using a water-based latex ink formula that dries quickly and is fully cured when it comes out of the printer.
