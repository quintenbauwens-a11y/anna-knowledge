What is this product? And what can I use it for?

Pad printing: the printing is done by pressing a silicone stamp (tampon) to the article. Because of the flexibility of the tampon, the shape of the product has little or no effect on the printing.

Digital printing: A digital process where the ink is directly applied onto the surface, allowing for a full-colour replication of almost any colour in the spectrum. The biggest benefit of this technique is the speed of printing and the capability of full-colour printing on all sides.
