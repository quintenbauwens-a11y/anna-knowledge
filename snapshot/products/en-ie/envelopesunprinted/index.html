Which papertypes are used for the production of envelopes?

120gsm offset: EA4, C4 - 90gsm offset: EA5, C56, C5 - 80gsm offset: C6, DL, C56

80gsm offset: A budget offset paper.

90gsm offset: The most chosen offset paper type, especially for letterheads.

120gsm offset: Mainly chosen when a writable stock is needed.

What is a gummed or self-adhesive strip?

Gummed strip: The gummed strip is situated on the envelope flap. Simply moisten the strip for convenient closing. Gummed envelopes are compatible with automated machines.

Self-adhesive strip: Envelopes with self-adhesive strips are extremely easy to close. Simply remove the protective film to reveal the self-adhesive strip.

Which envelope size should I choose?

EA4: 22x31.2cm. Size of a paper A4

C4: 22.9x32.4cm. Large enough for multiple A4 papers

EA56: 22x11cm. Size of a tri folded A4

C56: 22.9x11.4cm. Large enough for multiple trifolded A4 or, one tri folded A4 and an extra (EA56) envelope

C5: 22.9x16.2cm. Large enough for multiple single fold A4 paper or, one single fold A4 and an extra (EA5) envelope

C6: 16.2x11.4cm. For the standard greeting card sizes

As a reference: A4 paper is 21x29.7cm (normal print paper)
