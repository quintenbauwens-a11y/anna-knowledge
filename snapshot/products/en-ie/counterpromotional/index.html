Description

The Unprinted Counter Promotional is a practical and eye-catching solution for product demonstrations, events, and promotional campaigns. Designed to create strong visual impact, this promotional counter features a tall 2-meter display board that ensures your branding and messaging stand out in any environment. The entire surface can be fully covered with sticker foil (vinyl), allowing you to apply custom graphics across the counter and the top card for maximum visibility. The structure is easy to assemble and just as simple to dismantle, making it convenient to transport and reuse at different locations. The set includes the hardware, a white plastic shelf, a sturdy tabletop, and a soft carrying bag for effortless mobility. The tabletop supports up to 10 kg, while the internal shelf can hold up to 5 kg. Graphics are not included, giving you full flexibility to personalise the counter with your own design.
