Description

Counter with Shelf is a sleek and practical event counter designed to bring professional style and functionality to trade shows, exhibitions, and promotional events.

The sturdy aluminium frame features a smart collapsible design that allows for quick setup, effortless transport, and compact storage, making it ideal for businesses that attend events regularly. The counter includes a durable wooden tabletop available in standard colours together with a matching shelf, providing a clean and professional appearance while offering useful storage space. The tabletop can support up to 30 kg and the internal shelf up to 10 kg, making it suitable for displaying products, brochures, or presentation materials. An open backside allows easy access to stored items during events.

Thanks to the Velcro graphic attachment system, graphics can be changed in seconds so you can quickly adapt your branding for different campaigns or exhibitions.

For maximum convenience, Counter with Shelf can be packed into the included wheeled trolley, ensuring easy transport between events while maintaining a professional and eye-catching presentation wherever you go.

File guidelines

Save your files in uneditable, print-ready file formats.

You can download our guides and templates in the template section. Remember to delete the template layers before exporting your design to PDF.

If you already have a design, no worries; we accept other file formats, including JPEG, PNG, or EPS.

For photographic content, high-resolution JPEGs are recommended, preferably without compression. For graphics or text-based content, we recommend print-ready, vector-based PDFs. For content that is a combination of graphics and photography, we suggest using print-ready PDFs to ensure the best quality.

Resolution of at least 150 dpi.

Make sure the resolution of the images and graphics in your artwork are at least 150 dpi.

Add 10 mm bleed.

To allow leeway for cutting differences in production, always add a bleed of 10 mm around all edges of your design. Make sure colours, background pictures and layouts extend into the bleed area so as to avoid leaving white lines as a result of cutting tolerances. You can submit your file with crop marks and printer's marks.

Keep in mind the safety margins.

Keep all important text and images within the safety margins which are displayed in the templates.

Use CMYK as the colour mode.

Prepare your files using CMYK as the colour mode, not RGB. Use colourmode FOGRA39 (ISO Coated v2). If you are unsure of the colour mode of your file, don't worry we can always convert this for you. Keep in mind this can cause the colours to differ from the original file.

Keep 4 mm safety margin

For this product, the minimum font size is 12 pt. To avoid any issues, we recommend the following:

  • Fonts must be embedded or outlined.
  • If your design contains small black text, make sure it is 100 % black (Cyan: 0 Magenta: 0 Yellow: 0 Black: 100).

Note that we don't check spelling or typographical errors.
