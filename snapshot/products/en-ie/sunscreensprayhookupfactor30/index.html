What are Sunscreen Spray HookUp SPF30?

Sunscreen Spray HookUp SPF30 is a photoprotective topical product for the skin that absorbs/reflects some of the sun's ultraviolet (UV) radiation and thus helps protect against sunburn and most importantly, helps to prevent against skin cancer.

Ingredients: Water, Octinoxate, Octisalate, Oxybenzone, Titanium dioxide, C12-15 alkyl benzoate, Caprylic/capric triglyceride, Cetearyl Alcohol, Isopropyl Myristate, Ceteth-25, Glyceryl Stearate, Propylparaben, Methylparaben, Dmdm hydantoin, Fragrance, Glycerin.

Which production techniques do you use?

Pad printing: the printing is done by pressing a silicone stamp (tampon) to the article. Because of the flexibility of the tampon, the shape of the product has little or no effect on the printing.
