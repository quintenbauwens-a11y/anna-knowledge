What is this product? And what can I use it for?

Hand Soap 500ml are used to desinfect your hands when you cannot wash them with soap and water. Washing your hands regularly is one of the most important thing to do during these Covid-19 times. In fact, our hands are a great transporter of bacterias, germs and virus and is a major vector of contamination. Our hand sanitiser helps you keeping clean hands in any circumstances.

Thanks to its convenient format, our hand soap is perfect to place in the bathroom or the kitchen of your shop or office. To use it, just follow the guidelines regarding how to wash your hands properly: Wet your hands, apply some soap, scrub your hands for at least 20 seconds, rinse your hands with clean water and dry them with a clean towel!
