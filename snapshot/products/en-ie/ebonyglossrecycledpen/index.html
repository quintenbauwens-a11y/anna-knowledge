Which production techniques do you use?

Laser engraving with a UV laser (ultraviolet laser) uses a laser that emits light in the ultraviolet spectrum (wavelengths around 355 nanometers). UV lasers are different from CO2 lasers in that they have a much shorter wavelength, which allows them to focus on a much smaller spot size, resulting in higher precision. UV lasers are excellent for engraving delicate materials, such as certain plastics, glass, and ceramics, because they cause minimal thermal damage. The UV laser’s photochemical process (breaking molecular bonds with photons) results in high-quality engravings with smooth edges and minimal heat-affected zones.
