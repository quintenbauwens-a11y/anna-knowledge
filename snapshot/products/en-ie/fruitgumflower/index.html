Description

The all-time favourite promotional item! These cheerful fruit gum flowers contain 10% fruit juice concentrate and are made with natural aromas and colouring plant extracts. Each piece bursts with flavour and charm, making them a perfect handout at events or to promote your brand in a sweet way. Packed in a stylish white sachet personalised with your design. Surprise and delight your audience with a colourful, fruity treat!

Ingredients: Glucose syrup, sugar, water, gelatine, white grape juice concentrate, acidifier: citric acid; plant extracts (spirulina, safflower, elderberry, red radish), glazing agent: carnauba wax; aromas, natural fruit sweetness, apple, apricot and pear juice concentrate.
