What is this product? And what can I use it for?

The Backpack Eco-friendly Large is a roller-top cooler backpack made of RPET Polyester. It is available in two colours, grey and black. The backpack is perfectly suited for outdoors activities such as hiking, picnics and even food delivery thanks to its cooler compartment, multiple pockets, foam-padded back and a large carrying capacity of 22 litres!
