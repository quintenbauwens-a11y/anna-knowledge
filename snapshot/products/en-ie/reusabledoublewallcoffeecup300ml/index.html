Description

Stop using disposable items and contribute to a more sustainable world by bringing your own cup. Our lightweight, durable coffee cups are perfect for on the go. They are double-walled to keep your drink warm longer, reusable, and made from 100% recyclable, eco-friendly PP material. Plus, they are food-safe approved up to 100 degrees Celsius. The screw lid means you don't have to worry about the lid coming off.

Note: These cups are only suitable for hand wash and have a capacity of 300 ml.

What printing techniques are used for this product?

Pad printing: the printing is done by pressing a silicone stamp (tampon) to the article. Because of the flexibility of the tampon, the shape of the product has little or no effect on the printing.
