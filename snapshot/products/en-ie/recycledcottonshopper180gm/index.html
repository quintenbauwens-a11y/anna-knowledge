File guidelines

Save your files in uneditable, print-ready file formats.

If you already have a design, no worries; we accept other file formats, including JPEG, PNG, or EPS.

For photographic content, high-resolution JPEGs are recommended, preferably without compression. For graphics or text-based content, we recommend print-ready, vector-based PDFs. For content that is a combination of graphics and photography, we suggest using print-ready PDFs to ensure the best quality.

Submit your file in Vector (1-4 colours or embroidery) or in Pixels (Full colour print)

The type of file you need to upload for this product depends on the number of colours you would like to print the product with. When you choose to print your product with solid colours, we need a vector file to ensure the print quality. When you choose to print your product in full-colour, we need a pixel file. Which production techniques do you use?

For the production of Recycled Cotton Bag 180 g/m²s, we use the following technique(s):

Screen printing: in this technique, products are tensioned and then placed under a screen. A layer of ink will be lowered on top of the screen. The ink only adheres to the part of the screen that is permeable (your imprint).

Full colour print: Digital Transfer (Direct to Film Transfer) printing is a cutting-edge method for clothing and textiles. It involves transferring designs from a special film onto the fabric using heat and pressure. It's great for high-resolution, full colour prints with intricate details and is the most economical option for lower quantities.
