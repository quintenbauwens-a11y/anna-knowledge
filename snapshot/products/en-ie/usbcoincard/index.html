Description

USB Coin Card is a small and thin (2.9 mm only) USB stick. It offers unique possibilities for round logo's. Both sides can be printed with high-quality full color print. Individual prints are possible as well.

Specifications

  • Size: 43 x 43 x 2.9 mm
  • Weight: 4 g
  • Maximum imprint: 43 mm diameter
  • Print: Double-sided | Full colour
  • Core Color: White

Print options

  • Product colours: White
  • Print colours: Full colour
  • Print methods: Digital print
