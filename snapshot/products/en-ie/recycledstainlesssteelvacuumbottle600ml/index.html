What does BPA Free mean?

BPA stands for bisphenol A, which is a chemical that is commonly used in the production of certain types of plastic products, including food containers and water bottles. However, BPA has been linked to potential health risks, such as hormone disruption, and many people prefer to avoid it.

Therefore, "BPA-free" means that the product is made without the use of BPA, or that it contains only trace amounts of BPA that are considered safe for human consumption. Products labeled as BPA-free are typically made with alternative materials that do not contain this chemical.

What printing techniques are used for this product?

Pad printing: the printing is done by pressing a silicone stamp (tampon) to the article. Because of the flexibility of the tampon, the shape of the product has little or no effect on the printing.

Laser engraving: A modern engraving method where the material is processed with a laser beam. This operation is applicable to virtually all metals.
