What is GaN in charging plugs?

GaN (Gallium Nitride) is a material used in modern chargers. It makes them smaller, faster, and more efficient than traditional chargers. GaN chargers waste less energy, stay cooler, and can charge devices like phones and laptops quickly without overheating. They’re a big upgrade from older silicon chargers!
