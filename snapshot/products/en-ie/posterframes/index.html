What is this product? And what can I use it for?

A snap frame is a real eye-catcher for your shop or restaurant. The snap frames are designed so that you can easily and quickly change your poster. The snap frames are available in different types and sizes to match our posters.

This product can be used:

• To introduce people to your business

Decorate the entrance of your store or hospitality premises
