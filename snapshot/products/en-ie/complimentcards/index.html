What a Compliment Card Says That an Invoice Can't

Two Names for One Small Card

Compliment slips go by two names, and plenty of businesses ask for compliment cards instead. Whatever you call it, the job is the same: a small printed card with your logo and contact details goes in with an online order, a letter or an invoice, and turns a routine piece of business post into a quick, personal thank you.

An invoice tells a customer what they owe. A compliment slip beside it tells them somebody noticed the order, without anyone writing a full letter each time. A first name in pen, a signature or one short line is usually all it takes, and the printed card does the rest. If the customer files the card with the paperwork, your phone number and website stay close at hand too.

A Design Customers Recognise

Keep the front simple: your logo, your brand colours and a short thank you, with a phone number or website underneath. When the card matches your packaging, your invoices and your email signature, customers know at a glance who it is from, before they have read a word. A clean layout also leaves space for the handwritten line that makes each slip feel meant for them.

Logo colours print clearly on the silk papers, with little reflection. On silk you can add a gloss or matt lamination on both sides for an extra protective layer, or a velvet lamination that feels very smooth to the touch.

Where a Compliment Slip Fits, and Where It Doesn't

Compliment slips are made for everyday business post: the online order going into its box, the documents heading to a client, the invoice at the end of the month. They are not the card for every occasion. For the cards that carry a bigger message, such as a Christmas greeting to your regulars, our greeting cards are the better fit, and the invite to a shop opening goes on one of our cards and invitations. The compliment slip stays the small, everyday card in the box or the envelope.

Frequently Asked Questions for Compliment Slips

Are compliment cards available in A5?

Yes, A5 measures 148 x 210 mm and sits alongside A6 105 x 148 mm, DL 210 x 99 mm and the slim 216 x 92 mm. Of the four, A5 gives the most room, handy for a longer thank you or a short offer for the next order.

Can the reverse side be printed as well?

Yes, double-sided full colour printing puts your design on the front and the back, while single-sided printing leaves the back blank. A second message fits on the reverse with double-sided printing, for example the same greeting as Gaeilge.

What envelope options are there?

You can order the printed cards on their own, or add blank white envelopes to match. The envelopes arrive unprinted, so you write the address by hand or add your own label, which is useful when a card goes out by post without an order.

Can I write a personal note on each card?

Any of the writable papers will do: 300 gsm sulfate cardboard or uncoated offset in 90, 100, 120 or 300 gsm. Uncoated paper takes a pen easily, so a first name, a signature or a quick line for a regular customer is simple to add.

Is there a recycled paper option?

Yes, RecyStar® Nature 300 gsm is a recycled paper. It sits with Bio Top 90 gsm and Bio Top 300 gsm among the eco papers, so a business that wants its slips on recycled stock can choose it and keep the same design.

Which papers have a metallic look?

Metallic gold 300 gsm and metallic silver 300 gsm reflect the light with a metallic sheen, and pearl marble 300 gsm adds a shimmering look. These special papers give the cards of a salon, a jeweller or a gift shop a look that catches the light.

File guidelines

Save your files in uneditable, print-ready file formats.

You can download our guides and templates in the template section. Remember to delete the template layers before exporting your design to PDF.

If you already have a design, no worries; we accept other file formats, including JPEG, PNG, or EPS.

For photographic content, high-resolution JPEGs are recommended, preferably without compression. For graphics or text-based content, we recommend print-ready, vector-based PDFs. For content that is a combination of graphics and photography, we suggest using print-ready PDFs to ensure the best quality.

Use CMYK as the colour mode.

Prepare your files using CMYK as the colour mode, not RGB. Use colourmode FOGRA39 (ISO Coated v2). If you are unsure of the colour mode of your file, don't worry we can always convert this for you. Keep in mind this can cause the colours to differ from the original file.

Resolution of at least 300 dpi.

Make sure the resolution of the images and graphics in your artwork are at least 300 dpi.

Keep 4 mm safety margin

During production, there might be a slight cut deviation. To avoid any issues, we recommend the following:

  • Add a 3 mm bleed around all edges of your design.
  • Keep important elements 4 mm away from the edge or folding lines.
  • Avoid using borders or frames on smaller formats like labels or business cards.

Keep 4 mm safety margin

For this product, the minimum font size is 6 pt. To avoid any issues, we recommend the following:

  • Fonts must be embedded or outlined.
  • If your design contains small black text, make sure it is 100 % black (Cyan: 0 Magenta: 0 Yellow: 0 Black: 100).

Note that we don't check spelling or typographical errors.

Check line thickness.

Lines should be at least 0.25 pt. Negative lines should be at least 0.5 pt.

Check your artwork on overprint.

Make sure no elements in your artwork are set on overprint unless you want this effect in your design. You can read more about overprint in our Help Centre.

Make sure the ink coverage isn't higher than 300%.

Force Colour Images to CMYK with a 260% ink limit (in Photoshop > Convert Colour Profile > Photoshop 5 Default CMYK). Make sure the ink coverage isn't higher than 300%.

Using a border/frame is not recommended

It is recommended to count with a margin of error when stacks of copies are trimmed during the production process. This slight cutting deviation is usually not noticeable, nevertheless, we recommend avoiding adding borders smaller than 20 mm in your design for any product smaller than an A4 (210 x 297 mm). Otherwise, the design might end up looking off-centre.
