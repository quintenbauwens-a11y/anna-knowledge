Description

Give away tasty mints with this conveniently sized credit card shaped mint dispenser! Choose your favourite colour, personalise it with your logo or design and enjoy the freshness. These mints are a great advertising tool to hand out in the streets, as a giveaway gift, or as a token of appreciation. So, what are you waiting for? Order yours today!
