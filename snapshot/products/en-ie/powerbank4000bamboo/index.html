Questions on the Bamboo Power Bank 4000 mAh product page

What is this product? And what can I use it for?

This product is a power bank (a portable battery), which is used to charge your device(s) when on the go without requiring a power socket. Simply charge the power bank in advance and and carry it around whether you go! Your design can be printed in up to 4 colours.

How can I tell how much it would charge my device?

By simply taking the capacity (mAh) of your phone (which you can find on the back or online) and dividing it by the capacity of the power bank, you can find out how many full chargers your powerbank can supply. Generally speaking, 4000mAh is enough to charge the battery of most smartphones fully. The iPhone 12 has a battery capacity of 2815mAh which (4000/2815) can be charged more than once.

Are these powerbanks guaranteed to be safe and compatible with my device?

All our powerbanks are compliant with European commission guidelines, which means they are both safe to use (high quality lithium batteries), and compatible with the standard Voltage used in appliances, and therefore compatible with any smartphone, tablet, digital camera, GPS or music player.

Can I charge my device using the ports available in this powerbank?

Most of our powerbanks come with a USB-C, Micro-USB and regular USB port. Please read the product specs carefully to see if you charger can be used with this device.
