Description

With its long straps and soft corduroy feel, this bag brings a retro seventies vibe right up to date. Made from recycled corduroy polyester at 450 g/m², it’s a super-soft ribbed velour that’s bang on trend. The zip closure keeps your essentials safe, and there’s a handy inside zip pocket for your phone, keys or wallet. On the outside, you’ll find an extra slip pocket, perfect for your water bottle or other bits and bobs. With a roomy 30-litre capacity, it’s ideal for a day at the beach, a trip out, or as a large shopping bag. The bag is GRS certified and made with 24% recycled materials.
