Description

Ready for a challenge? This bamboo brain teaser puzzle will keep your mind sharp. Housed in a recycled paper box, it's perfect for those who love sustainable play. Since bamboo is a natural product, slight variations in size and shape make each puzzle unique and gives your brain something new to work with every time!

The challenge: disassemble this interlocking bamboo cube and put it back together piece by piece. It looks simple, but only one precise combination will form the perfect cube again.
