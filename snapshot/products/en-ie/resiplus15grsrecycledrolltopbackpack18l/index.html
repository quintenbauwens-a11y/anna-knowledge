Description

Crafted from top-quality recycled PU leather with a matching RPET lining, this roll-top backpack blends contemporary design with long-lasting strength. The front zipped pocket with a waterproof zip offers easy, secure access, and the padded, adjustable shoulder straps with a breathable back panel ensure comfort all day long.
