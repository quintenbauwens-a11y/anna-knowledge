What is RCS and why is it important?

RCS, or the Recycled Claim Standard, is a global certification for tracking recycled raw materials through the supply chain. It verifies the presence and amount of recycled content in a final product. This is crucial for ensuring transparency and authenticity in claims of sustainability, promoting ethical manufacturing practices, and encouraging the use of recycled materials, thereby reducing environmental impact.
