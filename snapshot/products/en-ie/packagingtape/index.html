Description

Give your packaging the attention it deserves with custom printed tape!

Tough, waterproof, and temperature-resistant (-10°C to +45°C), this tape keeps your parcels secure while showing off your brand. With up to three colours to print your logo or design, each roll becomes a rolling billboard. Thanks to protective varnish and durable lamination, your branding stays vibrant from start to delivery.

Sized to fit standard 50mm wide/75mm core dispensers, it’s perfect for shipping with style.
