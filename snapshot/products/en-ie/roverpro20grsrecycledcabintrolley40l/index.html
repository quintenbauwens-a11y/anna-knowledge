What does GRS stand for?

The Global Recycled Standard is a certification standard for products made with recycled content. The standard sets requirements for the traceability of recycled materials, social and environmental practices, and labeling of products that use recycled materials. The GRS is often used by companies to demonstrate their commitment to sustainable and responsible sourcing of recycled materials.
