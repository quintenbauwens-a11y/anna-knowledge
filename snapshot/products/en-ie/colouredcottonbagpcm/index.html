Questions on the Coloured Cotton Bag product page

What is this product? And what can I use it for?

Cotton bags are the replacement of your plastic bags. These bags are used very often as promotion material. They have a great look and are available in many different colours!

This product can be used:

• Promote at fairs and events

• To introduce people to your business

• To represent your company's identity or brand

I can't find the product I'm looking for. Can I request a quotation?

The Coloured Cotton Bag that is chosen in most cases can be selected via our website.

If your material type, weight, quantity, size or other specification is not listed, you can request a quotation. We aim to get back to you within 2 working days.

Depending on the complexity, this can take a bit longer.

What material are cotton bags made of?

The cotton bags are made of 100% cotton. Cotton is a 100% natural product and therefore biodregradble.

How do I have to supply my artwork?

It is possible to supply your design files in multiple ways. For the best result, we kindly ask you to please supply your files in vector. If the logo consists of Pantone colours, it should be saved as vector. Vector means that the design isn't made of pixels, but out of lines.

Can the print positions deviate from the print?

Printing Coloured Cotton Bag is different from printing paper, not a single Coloured Cotton Bag is the same. It can be that there is a small deviation in the position of the print. This is minimal and often not visible in the final result.
