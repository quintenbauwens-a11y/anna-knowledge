Description

Give your product the standout packaging it deserves with our custom printed doypacks. These versatile stand-up pouches are perfect for a wide variety of dry products, including tea, herbs, powders, protein shakes, snacks, nuts, grains, dry cosmetics, sample packs and pet food.

Designed to help your brand shine, they offer great durability, stable presentation and excellent protection for your contents. With full personalisation available, you can create packaging that perfectly reflects your brand identity. Whether you want something bold and eye-catching or clean and minimalistic, these doypacks turn your design into a professional-quality product that captures attention instantly. Their stable stand-up shape ensures your items are displayed beautifully, making your packaging a powerful part of your marketing.

Doypacks are not suitable for liquids, high-moisture products, hot fillings straight after production, sharp materials or solvent-rich contents, as these may affect or damage the laminate.

Flexible, durable and visually impactful, these pouches give your product the premium look it deserves. If you're looking for personalised packaging that is easy to brand and built to impress, custom doypacks are the ideal solution to bring your product to life.

File guidelines

Resolution of at least 300 dpi.

Make sure the resolution of the images and graphics in your artwork are at least 300 dpi.

Use CMYK as the colour mode.

Prepare your files using CMYK as the colour mode, not RGB. Use colourmode FOGRA39 (ISO Coated v2). If you are unsure of the colour mode of your file, don't worry we can always convert this for you. Keep in mind this can cause the colours to differ from the original file.

Check your artwork on overprint.

Make sure no elements in your artwork are set on overprint unless you want this effect in your design. You can read more about overprint in our Help Centre.

Save your files in uneditable, print-ready file formats.

Save your files in an editable, print-ready file formats (PDF (1.4 or higher), TIFF, EPS, JPG). Deliver your files in one layer: the background layer.

Keep 4 mm safety margin

Keep all important text and images at least 4 mm away from the edge of the finished size.
