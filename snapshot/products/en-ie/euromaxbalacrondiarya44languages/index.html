Description

Multilingual desk diary in NL, EN, FR and DE, designed for practical everyday planning at home or in the office. It features a durable padded imitation-leather Balacron cover, finished with the year discreetly blind embossed on the front. Inside, 80 gsm cream-coloured wood-free writing paper and thread-sewn binding provide a quality finish. The spacious weekly layout is spread over two pages, giving plenty of room for appointments, plans and notes. Annual planners for the current and following year are included, along with additional pages containing useful information. The extra-large 20 x 1.3 x 27.2 cm format provides ample space for organised day-to-day planning.

What are the print techniques used for this product?

If no colour is specified, blind debossing is used automatically.

If a colour is specified, foil stamping is used. Maximum 1 colour, supplied in PMS.

Blind debossing creates a recessed impression directly in the material, without ink or foil.

Foil stamping applies coloured foil to the surface, most commonly white, gold or silver.
