Which production techniques do you use?

Embroidery: This method involves stitching thread directly onto the fabric to create your designs or logos. The stitching leaves a textured and durable finish for a touch of sophistication. The embroidered design can have up to 6 colours.

Full colour print: Digital Transfer (Direct to Film Transfer) printing is a cutting-edge method for clothing and textiles. It involves transferring designs from a special film onto the fabric using heat and pressure. It's great for high-resolution, full colour prints with intricate details and is the most economical option for lower quantities.

Unprinted samples: Want to check out the quality of your clothing before ordering a full print run? No problem! Simply choose the unprinted option.

What does GRS stand for?

The Global Recycled Standard is a certification standard for products made with recycled content. The standard sets requirements for the traceability of recycled materials, social and environmental practices, and labeling of products that use recycled materials. The GRS is often used by companies to demonstrate their commitment to sustainable and responsible sourcing of recycled materials.
