Full colour print: Digital Transfer (Direct to Film Transfer) printing is a cutting-edge method for clothing and textiles. Embroidery: This method involves stitching thread directly onto the fabric to create your designs or logos. The stitching leaves a textured and durable finish for a touch of sophistication. The embroidered design can have up to 6 colours.
