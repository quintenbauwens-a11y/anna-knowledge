Description

This spacious bento lunch box from Mepal is ideal for bringing a fresh and varied lunch with you wherever you go. Featuring two separate compartments, two removable bento trays, and a handy fork, it keeps your food organised and easy to access. Perfect for sandwiches, salads, snacks, or leftovers, with an airtight seal to keep everything fresh throughout the day. Built to last for everyday use, the bento trays are microwave safe (without the lid) for quick reheating. All parts are dishwasher safe (up to 65 °C). Capacity: 1500 ml. Made from polypropylene.
