Description

Give your luggage a touch of class! This luggage tag features a clear window and is easy to attach to any suitcase or bag. Made from pineapple imitation leather, it looks and feels like real leather but is made from pineapple leaves, a byproduct of pineapple cultivation. This eco-friendly material is soft, attractive, and durable—perfect for marking your bag in style.
