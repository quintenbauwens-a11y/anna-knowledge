Description

A picture says more than 1000 words. Whether it's a photo of your colleague, a family photo or a team photo; print it directly on chocolate! The chocolate photo consists of 24 chocolate cubes, made of a layer of white and milk chocolate. The chocolate is made from sustainably produced Cocoa Horizons chocolate from the Belgian brand Barry Callebaut. Your photo is printed on the chocolate using a special printing process with edible dyes. The chocolate comes in a luxurious red gift box, making it a perfect gift.

How should the chocolate be stored?
