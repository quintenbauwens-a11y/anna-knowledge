Description

Made from 90% recycled stainless steel, this insulated 500 ml tea mug keeps drinks hot for up to 6 hours or cold for up to 12 hours. The integrated tea filter makes it easy to brew fresh loose-leaf tea, tea bags or herbs wherever you are. Its leakproof design and compact shape mean it fits standard car cup holders, while the carry handle makes it convenient to take along on the go. After use, the mug is easy to clean and dishwasher safe. It's BPA-free and made from stainless steel, polypropylene and silicone.
