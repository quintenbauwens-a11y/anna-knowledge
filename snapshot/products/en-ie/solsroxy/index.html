What printing methods are used for SOL'S Roxy Jacket?

Full colour print: Digital Transfer (Direct to Film Transfer) printing is a cutting-edge method for clothing and textiles. Screen print: This is a popular method for clothing and textiles where ink is pushed through a mesh screen onto the fabric, creating vibrant and long-lasting prints. It's cost-effective available from a quantity of 25 pieces and allows for up to 4 colours.
