Description

Gift boxes from HelloPrint are ideal for giving away special presents. It gives each gift a super luxury appeal. Hot-foil your brand, image or words in the box. HelloPrint presents gift boxes in various colors. They are glued and only need to be folded. The box includes inlay for partitioning and bottles!


The gift boxes of HelloPrint are made of GD2 300gsm + E flute. The boxes are foldable and there is no need for any glue. The box is finished with hot-foil and delivered unfolded. The boxes include inlays for bottles.
