File guidelines

Save your files in uneditable, print-ready file formats.

You can download our guides and templates in the template section. Remember to delete the template layers before exporting your design to PDF.

If you already have a design, no worries; we accept other file formats, including JPEG, PNG, or EPS.

For photographic content, high-resolution JPEGs are recommended, preferably without compression. For graphics or text-based content, we recommend print-ready, vector-based PDFs. For content that is a combination of graphics and photography, we suggest using print-ready PDFs to ensure the best quality.

Here you can find the customized submission specifications for contour cutting.

If you want your product to be cut to a custom shape, you will have to add it as a vector shape on top of your design. Make sure the width of the stroke is 1 pt, the colour is a 100% magenta spot and named "cutcontour". You only need to add one contour line. If you want us to adjust it for you, please send the custom shape on a separate page in the desired position.

Please note that bleed is still needed for custom shaped designs, meaning your design must go at least 5 mm past the contour cut line.

Use CMYK as the colour mode.

Prepare your files using CMYK as the colour mode, not RGB. Use colourmode FOGRA39 (ISO Coated v2). If you are unsure of the colour mode of your file, don't worry we can always convert this for you. Keep in mind this can cause the colours to differ from the original file.

White will not be printed.

White colors in your design will not be printed. All objects in your design that are white, will have the color of the material on the final result.

Resolution of at least 150 dpi.

Make sure the resolution of the images and graphics in your artwork are at least 150 dpi.

Keep 4 mm safety margin

During production, there might be a slight cut deviation. To avoid any issues, we recommend the following:

  • Add a 5 mm bleed around all edges of your design.
  • Keep important elements 10 mm away from the edge or folding lines.
  • Avoid using borders or frames on smaller formats like labels.

Questions on the Printed Channel Plate Signs product page

What is this product? And what can I use it for?

Panels are one of our most-sold products and are a very popular promotional material. A panel is a sign for advertisement of your own store or project with your own design on it.

What is the diameter of the holes in Printed Channel Plate Signs?

The standard diameter of the drilled holes is 5mm.

Where are the drilled holes in the Printed Channel Plate Signs located?

The distance between the center of the hole and the edge of the panel is a standard 15mm. Beware when ordering a very small sign that this could be drilled into the main area of your artwork.

Can Printed Channel Plate Signs be used outside?

All panels are made for inside use. For outside use, it is best to choose one of the following materials: Trespa, correx, painted or brushed aluminium.

Why do we charge shipping costs?

Delivery costs are charges incurred to cover expenses related to shipping your order(s). These costs vary based on factors such as distance, weight, size, speed, and destination. You will find the specific delivery cost for your product clearly indicated during the ordering process.

Note: Keep in mind that as a standard practice, delivery is free if your order exceeds £60.00. However, certain exclusions apply. Products like custom banners, flags, and panels always incur an additional delivery charge.

Is this panel fire resistant?

Our Foamex signs panels are B1 certified, which makes them fire retardant.

The B1 fire retardant certificate certifies that certain materials are "hardly flammable," making them safe for indoor use. Such materials have a low risk of catching fire and do not easily spread flames, providing additional protection against potential hazards.
