How do I mount a hanging plate?

  1. Bring panel and suspension plate to room temperature (20°C).
  2. Clean and dry the area where the panel is to be placed.
  3. Remove protective foil & the back of the sticker from panel.
  4. Attach suspension plate to panel, pressing firmly for 20 seconds.
  5. Place a heavy object (15 kg) on suspension plate for 1 hour.
  6. Wait 24 hours before hanging panel.

How do I mount the doublesided wall tape?

  1. Clean the surface with alcohol or silicone-free cleaner.
  2. Apply the wall tape, press for at least 5 seconds.
  3. Attach the object to surface, press for at least 5 seconds.

Will the colours on my look just like the colours on my screen?

Digital photos and printed photos will look different due to the processing of colours in a different colour mode. RGB is the colour mode used in phone or camera photos, but it is not ideal for printing as it can appear overly dark. Therefore, we convert your RGB files to the recommended CMYK colour mode, which is also used by printers to achieve accurate colour tones by mixing colours. While this produces a more precise outcome, the shades may differ by up to 10% compared to the proof or a previous order.

File guidelines

For preparing your file we recommend to use our template.

For preparing your file we recommend using our template. This is the best way to deliver your file perfectly.

Resolution of at least 150 dpi.

Make sure the resolution of the images and graphics in your artwork are at least 150 dpi.

Add 5 mm bleed.

To allow leeway for cutting differences in production, always add a bleed of 5 mm around all edges of your design. Make sure colours, background pictures and layouts extend into the bleed area so as to avoid leaving white lines as a result of cutting tolerances. You can submit your file with crop marks and printer's marks.

Use CMYK as the colour mode.

Prepare your files using CMYK as the colour mode, not RGB. Use colourmode FOGRA39 (ISO Coated v2). If you are unsure of the colour mode of your file, don't worry we can always convert this for you. Keep in mind this can cause the colours to differ from the original file.

Check your artwork on overprint.

Make sure no elements in your artwork are set on overprint unless you want this effect in your design. You can read more about overprint in our Help Centre.

Save your files in uneditable, print-ready file formats.

Save your files in an editable, print-ready file formats (PDF (1.4 or higher), TIFF, EPS, JPG). Deliver your files in one layer: the background layer.
