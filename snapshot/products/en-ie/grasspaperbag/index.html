Description

Grass Paper Bags are the perfect alternative to plastic bags. Made from recycled paper with grass fibres, these bags are waiting to be personalised with your brand! Complete with twisted handles and available in numerous sizes, they are ideal for shops, takeaways or events. Order today to make a big impact on your customers while having little impact on the environment!

Questions on the Grass Paper Bags product page

What are Grass Paper Bags?

Grass Paper Bags are made from grass paper, are fully recyclable and are perfect for all industries. Consumers and companies are increasingly environmentally conscious, show that you are too!

What printing techniques are used for this product?

Screen printing: in this technique, products are tensioned and then placed under a screen. A layer of ink will be lowered on top of the screen. The ink only adheres to the part of the screen that is permeable (your imprint).

Which carriers are used for the delivery?

For the delivery of Grass Paper Bags, we use our most reliable carriers which, in most cases, are UPS or DPD.

How do I have to supply my artwork?

It is possible to supply your design files in multiple ways. For the best result, we kindly ask you to please supply your files in vector. If the logo consists of Pantone colours, it should be saved as vector. Vector means that the design isn't made of pixels, but out of lines.
