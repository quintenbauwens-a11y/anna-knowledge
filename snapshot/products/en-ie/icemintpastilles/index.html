Cool Ice pastilles are sugar-free, velvety oval mints (13 x 10 mm) with an intense ice flavour. Packed in a sleek white bag, they're perfect for trade shows, mailings or giveaways. Only 1.6 kcal per mint – for a refreshing breath anytime, anywhere.
