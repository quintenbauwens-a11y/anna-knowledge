Description

For university, school, work or your next trip, we've got what you need!

The iconic Bobby Soft Anti-theft Backpack features hidden RFID protected pockets, no front access and hidden zippers for extra safety. They even have a safe zip puller on the main compartment! Inside, you can easily organise your gear with a padded 15.6" laptop compartment, notebook pocket, smart pockets and a keychain clip. The top of the backpack is expandable, giving additional space. This backpack is also equipped with an integrated USB charging port and water-repellent material.

Comfortable to carry and customised with your desired print, what more could you want?
