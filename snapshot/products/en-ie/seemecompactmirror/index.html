Questions on the Compact Mirrors product page

What are Compact Mirrors?

Compact Mirrors are small mirrors that can be opened and closed. They are small enough that you can take them in your bag, purse, or even pocket. The plastic holder serves as a protective cover for the glass mirror.

This product can be used:

• As a gift

• Filling up goodie bags

• To introduce people to your business

Which production techniques do you use?

Digital printing: Uses four colours (CMYK) with toners and inks to print digital files. This option is comparable to a large home printer, and is cheaper than Litho printing for smaller quantities. This is because there are fewer starting costs, fewer components involved, and no ink drying time required.

Pad printing: the printing is done by pressing a silicone stamp (tampon) to the article. Because of the flexibility of the tampon, the shape of the product has little or no effect on the printing.
