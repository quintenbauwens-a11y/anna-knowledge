Which production techniques do you use?

Embroidery: An embroidery sewing machine is the perfect tool for creating stunning patterns and designs on textile products. The first step in the process involves transforming your design into a digital embroidery card. This card is then used to operate the machine and transfer your design onto the chosen textile product.
