Description

This five-panel cap features embroidered eyelets for extra ventilation, keeping you cool and comfortable no matter what you’re doing. Crafted from 100% cotton twill at 175 g/m², it offers both durability and a soft, breathable touch. The adjustable fabric hook and loop fastening makes it easy to get the perfect fit, and with a 58 cm head circumference, it’s ideal for most head sizes.

For content that is a combination of graphics and photography, we suggest using print-ready PDFs to ensure the best quality.

Submit your file in Vector (1-4 colours or embroidery) or in Pixels (Full colour print)
