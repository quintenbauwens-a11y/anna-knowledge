Which production techniques do you use?

Screen printing: in this technique, products are tensioned and then placed under a screen. A layer of ink will be lowered on top of the screen. The ink only adheres to the part of the screen that is permeable (your imprint).

Which carriers are used for the delivery?

How do I have to supply my artwork?

It is possible to supply your design files in multiple ways. For the best result, we kindly ask you to please supply your files in vector. If the logo consists of Pantone colours, it should be saved as vector. Vector means that the design isn't made of pixels, but out of lines.

What colour is the inside of the bags?

The inner colour of the bags is dependent on the outer colour of the bag itself. If the bag is Brown, Black, Dark blue, Green, or Burgundy, it will have a brown inner colour. On the other hand, if the bag is White, Blue, Fuchsia, Orange, Silver (with a white handle), or Gold (with a white handle), it will have a white inner colour.
