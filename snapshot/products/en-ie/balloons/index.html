Description

Looking to make a statement at your next event? Elevate your branding game with custom printed balloons! These balloons come in eight vibrant colours and are made of natural latex, a renewable material drawn from rubber trees. Choose to add ribbons, so you can hand the balloons out or tie them down. With your unique logo or design displayed, these personalised balloons are guaranteed to make a lasting impression!

Note: For double-sided printing, we will print the same artwork twice. Request a quote if you need two different designs.

Your balloon will be in one solid colour of your choice, while your logo or design will be printed in a separate colour based on your uploaded artwork.

Which production techniques do you use?

For the production of Standard Balloons (30 cm), we use the following technique(s):

Screen printing: in this technique, products are tensioned and then placed under a screen. A layer of ink will be lowered on top of the screen. The ink only adheres to the part of the screen that is permeable (your imprint).
