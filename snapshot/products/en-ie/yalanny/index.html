Description

This 130 x 160 cm fleece blanket adds a festive touch to your home with its cheerful red colour and classic white Christmas motifs. Made from soft RPET polyester, the 200 g/m² fleece keeps you snug and warm on cold Irish evenings. Thanks to its anti-pilling finish, the blanket stays looking fresh and tidy, even after repeated washes. The RPET label highlights its eco-friendly, recycled polyester origins. Perfect for draping over your favourite chair or wrapping up on the sofa, it’s a thoughtful Christmas gift for family, friends, or colleagues.
