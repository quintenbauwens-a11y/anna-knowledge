For what sizes brochures are the brochure holders suitable?

The brochure holders are suitable for brochures with a size up and including A4. Larger sizes will not fit.

Are brochure holders easy to move?

The brochure holders with 5 and 6 compartments are foldable. The brochure holder with 6 compartments is also available with a matching case. These brochure holders are easy to carry and move. The brochure holder with 9 compartments is not foldable, but this one is really light weight. Slightly less practical than folding, but still easy to move.
