Questions on the Towels (Entirely Full Colour) product page

Which production techniques do you use?

For the production of Towels (Entirely Full Colour), we use the following technique(s):

Sublimation: the printing is carried out at a high temperature, thereby the eco-friendly water based ink attaches directly to the material. The ink integrates into the material. The material remains flexible and is waterproof.

How do I have to supply my artwork?

It is possible to supply your design files in multiple ways. For the best result, we kindly ask you to please supply your files in vector. If the logo consists of Pantone colours, it should be saved as vector. Vector means that the design isn't made of pixels, but out of lines.

What are the washing instructions?

Screen printing / embroidery: Wash inside out at a maximum of 40 degrees

Full colour print: Wash inside out at a max of 30 degrees

No chlorine or bleach

Do not dry clean

Do not iron on the print

How long will the print last?

The print will last at least 75 washes. After 75 washes the print may begin to fade in colour.

Can the print positions deviate from the print?

Printing Towels (Entirely Full Colour) is different from printing paper, not a single Towels (Entirely Full Colour) is the same. It can be that there is a small deviation in the position of the print. This is minimal and often not visible when the Towels (Entirely Full Colour) are worn.
