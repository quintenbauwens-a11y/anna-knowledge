Description

This scented candle comes in a round glass holder with a natural bamboo lid, instantly adding a sense of calm and comfort to any space. Made from paraffin wax blended with soy oil and 3% carefully chosen fragrance oils, it features two wicks for an even burn and a warm, inviting glow. Pick from two unique scents: Sandal Rose (light grey), a cosy mix of soft rose and creamy sandalwood with a subtle, luxurious warmth, or Coffee Cloud (brown), a rich coffee aroma with creamy notes and a gentle base of wood and amber. With a burn time of around 40 to 50 hours, it creates a lovely atmosphere and makes a thoughtful gift for any occasion.
