Description

Experience lasting hydration with the Gator Tumbler, a 32oz stainless steel water bottle designed for both convenience and performance. Built with a secure vacuum-seal lid, it ensures your drinks remain at the perfect temperature for hours, whether you prefer ice-cold water or a warm beverage. The tumbler includes a handy straw for effortless sipping, along with a flip-up mouthpiece for smooth, mess-free drinking wherever your day takes you. Durable, reusable, and easy to clean, the Gator Tumbler is perfect for work, travel, outdoor adventures, or everyday use. Stay hydrated in style and enjoy refreshment with every sip.
