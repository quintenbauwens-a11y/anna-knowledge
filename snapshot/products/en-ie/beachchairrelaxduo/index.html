Description

Relax side by side with the Beach Chair Relax Duo, a classic double beach chair designed for comfort, durability, and strong visual appeal. Ideal for terraces, events, chill-out zones, festivals, and promotional environments, this two-person beach chair combines functionality with high-end craftsmanship.

The frame is made from high-quality beech wood with a smooth, sanded finish free of sharp edges. It features three adjustable recline positions and integrated anti-folding plastic safeguards to ensure safe and stable use. The foldable yet sturdy construction supports up to 160 kg, making it suitable for both indoor and outdoor settings.

Thanks to the smart self-change fabric system, updating your design is quick and effortless. Simply slide the wooden fixing bar into the fabric sleeve, wrap the fabric over the frame, and secure it in place. The frame comes with four wooden fixing bars and allows full customisation with a full-colour print, making Beach Chair Relax Duo an excellent choice for branded seating and promotional furniture.

While durable, note that dye sublimation prints may fade slightly over time under direct sunlight due to limited UV exposure.
