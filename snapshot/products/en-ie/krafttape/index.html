Description

Seal your packages in style with this eco-friendly kraft paper tape!

Made from strong paper and natural rubber adhesive, this tape is plastic-free, recyclable and biodegradable — perfect for conscious brands. It’s dustproof, water-resistant and holds boxes up to 10kg with ease. The powerful adhesive forms a secure bond within 2–3 hours and ensures your tape stays put. Your design will look sharp and professional, even in temperatures ranging from -10°C to +45°C. Whether you're going for a modern, classic, or rustic look, this tape adds a stylish finish to every parcel. It fits all standard 50mm tape guns, making it easy to use in any workspace. Make every unboxing a branded experience — sustainably!
