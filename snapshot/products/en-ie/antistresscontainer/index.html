What are the print techniques used for this product?

Laser engraving on foam creates a precise, indented design by removing the top layer of foam material. This process leaves a permanent mark without any added colour, giving the foam a textured and slightly recessed look where the design has been etched.

Digital Print 3D combines high-resolution digital printing with a transparent resin layer applied over the design. The raised, glossy finish creates a striking three-dimensional effect, enhancing colours and details with a premium look and feel.
