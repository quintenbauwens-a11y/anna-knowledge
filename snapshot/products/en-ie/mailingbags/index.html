Description

Looking for an eco-friendly way to send your packages?

Bio Poly Mailers are lightweight bags made from natural materials – and yes, they're waterproof! They're perfect for shipping clothes, books or small boxes, offering great protection from splashes, dust and rough handling. Plus, they’re biodegradable, so your packaging won’t harm the planet.

Just seal with the handy self-adhesive strip and you’re good to go!

Questions on the Bio Poly Mailer product page

What kind of graphic designs can’t be printed on Bio Poly Mailer?

The printing capabilities on this material are restricted due to its tendency to absorb paint, making it challenging to print complex images, gradient designs, or neon colours. Please consider this when designing your artwork.

Why are my Bio Poly Mailer a different colour to the ones that are on your website?

Please be aware that the colour of these mailing bags may not be pure white. The whiteness of the material may vary depending on the vegetable fabric it's made of. If it's corn waste, the colour may have an ivory hue, while beetroot waste can result in a subtle coral shade. If having a perfectly stark white bag is crucial for your needs, bio poly mailers may not be an option for you.
