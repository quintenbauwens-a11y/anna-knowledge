Description

Enjoy your favourite drink wherever you are. This double-walled insulated mug keeps your drinks hot for up to 11 hours or cold for 38. Made mostly from recycled stainless steel and finished with a tough Klean Coat®, it’s perfect for coffee on the move. The leak-proof screw lid with Café Cap stops any spills, and it’s simple to clean. BPA and heavy metal free, it’s a safe and eco-friendly choice. Great for commuting, travelling, or the office.
