Description

Our compact and flat 5,000 mAh power bank is made from durable ABS, making it easy to carry with you wherever you go. With 3-in-1 integrated TPE cables, it's compatible with all mobile devices, ready to use right out of the box. Fully charged, it can recharge your phone up to two times. Equipped with a long-lasting, high-density 5,000 mAh lithium polymer battery, it also features power indicators that show the remaining energy, so you always know when to recharge. Input: 5V/2A, Output: 5V/2A.
