Finishing

  • Without Reinforced Edges or Rings – A trimmed finish on a banner involves cutting the edges of the material to create a neat and clean edge.
  • Rings every 30cm, with reinforcement – Edges are hemmed (folded and sewn to create a clean finish), with rings added every 30 cm for easy hanging. Rings are placed approximately 15 mm from the edge, an internal diameter of 1.20 cm.
  • Rings every 30cm, without reinforcement – A non-hemmed finish with rings every 30 cm on a banner involves leaving the edges raw and adding rings every 30 cm for easy hanging, suitable for short-term use.
  • Rings in the corners, with reinforcement – Edges are hemmed (folded and sewn to create a clean finish), with rings in the corners for easy hanging. Rings are placed approximately 15 mm from the edge of the banner and have an internal diameter of 1.20 cm.
  • Rings in the corners, with reinforcement – A non-hemmed finish with rings in every corner of a banner involves leaving the edges raw and adding rings in each corner for easy hanging indoors or outdoors, suitable for short-term use.
  • With Tunnel on both sides (5cm Diameter) – 8 cm tunnels on both sides, allowing for easy insertion of poles or ropes for secure hanging. Consider tunnel positions in your artwork submission.
  • With Tunnel on left side (5cm Diameter) – A banner with an 8 cm left-side tunnel has a fabric sleeve sewn onto it for inserting a pole/rod. The tunnel helps distribute weight evenly during display.
  • With Tunnel Top & Bottom (5cm Diameter) – 8 cm tunnels on the top and bottom, allowing for easy insertion of poles or ropes for secure hanging. Consider tunnel positions in your artwork submission.
