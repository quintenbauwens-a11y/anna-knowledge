Description

HelloPrint's reusable cups are an essential product for every event! You can fill them with anything you want: water, soft drinks, beer, and even hot drinks like tea and coffee! The cups are made from BPA-free polypropylene, which does not taint the taste, and were created to reduce the amount of plastic waste generated by throwaway plastic cups. Personalise with your logo or design and order today.

Note: If you choose full-colour printing and your design contains white, it will also be printed.
