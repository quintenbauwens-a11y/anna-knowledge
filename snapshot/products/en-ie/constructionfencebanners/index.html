Getting More from Heras Fence Covers on Site

Next to Your Statutory Site Notices

Heras fence banners add project information and branding, but they sit alongside the official site notices rather than replacing them. In Ireland, your PSCS must clearly display the project's HSA notification details on site, and a planning application has its own official site notice. Keep those visible and use the panels around them for the development name and your logo.

Fencing on a Public Road or Footpath

Check with your local council before you fix construction site banners to fencing on a public road or footpath, or before you carry third-party advertising, because a licence or permission may be needed.

Leave QR Codes Off a Construction Mesh Banner

A construction mesh banner is perforated, so a QR code printed on it will not scan. Put the website or a short phone number on the mesh in large type instead, and save QR codes for a panel printed on standard PVC.

Frequently Asked Questions About Heras Fence Banners

Will Heras fence covers fit the panels on my site?

Yes, on standard Heras panels: these measure about 3.5 x 2 m, and Heras fence banners are printed at 338 x 174 cm, a little smaller in both directions. The spare room around the edge leaves space for the rings and for any tie wraps you add.

Are tie wraps included with the banner?

No, not as standard: tie wraps are an option you add when you configure your Heras fence banners, with 20 per banner in black or white. Choose banner only if you already have fixings on site from an earlier job.

Is there a construction mesh banner option for Heras fencing?

Yes, windproof mesh canvas (270 gsm) is one of the four materials you can choose. The perforated fabric lets strong gusts pass through, which helps on open ground or a coastal site. For mesh outside site fencing, see mesh banners.

Which material meets a fire-retardant requirement on site?

Go for standard PVC (510 gsm). PVC is fire-retardant and sturdy enough to move from one site to the next. PVC-free polyester (225 gsm) holds no such certification, so keep it for Heras fence covers on jobs where the PSCS safety and health plan does not call for it.

Is the fencing included with Heras fence covers?

No, HelloPrint supplies the printed banners and the Heras fencing comes from your own yard or your hire firm. One banner is the smallest order, enough for the gate panel on a one-off house build, while a larger housing scheme can brand the whole street frontage.

Can I use construction fence banners on crowd barriers at a match?

Not really: construction fence banners come in one size, 338 x 174 cm, to cover a full Heras panel on a building site. For crowd barriers at a match, pick one of the barrier sizes on the fence banners page instead.
