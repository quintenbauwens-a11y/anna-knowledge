Description

Snack time? Sorted. This cocktail mix is the ultimate crunchy combo of salted crackers and mini snacks – all in fun shapes and sizes. Perfect for sharing, munching during meetings, or adding to your lunch break. It’s a salty, crispy bite in every handful.
