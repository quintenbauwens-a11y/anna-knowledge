Description

Make everyday tasks a breeze with this clever, key-shaped multi-tool. Personalise it with your logo to keep your business in mind every time someone needs a quick fix. An ideal way to stay remembered by clients and partners. This compact tool packs 12 handy functions into a key-shaped design that easily attaches to any keyring. Features include a bike spoke wrench, spanners (sizes 4-10 mm), bottle opener, Phillips and flat screwdrivers, wire bender, and rulers in both centimetres and inches.
