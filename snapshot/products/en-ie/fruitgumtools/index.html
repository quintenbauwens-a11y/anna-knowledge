Description

A builder’s favourite promo treat! These fruity tool-shaped gummies contain 10% real fruit juice, natural flavourings, and colour from plant extracts. Packed in a sleek white pouch ready for your design, they’re perfect for brands in construction, DIY, or tech. Fun, delicious, and memorable.

Ingredients: Glucose syrup, sugar, water, gelatine, white grape juice concentrate, citric acid, plant extracts (spirulina, safflower, elderberry, red radish), carnauba wax, flavourings, natural fruit sweetener, apple, apricot, and pear juice concentrate.
