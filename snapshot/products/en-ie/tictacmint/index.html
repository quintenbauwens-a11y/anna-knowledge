Questions on the Mints TicTac product page

What is this product?

Tic Tac is a brand of small, hard mints manufactured by the Italian company Ferrero. Minty and fresh, they're a household favourite!

Ingredients: sugar, maltodextrin, fructose, thickener (gum arabic), rice starch, anti-caking agent (magnesium salts of fatty acids), flavourings, peppermint oil, glazing agent (carnauba wax).

This product can be used:

• Filling up goodie bags

• As a gift

• To introduce people to your business

Which production techniques do you use?

For the production of Mints TicTac, we use the following technique(s):

Digital printing: Uses four colours (CMYK) with toners and inks to print digital files. This option is comparable to a large home printer, and is cheaper than Litho printing for smaller quantities. This is because there are fewer starting costs, fewer components involved, and no ink drying time required.
