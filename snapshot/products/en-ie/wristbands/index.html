Description

Be event ready with Fabric Wristbands! These fabric wristbands are perfect for any event, no matter how many days it is. They are printed in full colour for maximum exposure of your brand or identity. Choose between our most popular polyester, recycled polyester for an eco event or satin for a softer more luxury feeling your guests will want to wear long after the final act.

The polyester and recycled wristbands include a handy plastic closing, so they will stay firmly attached and are non-transferable. Satin bands are provided with an aluminium clip and special pliers (one per order) to close the wristband.

Important: Every wristbands is printed double sided. In the case that you submit a design for the outer side only, we will duplicate it on the inside to match - making you wristbands look awesome! We also advise submitting your artwork in a repeating pattern as we cannot guarantee the exact placement of the design in a specific part of the lanyard. Does it sound complicated? No problem! Deliver your design and we will contact you if something is not in order. Great, right?!

What is this product? And what can I use it for?

Wristbands are a very populair product in our assortment and are often used as a proof of entrance to an event, party or festival.

What materials can I choose from?

Polyester - a highly durable and resistant fabric, which makes it the most popular choice for Fabric Wristbands.

Recycled polyester - Also known as RPET is an environmentally friendly alternative to polyester. Made from recycled plastics, often plastic bottles, it creates a strong and durable material.

Satin - A premium fabric which is durable whilst also giving a soft and luxury feel.

How do the Fabric Wristbands fasten?

The polyester and recycled wristbands include a handy plastic closing, so they will stay firmly attached and are non-transferable. Satin bands are provided with an aluminium clip and special pliers to close the wristband.

Are Fabric Wristbands water proof?

No, Fabric Wristbands are not water repellent.

The print is durable and will not be damaged by water in normal use.

Are the Fabric Wristbands adjustable?

No, once the wristband has been closed with the fastening clip, it cannot be loosened or removed. The wristbands therefore cannot be removed or transferred, which makes them ideal for events. To remove the bands, they must be cut.
