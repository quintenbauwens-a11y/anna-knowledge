Description

Keep your screens crystal clear on the go with this innovative SmartKosi® mobile cleaner. Featuring a high-performance P-9000® HD microfibre front, it effortlessly removes fingerprints, dust, and smudges from smartphones and tablets without leaving a single streak or scratch. The self-adhesive reverse side allows it to stick securely to the back of any device, ensuring a professional cleaning tool is always within reach whenever you need it.

Customise the front with your full-colour logo or design to turn a practical daily essential into a high-visibility brand ambassador. Designed for longevity, this cleaner is easily washable under running water, making it a durable and reusable alternative to disposable wipes. Whether attached to a phone or a tablet, it’s the ultimate accessory for the modern user who values a flawless display and a smart, functional design.
