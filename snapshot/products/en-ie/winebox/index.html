Description

This versatile wine box can be tailored to accommodate every bottle and label on your store shelves. It is perfect for showcasing a variety of products, including whisky, prosecco, cognac, and olive oil. The wine box features a top-opening design with custom prints that make it ideal for retail displays. To enhance the aesthetic appeal and ensure longevity, the vividness of the print is safeguarded with a thin, slightly glossy film. This makes it an excellent choice for presenting a bottle of wine or other premium liquids in a retail setting, adding a touch of elegance and sophistication to your product presentation.

Questions on the Wine Box product page

Can I use images on the bottom insert of the wine box packaging?

Unfortunately we can’t print on the bottom insert of wine boxes. The lower part of a customised wine box is made from 4 foldable pieces of paper, which are placed onto each other during the assembly process. These pieces create a stabilized bottom for the product inside the box. As a result, this bottom part is not fully visible and a text or image wouldn’t look good. However, you can add a colour there.
