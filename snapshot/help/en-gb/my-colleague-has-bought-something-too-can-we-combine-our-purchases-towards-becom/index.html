My colleague has bought something too. Can we combine our purchases towards becoming a VIP member?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/353

Yes, you can combine your purchases to reach a VIP level by joining a company account.

The annual print spend for the company account is calculated based on the sum of the annual print spends of all current users. Orders placed before users are invited to company accounts are also included in the VIP level calculation.
