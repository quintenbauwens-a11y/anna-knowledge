Why did I receive a payment reminder?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/188

Category: Payment

We send you a payment reminder if we have not yet received a payment for your order. Please note that processing payments can take up to 3 business days.

When the payment term on the invoice has expired, we'll send a reminder via email once every two weeks. It is important to pay on time, otherwise you run the risk of incurring additional costs.

We can then investigate and resolve the issue for you.
