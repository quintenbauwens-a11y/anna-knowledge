Where can I find my order status?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/267

Category: After ordering

To easily track the status of your order, follow these steps:

  1. Click on your name located in the upper right corner of the screen to reveal a dropdown menu.
  2. From the dropdown menu, select "Orders" to access your order history.
  3. Your orders will be listed in chronological order, with the most recent ones displayed first. Look for the "Status" column to determine the current status of each order.

For a more comprehensive view of a specific order, simply click on "Order Details" on the right side of the screen, adjacent to the desired order."
