When do I get free shipping?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/309

Category: Shipping information

Want to save on shipping costs? At HelloPrint we make that easy for you. Spend over 60 pounds excluding VAT and the shipping is on the house. Simply add your favourite business cards, flyers or stationery to your cart and once the total amount reaches 60 pounds excluding VAT, shipping becomes free.

The shipping fees might differ, depending on which product and amounts you are ordering.

Please note: this offer does not apply to products of custom size, such as banners and custom posters, custom stickers etc.
