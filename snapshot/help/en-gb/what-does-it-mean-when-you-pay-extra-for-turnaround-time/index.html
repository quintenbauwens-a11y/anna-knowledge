What does it mean when you pay extra for turnaround time?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/286

Category: Delivery time

When you pay extra for your turnaround, this actually refers to a faster production time. We produce all orders on time, but unfortunately we cannot always guarantee that delivery will be on time where external factors are involved.

At HelloPrint, we’re lucky to have a large international network of production facilities. We work closely with a selection of delivery companies to make sure we can provide the best product, price and service possible.
