How do I increase my credit limit?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/345

Category: Invoice

If you're looking to extend your purchasing power with HelloPrint, increasing your credit limit is an easy process. Here's how it works:

  1. They'll guide you through the necessary steps and ensure you have the information needed, like your Chamber of Commerce number.
  2. Credit Check: We'll do a quick credit assessment to better understand your current financial situation. Don't worry, it's confidential. This helps us determine an appropriate new credit limit for you.
  3. Decision & Notification: Our team reviews the information and decides on your new limit. We'll let you know the outcome right away through your preferred method of contact.
