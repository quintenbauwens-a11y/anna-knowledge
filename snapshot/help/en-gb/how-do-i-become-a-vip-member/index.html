How do I become a VIP member?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/352

You automatically become a VIP member if you’ve spent more than £1,000 in the last 12 months. This discount is automatically calculated and appointed to you. You do not have to do anything additional to be signed up.
