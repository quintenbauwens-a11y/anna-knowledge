I would like to pay later with Billie. Which bank details should I use?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/395

Category: Payment

If you choose to pay later with Klarna or Billie, follow these steps:

1. Select Klarna or Billie as your payment method at checkout.

2. Wait for approval from the payment partner.

3. Receive a payment link from Klarna or Billie with all the necessary payment details.

Important: Always use the bank details provided by Klarna or Billie. Do not make payments to Helloprint’s bank account, as the transaction is processed directly through the selected payment partner.

Haven’t received a payment link or email?

•    Check your spam folder in your email. Sometimes the message may end up there.

•    Wait a few minutes, as the link may take some time to be sent.

For Billie payments:

  • Go directly to the Billie Buyer Portal via this link: https://portal.billie.io/login.
  • Request an access link using the email address associated with your order.
  • You will receive an email granting access to the customer portal.
  • Inside the portal, you can view all your invoices and their payment statuses.
  • From the Billie Buyer Portal, you can also contact Billie’s customer service directly for further assistance.
