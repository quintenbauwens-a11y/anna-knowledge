What is my current VIP-level?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/357

You can find your current VIP member level and your annual spend in your account:

1. Simply go to your account by hovering over your name in the top right corner of the screen and click on 'My Account'.

2. Go to 'My VIP' on the left side of your screen.
