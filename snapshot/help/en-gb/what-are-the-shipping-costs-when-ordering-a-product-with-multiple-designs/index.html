What are the shipping costs when ordering a product with multiple designs?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/339

Category: Shipping information

If you opt for expedited delivery, you will incur additional shipping costs per design. Although the designs may be produced at the same location, they still need to be processed individually.
