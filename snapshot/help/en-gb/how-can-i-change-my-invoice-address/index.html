How can I change my invoice address?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/183

Category: Invoice

If you would like to change the invoice address for an order which does not have an invoice yet, you can change the invoice address on the order detail page in My Account.

If the invoice has already been created for your order or you do not see an option to change your invoice address on the order detail page, fill out our contact form with your invoice number and your new address, and we will gladly make the necessary changes for you!
