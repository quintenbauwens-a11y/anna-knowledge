Does my order go directly in production when I select the pay by invoice?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/346

Category: Invoice

Once you've chosen the "pay on invoice" option while placing your order, your order will swiftly move into production once your artwork is approved. Following the successful delivery of your order, we will promptly send you an invoice. Upon receiving the invoice, your payment term will commence.

This is different than when you would choose 'pay by bank transfer' where your order will go into production once we've received your payment.
