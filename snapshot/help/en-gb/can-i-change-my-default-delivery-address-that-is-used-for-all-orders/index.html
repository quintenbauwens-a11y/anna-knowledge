Can I change my default delivery address that is used for all orders?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/378

Category: Account

Manage and update your delivery addresses for future orders through your account's 'My Addresses' page. Access this page by hovering over your name in the top right corner of your screen and selecting 'My Addresses.'

Edit the default billing and delivery address:

  • Click 'Edit Address' in the box labeled 'Standard Billing Address & Default Delivery Address.'
  • Make any necessary changes.
  • Click 'Edit Address' to save your updates.
