Which payment options do I have?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/182

Category: Payment

At HelloPrint , you have the following payment options:

  • Debit or Credit card: Pay with your Debit or Credit card first, and upload and proof your artwork later.
  • Bank transfer: Pay with Bank transfer first, upload and proof your artwork later. Please note that this method takes more time and delays may arise from this.
  • Pay by invoice: Pay later by invoice, upload and proof your files first. Want to know more about this payment method? Read more about it here.
  • P****aypal: Pay first with Paypal, and upload and proof your artwork later.
  • Billie: Buy now and pay later with Billie. Only for businesses.
  • Upload now & pay later: Begin by uploading your files, and you can settle the payment at a later time. Please note, that payment must be made before your artwork goes into production. Please keep in mind that prepayment is always required for the first order.

Payment can be made via your shopping cart after confirming your products and delivery address.
