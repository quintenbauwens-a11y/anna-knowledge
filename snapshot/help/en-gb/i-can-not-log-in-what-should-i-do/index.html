I can not log in, what should I do?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/389

Category: Account

If you're having trouble logging in, we’re here to help! Please follow these steps:

Make sure you are on the Helloprint website for your region. If your account is registered with a different Helloprint site, you won’t be able to log in from this one.

Try resetting your password by clicking the “Forgot my password” button on the login page.
