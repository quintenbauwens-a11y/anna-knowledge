What is the difference between a digital or print-ready design file?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/258

Category: Design definitions

It's essential to understand the difference between digital and print-ready design files. A digital file’s purpose is to be viewed on a screen. These files are typically saved in a lower resolution and use RGB colours. On the other hand, a print-ready file is optimised for printing. They are typically saved in a higher resolution and usually contain CMYK colours.
