What colour profile do I need to use for my design?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/380

Category: Creating your design

We use FOGRA39 (ISO Coated V2) for coated products and FOGRA47 (ISO Uncoated V2) for uncoated products. If you are unsure of the colour mode of your file or how to prepare this in your file, don't worry, we will always convert this for you in our art work check process.
