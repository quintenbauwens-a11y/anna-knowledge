Can I use borders or frames in my design?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/261

Category: Design dos and don'ts

We advise against this, as there’s a chance that the frame will not be in proportion on all sides after the trimming process, and your design may look off-centre. The borders in your artwork will make even just a few mm’s difference in the cut very noticeable. This is not something we can avoid.
