What is the deadline for submitting my design?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/160

Category: After ordering

The deadline for submitting your design depends on the desired delivery time and the products selected. You can find the deadline in the product selection summary below the delivery date options and on the "orders" tab of Your Account.
