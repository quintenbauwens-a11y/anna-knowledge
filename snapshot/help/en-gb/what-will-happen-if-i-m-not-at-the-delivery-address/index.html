What will happen if I'm not at the delivery address?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/212

Category: After delivery

If you’re not at home when your order is delivered, carriers will either:

  1. Leave your order in a safe place (on your property or with a neighbour)
  2. Take it back to the depot
  3. Try to deliver it on the next business day
  4. Leave it at a local drop-off point

You can use the track and trace information to check this.
