Can I return my order?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/204

Category: After ordering

At HelloPrint, most of our products are made especially for you. Because of this, whether you can return your order depends on the type of product you purchased.

Personalised products

First things first, what is a personalised product? A personalised product is any item that is printed, engraved or otherwise customised with your design or specifications. Because these products are made to order:

  • They cannot be returned
  • There is no cooling-off period

This is because customised products are created specifically for you and cannot be resold. We’ll always review the situation and help you find a solution.

Non-personalised products

Some products in our assortment are not personalised (for example, blank or unprinted items). For these products, a standard 14-day cooling-off period applies. This means:

  • You have 14 calendar days from the day you receive your order to request a return
  • The product must be unused
  • The product must be returned in its original, undamaged packaging
  • Return shipping costs are borne by the customer

To request a return, please contact our customer service team within 14 days of receiving your order. We’ll provide you with the return instructions and address.
