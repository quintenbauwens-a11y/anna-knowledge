Is it important to outline my text?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/234

Category: Design dos and don'ts

Yes it is! Failure to embed fonts correctly can result in several issues, including missing text or incorrect font substitution. This is why we strongly recommend outlining your text once you have finished designing your artwork.

Embedding includes all necessary information about a font or image within the artwork file to be adequately reproduced during printing.
