I have overpaid my order, how can this be resolved? 

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/385

Category: Payment

When you pay twice for an order, our systems will detect this excess amount and automatically refund the overpaid funds.

This process can take up to 5 working days.
