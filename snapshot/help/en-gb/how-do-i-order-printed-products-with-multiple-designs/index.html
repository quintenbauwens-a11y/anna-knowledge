How do I order printed products with multiple designs?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/306

Category: Before ordering

If you would like to print multiple designs of the same product, we offer this option for multiple products on our website, like banners, panels and custom-size flags.

To do this, select the total quantity you want to order, then select the total number of unique designs and specify the quantity per design. Keep in mind, that it is only possible to order up to 30 different designs online. If you would like to order more than 30 designs, you can contact our Bespoke team.

Once the order is placed, you can upload your designs separately for each variant. We aim to produce and ship all your designs at the same time, therefore, once you upload some of your designs, we will wait until all your designs are uploaded and approved before producing your order.

If the product you want to order does not have an option to select a number of designs online, you can follow these steps:

  1. Add each product separately to your shopping cart.
  2. Upload the corresponding design file for each product.

For example, if you want to purchase four A3 posters, each with a distinct design, add four individual posters to your cart and upload a unique design file for each.
