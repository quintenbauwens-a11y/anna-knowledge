How do I unsubscribe from the newsletter?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/361

Category: Account

It's completely understandable if you wish to unsubscribe from our newsletter. This article is designed to guide you through the process. Here's a simple step-by-step guide to help you unsubscribe:

  1. Visit 'My Account' by hovering your cursor over your name in the top right corner of your screen.
  2. Click on 'Email preferences' located in the My Account options on the left side of the screen.
  3. Select or deselect the types of emails you wish to receive from us.
  4. Click the blue 'update email preferences' button to confirm your selections.

We'll investigate the issue and ensure it's resolve.
