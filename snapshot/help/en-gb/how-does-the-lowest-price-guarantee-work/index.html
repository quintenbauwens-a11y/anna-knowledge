How does the lowest price guarantee work?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/362

Category: Account

We can offer you the lowest price guarantee if you compare the exact same product. That means we look at the print run, size, exact same product specifications and delivery time. Send us a message here with a link to the product you're comparing. We'll let you know if we can match the lowest price.

This does not apply to orders that you already paid and placed in production with us.
