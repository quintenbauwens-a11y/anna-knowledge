Do I need to embed the images in my design?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/235

Category: Design dos and don'ts

Yes! Not properly embedding images can lead to various issues, including images disappearing.

Embedding includes all necessary information about an image within the artwork file to be properly reproduced during printing.
