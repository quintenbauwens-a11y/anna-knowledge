What is Secured Delivery?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/174

Category: Before ordering

If you have selected the secured delivery option and your package is lost or delivered damaged, we've got you! This means:

  • Any issues that might come up are given urgent priority.
  • Receive the fastest reprint if your order is considered lost by our customer experience team.
  • Receive a 20% refund on order items delayed after the latest communicated delivery date.

Fill out our complaint form here, and we will start an investigation for you.
