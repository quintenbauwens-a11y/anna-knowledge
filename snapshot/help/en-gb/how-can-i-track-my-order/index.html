How can I track my order?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/168

Category: Tracking delivery

Once your order has been shipped, you will receive a confirmation email with tracking information.

You can also view the status of your shipment by logging into Your Account, selecting the 'orders' tab on the left side of the page, and select the order of which you want to view the status. This way, you can easily stay informed about the progress of your order.
