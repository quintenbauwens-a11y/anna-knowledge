Why have I moved down a level in the VIP loyalty program?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/355

If you bought something 13 months ago, it will be removed from your annual spending calculation for VIP. If this drops below the value of the tier you were in, you will automatically be moved down at the beginning of the month.
