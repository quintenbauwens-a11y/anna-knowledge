Can I request a sample?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/167

Category: Before ordering

Unfortunately, we do not offer samples. We work with a number of different print facilities that have an ever-growing and changing product range with over 1,000 product types. We do not hold onto any unprinted products on site. For the majority of our products, the production process does not allow us to produce just one unit.

If you would like to receive a sample, it is best to order the minimum quantity of the product you require - you can find this information on the individual product pages after filling in your desired specifications.

Please note: A small print run may be produced differently to a larger print run due to the printing methods - the colours may differ, but the materials, however, are the same.
