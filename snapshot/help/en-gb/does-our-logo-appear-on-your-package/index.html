Does our logo appear on your package?

Help centre article on Helloprint UK: https://www.helloprint.com/en-gb/cs/articles/209

We know that a lot of our customers are designers or print resellers. That is why our HelloPrint packages will always be whitelabel. This means that they come in unbranded packages without any mention of HelloPrint so that you can send your printed items directly to your customer.
