Is it possible to choose for a pick-up delivery option?

Help centre article on Helloprint US: https://www.helloprint.com/en-us/cs/articles/330

Category: Delivery address

No, this is not possible. In order to provide the best quality products at affordable prices in a timely manner, we need flexibility in our production and shipping processes. The reason for this is because we work with many production locations across the US.

When your order is placed, we work to determine the optimal production location and shipping carrier to get your order to you as efficiently as possible. Locking into set pick-up points would limit our ability to adapt to changing circumstances, suppliers and carriers, to provide you the best value.
