Why is my discount voucher not working?

Help centre article on Helloprint US: https://www.helloprint.com/en-us/cs/articles/337

Category: Before ordering

If you received the discount voucher via a newsletter or promotional e-mail, please double-check the terms and conditions listed in the email. It may have expired, exceeded the discount limit, or not be valid on certain products.

Note that you can use only one discount code per purchase.
