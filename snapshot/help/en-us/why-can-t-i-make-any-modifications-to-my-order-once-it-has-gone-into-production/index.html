Why can't I make any modifications to my order once it has gone into production?

Help centre article on Helloprint US: https://www.helloprint.com/en-us/cs/articles/277

Category: After ordering

To ensure efficient processing of all orders, our production sites use a queuing system. This means that any changes made to an order already in the queue can potentially cause delays for other orders waiting in the queue. As a result, we always send an alert before finalising your artwork proof, giving you the opportunity to make any necessary changes before production begins.

It is very important to take extra care before you approve the proof. If you have chosen to send your files directly to our production site, you should also have received a warning message.
