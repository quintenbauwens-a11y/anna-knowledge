When do I get free shipping?

Help centre article on Helloprint US: https://www.helloprint.com/en-us/cs/articles/309

Category: Shipping information

Want to save on shipping costs? At HelloPrint we make that easy for you. Spend over $60 excluding taxes and the shipping is on the house. Simply add your favourite business cards, flyers or stationery to your cart and once the total amount reaches $60 excluding taxes, shipping becomes free.

The shipping fees might differ, depending on which product and amounts you are ordering.

Please note: this offer does not apply to products on custom size, such as banners and custom posters, custom stickers etc.
