Do I need to add trim marks to my design?

Help centre article on Helloprint US: https://www.helloprint.com/en-us/cs/articles/232

Category: Design definitions

Registration marks ensure that all the colours in the printed artwork are correctly aligned, which is why these marks are printed using 100% of each ink in the CMYK mix. It's important to avoid using this colour as a design element since it could result in text blurriness or smudges on the final product.

It is not necessary for you to add trim marks, we will always add them during our check.
