What is resolution?

Help centre article on Helloprint US: https://www.helloprint.com/en-us/cs/articles/255

Category: Design definitions

Resolution is the number of pixels, or picture elements, that make up an image. It determines the amount of detail that can be seen in an image and is measured in terms of the total number of pixels, both horizontally and vertically. A higher resolution means more pixels and finer detail, while a lower resolution means fewer pixels and less detail.
