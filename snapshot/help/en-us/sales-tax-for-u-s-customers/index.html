Sales Tax for U.S. Customers

Help centre article on Helloprint US: https://www.helloprint.com/en-us/cs/articles/398

Category: Sales Tax

Please note that we do not charge U.S. sales tax at the time of purchase. Under current U.S. tax law, we are only required to collect sales tax in states where we meet specific economic nexus thresholds. As we have not yet reached these thresholds, no sales tax is applied to this order.However, it’s important to understand that in some cases, you as the customer may still be responsible for self-assessing and remitting Use Tax directly to your state. Use Tax generally applies when sales tax is not collected at the time of purchase, and the goods are used, stored, or consumed in a state that imposes such tax.We recommend consulting with your internal finance or tax team to determine any potential obligations related to Use Tax and to confirm that you are able to accept this quote as provided by HelloPrint in relation to Sales and Use Tax. Please note that HelloPrint does not accept any responsibility or liability for such obligations or charges related to this order.
