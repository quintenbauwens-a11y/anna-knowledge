Can I have my order delivered to multiple addresses?

Help centre article on Helloprint US: https://www.helloprint.com/en-us/cs/articles/172

Category: Delivery address

Did you know you can place a single order on {shop_name} and have it shipped to multiple locations? When checking out, simply add the destination addresses for each item in your cart. For instance, send a custom mug to your home office and a branded t-shirt to your brick-and-mortar store. Keep your order together but have it delivered wherever needed. No need to place separate orders for different locations.

You can do this by following these steps during checkout:

  1. While in your cart, go to 'Pay & Upload Files.' This will first take you to the delivery options.
  2. Choose the preferred address for each product. If your address isn't in your address book yet, you can add it by clicking 'Change Address' next to the house icon on the left side of your product details.
  3. Once you have everything in place, proceed with the remainder of the checkout process.

Please note: It's not possible to split a single product across two addresses. For instance, if you want to send 1000 flyers to your home office and another 1000 to your store, please add them to your order as two separate products. This way, they will be packaged separately for delivery.
