My order has been delivered damaged. What should I do?

Firstly, we're very sorry to hear that your order has arrived damaged.

The best thing to do is to fill out our complaint form here. Make sure to add clear pictures or a video of the damage, the box/packaging and the shipping label. This way, we can solve it for you right away.
