How do I upload my multi-page or double-sided artwork?

Help centre article on Helloprint US: https://www.helloprint.com/en-us/cs/articles/329

Category: Before ordering

When ordering double-sided or multi-page prints, your file must be submitted as a single PDF. There’s no need to provide separate files for each page or side. Simply design your full artwork in one file, with pages laid out in the proper sequence for your final product.

For example, when designing a 6-page stapled booklet, arrange pages front to back, 1 to 6. For a double-sided product like a business card, just submit one artwork with two pages - one for the front and one for the back. Our printing process will automatically detect page/side placements to achieve your intended final layout.

Keeping your entire design in one file streamlines the uploading and ordering process, and ensures cohesion across all pages. Your one print-ready file is all we need to bring your creative vision to life.
