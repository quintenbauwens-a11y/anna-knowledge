How do I pay on invoice?

Help centre article on Helloprint US: https://www.helloprint.com/en-us/cs/articles/327

Category: Invoice

We offer all customers an option to pay on invoice. To be eligible, you must have placed and completed at least three orders with us.

Payment terms

Once this option is available, you can place an order and pay for it within 14 days of receiving the invoice. This is our standard payment term.

Credit limit

Our standard credit limit is 250 dollars. If you reach this limit, the option to pay by invoice will be temporarily disabled until outstanding invoices are paid.

Requesting different payment terms or credit limit

We review requests on a case-by-case basis and will inform you if we can accommodate your needs.
