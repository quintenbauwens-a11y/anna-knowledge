How can I download designs from my previous orders?

Help centre article on Helloprint US: https://www.helloprint.com/en-us/cs/articles/388

Category: After ordering

You can download your previous designs in "My account".

  1. Go to "Orders" in "My account"
  2. Select the order from which you want to download the designs
  3. On the order page, you can download the original artwork you uploaded and a print-ready PDF we use for the print.
