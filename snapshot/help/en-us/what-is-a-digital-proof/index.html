First things first - what is a digital proof? 

Well, the digital proof is a preview of your print ready design that will be sent to production.

After uploading your file to the order, you may receive a digital proof right away, automatically created by our system. If this is the case, you will be given the choice to approve it directly and send it to production, or have a digital proof created by our artwork specialists. Either way, remember to only approve your digital proof once you’re 100% happy with the design!

TIP: Take an extra look at the proof if you have received warnings from our artwork team. Warnings mean that something has been changed in your file or that the file is not 100% suitable.

We show proofs sometimes without bleed and sometimes with bleed.

  • Do you see your proof WITH cut marks? Then the proof is shown WITH bleed.
  • Do you see your proof WITHOUT cutting marks? Then the proof is shown WITHOUT bleed.
