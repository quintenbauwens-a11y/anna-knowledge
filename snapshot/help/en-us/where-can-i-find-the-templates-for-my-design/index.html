Where can I find the templates for my design?

Help centre article on Helloprint US: https://www.helloprint.com/en-us/cs/articles/364

Category: Creating your design

To make your design process a breeze, we've crafted user-friendly templates available in both PDF and InDesign formats. These templates come complete with precisely calibrated trim, bleed, and safety margins, ensuring your design is print-ready. You can easily access these templates on every product page under 'Design Guidelines'

Please note:

  • For optimal results, we recommend using the template specific to the product you're designing. Despite similar dimensions across products, nuances in trim, bleed, and safety margins may exist.
