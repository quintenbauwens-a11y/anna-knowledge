I'm missing the accessory I ordered with my purchase. What now?

Help centre article on Helloprint US: https://www.helloprint.com/en-us/cs/articles/343

Category: After ordering

Oh no! Your order has arrived, but you can't locate the accessory that was supposed to come with it? Don't worry, we're here to help. Follow these steps if you find yourself in this situation:

  1. Sometimes, accessories are shipped separately. Have you received multiple tracking codes?
  2. Check with your neighbors and search carefully for any secure hiding spots where the delivery carrier might have placed your order.
  3. We are committed to resolving this for you. Also attach photos of the products you did receive, the box, and the label. These images will greatly assist us in expediting the resolution process.
