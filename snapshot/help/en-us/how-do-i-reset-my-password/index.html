How do I reset my password?

Help centre article on Helloprint US: https://www.helloprint.com/en-us/cs/articles/265

Category: Account

If you've forgotten your password and need to reset it, don't worry! It happens to the best of us, and we've got you covered. You can easily reset your password by following these steps:

  1. Click here and enter the email address associated with your account.
  2. Shortly after, you'll receive an email requesting you to create a new password.
  3. Click on the button in the email, which will redirect you to a new web page.
  4. On the web page, you'll find a new password that we've generated for you. Feel free to use it! Alternatively, you can create a password of your choice on the same web page.
  5. Once you've reset your password, you'll be logged in again and ready to order all your favourite prints!
