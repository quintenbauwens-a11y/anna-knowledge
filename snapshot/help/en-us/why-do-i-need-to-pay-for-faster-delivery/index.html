Why do I need to pay for faster delivery?

Help centre article on Helloprint US: https://www.helloprint.com/en-us/cs/articles/210

Category: Delivery time

To ensure your order is delivered faster, your order needs to be printed / produced earlier. Therefore you pay extra for priority on the production line.

Note that delivery on the chosen date cannot always be guaranteed due to unexpected circumstances at the carrier, such as busy delivery schedules. We are dependent on our carriers and the reliability of delivery services can differ.
