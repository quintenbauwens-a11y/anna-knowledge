Is my order guaranteed to be delivered on the chosen date?

Help centre article on Helloprint US: https://www.helloprint.com/en-us/cs/articles/272

Category: Before ordering

Besides unforeseen circumstances on the carrier's side, 98% of our orders are delivered on time.

Please note:  Any delays in the production process are not included in this. For orders to go into production, the design has to be uploaded and approved before the deadline shown at the bottom of the page when ordering.
