What are Pantone colours?

Help centre article on Helloprint US: https://www.helloprint.com/en-us/cs/articles/245

Category: Design definitions

The Pantone company has defined more than 2,100 colours that each have their own unique number. These numbers guarantee that all colours anywhere in the world are exactly the same, making Pantone colours particularly well-suited for corporate logos and house styles.

Note: Pantone colours are also called PMS colours.
