How do I add a discount voucher to my order?

Help centre article on Helloprint US: https://www.helloprint.com/en-us/cs/articles/334

Category: Before ordering

You can add a discount voucher to your order in the cart overview just before paying and confirming your order. Make sure all the desired products are in your cart before adding the discount code.

Add your discount voucher by following the steps below:

  1. Click the tab 'Do you have a discount code?' beneath the 'Continue'-button.
  2. A box will appear where can enter your voucher code
  3. Click 'Save' to add the voucher to your order
  4. The website will recalculate the total amount and show the discounted amount
  5. Click 'Continue' to proceed with the order at the discounted price

Please note: after payment and completing an order, you can no longer add a discount code.
