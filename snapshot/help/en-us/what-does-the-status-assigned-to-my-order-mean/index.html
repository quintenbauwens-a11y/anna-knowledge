What does the status assigned to my order mean?

Help centre article on Helloprint US: https://www.helloprint.com/en-us/cs/articles/386

Category: Before ordering

Understanding the various stages of your order's production process is crucial. Below are the different statuses your order may go through and what each one means:

  1. Waiting for filesWe haven’t received the necessary files for your order yet. Please upload them as soon as possible so we can review them.
  2. Waiting for new filesThe files you previously provided were not print-ready and were rejected. We are now waiting for new print-ready files for review.
  3. Service completedYour design service (either premium or deluxe artwork check) has been completed.
  4. Your approval is neededOur AutoCheck or artwork team has prepared your proof. Please review it carefully, as production will only start once you give your approval.
  5. Ready for productionYour print-ready files have been approved and sent to the printing facility. The order is now in the queue, waiting to enter production.
  6. In productionYour order is currently being printed, finished, or packaged.
  7. Ready for shipmentThe package has been packed and labeled, awaiting pickup by the courier.
  8. ShippedYour package is on its way! You can track the delivery through the tracking link provided in your order.
  9. DeliveredThe order has been successfully delivered to its destination.
  10. We are processing your orderYour order is on backorder due to a logistical issue. We are working on a solution and will contact you shortly.
