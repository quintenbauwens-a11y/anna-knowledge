Why have I not received my order confirmation?

Help centre article on Helloprint US: https://www.helloprint.com/en-us/cs/articles/163

Category: After ordering

It is possible that your order confirmation email ended up in your spam/junk folder, please check that folder first.

If you can't find an order confirmation there, please check if your order has been placed correctly by going to the "Orders" page in your account. If your order is listed there, there is no need to worry - we've received it. You can find the status of your order there.

If you don't see it listed after fifteen minutes, there may have been an error during the submission process.
