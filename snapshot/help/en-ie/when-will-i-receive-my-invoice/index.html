When will I receive my invoice?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/when-will-i-receive-my-invoice

Category: Invoice

We email your invoice as soon as your order status shows Your order has been shipped. You can also download your invoice at any time from Invoices in your account.

Where to find your invoice

Go to Invoices in your account to download every invoice and credit note as a PDF. See how to download invoices and credit notes.

If your invoice hasn't arrived

  1. Check your order status in Orders. If your order hasn't shipped yet, we haven't sent the invoice yet.
  2. Check your spam or junk folder, and check which email address receives your invoices.
  3. Download the invoice from Invoices in your account.

If you need your invoice before your order ships

Paper invoices

We don't send paper documents with your delivery. Your invoice is always sent by email and is always available in your account.
