When will I receive my invoice?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/when-will-i-receive-my-invoice

Category: Invoice

Once your order status shows "Your order has been shipped", we’ll send you the invoice by email. You can also access and download your invoice in My Account.

Just to remind you, we’ll never send any physical documents related to your order upon delivery.
