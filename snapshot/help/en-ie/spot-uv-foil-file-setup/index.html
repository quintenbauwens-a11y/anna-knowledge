How do I set up my file for spot UV or foil?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/spot-uv-foil-file-setup

Category: Creating your design

Upload two files: one with your complete design, and one that shows only the parts that get spot UV or foil, coloured 100% black. The finish can't run all the way to the edge of your product, so keep those parts away from the edge.

Prepare your two files

  1. Save your complete design as one file, with both sides if your product is double-sided. Follow the file requirements for paper products.
  2. Make a copy of that file and delete everything except the elements that get the finish.
  3. Colour those elements 100% black and leave the rest of the page empty.
  4. Upload both files to your order.

We then check both files and send you a digital proof to approve before your order goes into production.

Keep the finish away from the edge

Spot UV and foil can't be applied right up to the cut edge of your product. Keep every finish element inside the safety margin.

Use the guide on the product page

Each product with spot UV or foil has a guide and templates on its product page that show how to set up the finish file for that product.
