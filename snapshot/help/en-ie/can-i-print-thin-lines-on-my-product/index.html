Can I print thin lines on my product?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/can-i-print-thin-lines-on-my-product

Category: Design dos and don'ts

Yes, as long as every line in your design is at least the minimum line thickness for your product. You find that minimum on the product page under Design Guidelines, because it depends on the print technique and the material.

Find the minimum line thickness

  1. Open the product page of the product you want to order.
  2. Scroll to Design Guidelines.
  3. Check the minimum line thickness for your product.
  4. Make every line in your design at least that thick.

Check your lines before you upload

  • Lines thinner than the minimum may not print clearly.
  • Lines placed very close together need enough space between them to stay clearly separate in print.
  • Small text has its own minimum size: see printing small text.

If a line is thinner than the minimum

Make the line thicker or remove it, then upload your file. We check your file and send you a digital proof to approve. If you have already uploaded your file, you can replace your file until your order goes into production; a new file means a new digital proof.
