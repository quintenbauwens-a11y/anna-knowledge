Can I merge orders or move an order to another account?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/can-i-merge-orders-or-move-an-order-to-another-account

Category: Account

No, orders can't be merged once they are placed, and an order can't be moved to another account: each order stays with the account it was placed with. If your orders haven't gone into production yet, you may be able to cancel them and place one new order with everything in it.

If you want to combine orders

  1. Check whether each order can still be cancelled: see cancelling your order.
  2. Cancel the orders you want to combine.
  3. Place one new order with all your products in the same basket. For different designs, see ordering different designs in one order.
  4. Upload your files and approve your digital proofs before the deadline on your order page.

What happens next: for the cancelled orders, see how refunds work. Your new order gets its own delivery date. Different products can still ship separately: see why you may pay shipping more than once.

If an order is in the wrong account

Log in with the account you used to place the order to see it and download its invoice. You have one account for every Helloprint country site, so you can log in with that account on any of them.

If you want one invoice for several orders

Invoices can't be merged either. Download the invoice for each order from Invoices in My Account.

Once an order has gone into production, it can no longer be cancelled, so it can no longer be combined with another order.
