Can I order without VAT?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/can-i-order-without-vat

Category: Payment

We add VAT to your order wherever it applies, and you see the exact amount in your cart before you pay. Adding a VAT number to your account puts it on your invoice; whether you can reclaim the VAT depends on your own VAT registration.

How we work out the VAT on your order

Whether VAT applies, and how much, can depend on the product and how it's used. That's why we calculate the VAT for your specific order at checkout. You see it itemised in your cart and on your invoice. Read more in do I need to pay VAT?

Add your company and VAT details to your invoice

Add your company name and VAT number in My Account under Personal details, in the Company info card. Your VAT number then appears on your invoices. See updating your company and VAT details.

Reclaim VAT as a business

If your business is registered for VAT, you may be able to reclaim the VAT on your order through your own VAT return, using our invoice. Check this with your accountant. You can download your invoices at any time from Invoices in My Account.

Our VAT number

Our Irish VAT number is IE3469492VH. You find it with our other details in our bank details.
