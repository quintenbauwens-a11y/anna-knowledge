Which payment methods can I use?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/which-payment-options-do-i-have

Category: Payment

In Ireland you can pay by credit or debit card, PayPal or bank transfer (SEPA). Approved business customers can also pay on invoice.

Payment methods

  • Credit or debit card: an instant payment.
  • PayPal: you pay in your PayPal account; an instant payment.
  • Bank transfer (SEPA): you transfer the amount yourself. We start production planning once your payment arrives in our account.
  • Pay on invoice: for approved business customers. Paying on invoice explains who can use it and how.

When you pay

You pay in your cart when you place your order. You can upload your file on the product page, on the order confirmation page after checkout, or later from your order page; upload and approve before the deadline to keep your delivery date.

If your payment fails

What to do when a payment fails explains how to check your payment and try again.

If you pay by bank transfer, we start production planning only once your payment arrives, so on a tight deadline choose card or PayPal.
