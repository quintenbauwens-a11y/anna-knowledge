Which payment methods can I use?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/which-payment-options-do-i-have

Category: Payment

You can pay securely with:

  • Credit or debit card
  • PayPal
  • Bank transfer (SEPA)
  • Pay on Invoice - for approved business customers (Check if you're eligible)

The exact options you see at checkout depend on your country — local methods like iDEAL or Bancontact appear automatically wherever they're available.

One thing to know about bank transfer: we start production planning once your payment arrives, so on a tight deadline a card or another instant method is the faster choice. Every payment is processed securely.
