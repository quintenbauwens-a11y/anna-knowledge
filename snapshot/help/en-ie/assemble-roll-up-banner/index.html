How do I assemble my roll-up banner?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/assemble-roll-up-banner

Category: After delivery

You assemble your roll-up banner by putting the 3-piece pole into the base and pulling the banner up onto the pole. Everything is in the carrying bag: the base with the banner rolled inside it, and the pole, which is stored inside the base.

Prepare the base and pole

  1. Take the roll-up banner out of the carrying bag and lay the bag, base and pole next to each other.
  2. Turn the support feet of the base 90 degrees and put the base back on the ground.
  3. Join the 3 parts of the pole and put the pole into the hole in the base, all the way to the bottom.

Pull up the banner

  1. Stand with both feet on the support feet of the base to keep it steady.
  2. Pull the banner straight up slowly with both hands, without twisting it.
  3. Hook the clamping rail (the metal rail at the top of the back of the banner) onto the pole.

Your banner now stands on its own. Place it where your audience can see it clearly.

Take down your banner

  1. Stand with both feet on the support feet and unhook the clamping rail from the pole.
  2. Hold the banner and let it roll back into the base slowly and straight.
  3. Pull the pole out of the base, separate the 3 parts and store them in the back of the base.
  4. Turn the support feet 90 degrees so they line up with the base.
  5. Put the roll-up banner in the carrying bag and store it in a dry place.

If you need a new print for your existing frame

We advise against ordering a replacement banner without the frame: small differences between mechanisms can stop the banner from rolling or fitting properly. Order the complete roll-up banner with frame for the most reliable result. If you'd still like a banner-only print and accept that it may not fit perfectly, request a quote for the banner only, and our team looks at the possibilities with you.

If you let go of the banner while it rolls back, it shoots into the base and can be damaged.
