Can I add two different flyers to the same order?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/can-i-add-two-different-flyers-to-the-same-order

Category: Before ordering

Yes. Add each design to your basket as its own product, check out once and upload one file for each design.

Order different designs

  1. Choose your product and options, and add it to your basket.
  2. Add the same product again for your second design. Repeat this for every design.
  3. Check out once for everything.
  4. Upload the matching file for each product: on the product page, on the order confirmation page after checkout, or later from your order page.

We then check each file and send you a digital proof for each design to approve. Upload your files and approve your digital proofs before the deadline to keep your chosen delivery date.

Example: two different flyers

Two A5 flyers, each with a different design: add two separate A5 flyers to your basket and upload a different file for each.

This works the same way for other products, such as posters or business cards.

Faster delivery for several designs

Each design is produced as its own product. If you choose a faster delivery date, you pay for the faster turnaround separately for each design. See why you can pay more than once for faster delivery.
