Can I use borders or frames in my design?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/can-i-use-borders-or-frames-in-my-design

Category: Design dos and don'ts

You can, but printing a border or frame is at your own risk. Trimming can vary slightly, so a border can end up thicker on one side than on the other, and we advise against it.

Why borders show small cutting differences

We cut every product to its final size after printing, and the cut can vary by a few millimetres. On a design that runs to the edge you won't notice this, but next to a straight border even a small difference stands out. We can't avoid this, so we can't guarantee an even border.

If you still want a border

  • Keep the inside edge of the border at least 5 mm inside the final size, in the safety margin.
  • Extend the background colour into the 3 mm bleed on every side.
  • Check your digital proof before you approve it.

The full specs are in the file requirements for paper products. Where we cut your product explains the trim line and crop marks.

If your design is a bookmark or another narrow product

On narrow products a difference in cutting is easier to see, because the border runs close to the edge on both long sides. For an even result, leave the border out or let the background colour run to the edge.

What happens next

Check your digital proof before you approve it. If you want to remove or move the border, replace your file before your order goes into production. You then get a new digital proof to approve.
