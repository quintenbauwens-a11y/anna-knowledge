Can I delete my account?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/can-i-delete-my-account

Category: Account

Yes — but please note that deleting your account is permanent and can't be undone. Once your account is deleted you lose access to it for good: you won't be able to log back in, and your order history and saved details can't be recovered. If you'd like to use Helloprint again in future, you'll need to create a new account.
