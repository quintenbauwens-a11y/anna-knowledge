How does ordering at Helloprint work?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-does-ordering-at-helloprint-work

Category: Before ordering

Ordering takes four steps: choose a product, set your options, choose your delivery date and add your design. You can upload your own file, design in Canva or upload your file later.

Order in 4 steps

  1. Pick a product. Use the menu or the search bar.
  2. Set your options on the product page: size, quantity, print method, finishing and binding.
  3. Choose your delivery date. Shipping is free above a minimum order amount: see when you get free shipping. A faster delivery option costs extra.
  4. Add your design and go to your cart.

Three ways to add your design

If you have a print-ready file

Upload your own file. Templates and file requirements are on the product page.

If you don't have a design yet

Design in Canva. You can't change the quantity or the template after you've designed in Canva, so choose them carefully first.

If your design isn't ready yet

Place the order now and upload your file later, on the order confirmation page or from your order page. Upload and approve before your deadline to make your chosen delivery date.

In your cart

Check your order, add a discount code or change your options. You can also choose a paid Premium or Deluxe design check; every order includes the free Basic design check.

What happens next

  1. We check your file. If it passes the Basic design check, you see your digital proof before you pay: see seeing a proof before paying and how long a design check takes.
  2. You pay and approve your digital proof.
  3. Production starts and your file can no longer change.
  4. We ship your order and send you a track and trace link.

Once your order is in production, you can't change your file.
