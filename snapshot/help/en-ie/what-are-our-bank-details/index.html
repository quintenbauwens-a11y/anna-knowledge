Pay by bank transfer

  1. Find your invoice number in Invoices or your order number in Orders in My Account.
  2. In your online banking, make a transfer to the account above for the amount on your invoice or order.
  3. Put your invoice number or order number in the payment reference. If you pay several invoices or orders at once, put all the numbers in the reference.

What happens next: the reference lets us match your payment to the right invoice or order. Your order and invoice are then shown as paid in My Account.
