How do I download my invoice or credit note?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-do-i-download-my-invoices-and-credit-notes

Category: Invoice

You can download every invoice and credit note as a PDF from Invoices in My Account. A credit note is listed with the invoice of the same order.

Download an invoice or credit note

  1. Open My Account → Invoices.
  2. Find the order by its order number or date.
  3. Download the invoice or credit note PDF.

The PDF saves to your device, ready to print or forward. We also email you your invoice: when you receive your invoice explains when.

If you're looking for a credit note

A credit note is the document that shows an amount we have credited to you, for example after a cancellation or refund. You find it in My Account → Invoices, together with the invoice of the same order. When you get your refund explains when the money reaches your account.

If your invoice isn't there yet

An invoice appears in My Account → Invoices once we have created it for your order. When you receive your invoice explains when that happens.

If the details on your invoice are wrong

Update the address for your next invoices with how to change your invoice address, and your company name or VAT number with updating your company and VAT details.
