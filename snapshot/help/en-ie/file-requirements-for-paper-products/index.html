What are the file requirements for paper products?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/file-requirements-for-paper-products

Category: Creating your design

A print-ready file for Helloprint paper products is one PDF at 300 dpi in CMYK with 3 mm bleed on every side. Keep text and logos at least 5 mm inside the final size.

Specs at a glance

  • File type: use a PDF. We also accept other file types, such as JPG and PNG, if the resolution is high enough. See which file types we accept.
  • Resolution: use 300 dpi.
  • Colour mode: use CMYK, not RGB. We print with the FOGRA39 profile (ISO Coated v2) on coated paper and FOGRA47 (ISO Uncoated v2) on uncoated paper.
  • Bleed: add 3 mm on every side.
  • Safe zone: keep text and logos at least 5 mm inside the final size.
  • Fonts: embed the fonts or convert them to outlines.
  • Crop marks: switch crop marks off. We add them during production.

If you design online

Start the online design editor from the product page. The right template is applied for you.

If you use your own software

Download the free template for your product (for example for flyers or business cards) and open it in Illustrator, InDesign or Photoshop. In InDesign or Illustrator, set the bleed to 3 mm under Document Setup and tick Use document bleed settings when you export the PDF.

Check before you export

  • Colours look different on different paper types. Keep your chosen paper in mind.
  • All images are embedded.
  • No unwanted overprint settings in your layers.

After you upload

We check your file and create your digital proof for you to approve.
