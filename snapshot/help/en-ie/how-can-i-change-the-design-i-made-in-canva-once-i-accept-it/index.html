How can I change my Canva design after I've accepted it?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-can-i-change-the-design-i-made-in-canva-once-i-accept-it

Category: Canva

You can change your Canva design until you pay: in your cart, click Edit your design to go back to the design editor.

Change your design before you pay

  1. Open your cart.
  2. Click Edit your design next to the product.
  3. Make your changes in the design editor and finish your design.

Your changed design replaces the old one for that product in your cart. After you pay, we check your design and send you a digital proof to approve.

If you've already paid

As long as your order isn't in production, we look at what we can do with you.

If your order is already in production

Once production has started, your design can't be changed. You can place a new order with a new Canva design from the product page. To see where your order is, check what your order status means.

Find a previous Canva design

Designs you save during your session are in the My Designs tab of the Design Online section. A design can disappear if you close the design editor before you order, or once it has been processed for production. You can't download a Canva file from Helloprint, but you can download designs from your previous orders.

You can edit your Canva design yourself only until you pay.
