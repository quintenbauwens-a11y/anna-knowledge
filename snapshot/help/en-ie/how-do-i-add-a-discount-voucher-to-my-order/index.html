How do I add a discount voucher to my order?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-do-i-add-a-discount-voucher-to-my-order

Category: Before ordering

Add your discount voucher by following the steps below:

  1. Go to the cart to finalise the purchase
  2. Click the tab 'Do you have a discount code?' beneath the 'Continue'-button.
  3. A box will appear where you can enter your voucher code
  4. Click 'Save' to add the voucher to your order
  5. The website will recalculate the total amount and show the discounted amount
  6. Click 'Continue' to proceed with the order at the discounted price

Please note: after payment and completing an order, you can no longer add a discount code.
