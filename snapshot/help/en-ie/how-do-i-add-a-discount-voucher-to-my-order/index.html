How do I add a discount voucher to my order?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-do-i-add-a-discount-voucher-to-my-order

Category: Before ordering

Add your discount code in your cart, before you pay: click Do you have a discount code?, enter your code and click Save. A discount code can only be added before you pay for your order.

Add your discount code

  1. Go to your cart.
  2. Click Do you have a discount code? below the Continue button.
  3. Enter your code in the box that appears and click Save.
  4. Check your new total and click Continue to complete your order at the discounted price.

What happens next

Your cart recalculates the total straight away and shows the discounted amount. You can use one discount code per order.

If your code isn't accepted

Why a discount code isn't working explains the most common reasons and what you can do about each one.

If you've already paid for your order

A discount code can no longer be added once you have paid.
