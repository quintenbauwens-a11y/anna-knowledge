My track and trace code is not working, what can I do?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/my-track-and-trace-code-is-not-working-what-can-i-do

Category: Tracking delivery

Once your order has been shipped, you will receive an email with the tracking link to follow your order. Did you open this link and can't find your package? Don't panic! This is probably due to the carrier not scanning your package yet. If that is the case, the tracking link will be active the next day
