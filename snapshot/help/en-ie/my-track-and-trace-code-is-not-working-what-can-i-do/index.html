My track and trace code is not working, what can I do?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/my-track-and-trace-code-is-not-working-what-can-i-do

Category: Tracking delivery

Your track and trace link usually only works once the carrier has scanned your parcel, which can take until the day after you receive our shipping email.

Why the link doesn't work yet

We send you an email with the tracking link as soon as your order has shipped. The carrier's tracking page only shows your parcel after the carrier has scanned it for the first time. Until then, the link can show an error or no information about your parcel.

Check your tracking link

  1. Go to Orders.
  2. Click Order details on your order.
  3. Click Track parcel for the parcel you want to follow.

What happens next: the carrier's tracking page opens with the latest information about your parcel. If your order was shipped in more than one parcel, each parcel has its own link: see tracking a multi-parcel order.

If the link still doesn't work the next day

We look into it with you and let you know what we find.
