Can I repeat a previous order?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/can-i-repeat-a-previous-order

Category: After ordering

Yes. Open the order in Orders, click Reorder under the product and choose whether to print it again with the same design.

Repeat your order

  1. Open Orders and click Order details on the order you want to repeat.
  2. Click Reorder under the product.
  3. Select the products and choose With this design to use the same file again.
  4. Click Add products to cart.
  5. Check your cart and click Pay & Upload.

Your new order gets its own order number and follows the normal steps from payment to delivery. We use the same delivery speed as your previous order.

Change the size, quantity or options

In your cart, click the pencil icon next to the product. You go to the product page, where you can change the size, quantity or other options before you pay. If you change the size, upload a file in the new size; file requirements for paper products list what the file needs.

If you want a new design

Don't choose With this design. You then upload your new file from your order page, before or after you pay.

If reordering gives an error

We have changed something on that product, so it can't be repeated automatically. Open the product page, choose the same options and quantity as before, and upload your file again. You can download the design from your previous order first.
