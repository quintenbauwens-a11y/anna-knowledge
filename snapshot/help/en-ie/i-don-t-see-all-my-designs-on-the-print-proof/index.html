Why don't I see all my designs on my digital proof?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/i-don-t-see-all-my-designs-on-the-print-proof

Category: Artwork check & digital proof

Almost always, more than one design was uploaded to a single product: each product in your order prints one design, so your digital proof shows only one. Upload each design to its own product, or order a product for every design.

If you uploaded several designs to one product

You'll see this if your file holds several different designs, for example four flyers in one PDF, and your digital proof shows only one of them, or your file was declined. If you upload several files to one product, we combine them into one document for the check, and that document can't have more pages than your product.

What you can do: if your order has a product for every design, open your orders, click Order details, then Change submitted files, and upload one design to each product. We send you a new digital proof for each product to approve.

If your order has fewer products than designs, the extra designs can't be printed on this order. Place a separate order for the remaining designs, adding each design as its own product (see ordering different designs). If your order hasn't gone into production yet, you can also cancel it and order again with a product for every design.

If parts of your design are missing at the edges

You'll see this if text, logos or images close to the edge are cut off or missing on your digital proof.

What you can do: keep important elements inside the safety margin and upload a corrected file with Change submitted files. We send you a new digital proof to approve. See The lines and markings on your digital proof to check what will be cut.

Once you approve your digital proof, we print only what it shows.
