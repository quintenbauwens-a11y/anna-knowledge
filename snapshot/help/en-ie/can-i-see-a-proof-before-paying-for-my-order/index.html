Can I see a proof before paying for my order?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/can-i-see-a-proof-before-paying-for-my-order

Category: Before ordering

  1. Add your product(s) to your cart.
  2. Once you have added all products to the cart, select "Continue".
  3. Choose your delivery options and continue to payment.
  4. Select "Pay later and upload my artwork first" on the payment page to upload your artwork and see a proof before paying for the order.
  5. Upload your artwork, and we will show you a proof to approve. If you are unhappy with the proof, you can opt to upload a new file.
  6. Don't forget to pay your order, otherwise your order will not go in production.
