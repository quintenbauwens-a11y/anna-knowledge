Can I see a proof before paying for my order?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/can-i-see-a-proof-before-paying-for-my-order

Category: Before ordering

Yes, if your file passes our Basic design check: you see your digital proof within about 5 minutes, before you pay. If your file needs a manual check by our artwork team, that check starts once we've received your payment.

If your file passes the Basic design check

Upload your file on the product page while you order. Within about 5 minutes the Basic design check shows your digital proof, so you can see exactly what we will print before you pay. Your order goes into production once you've paid and approved your digital proof.

If your file needs a manual check

Some files need a manual check by our artwork team. The manual check starts once we've received your payment and takes about 60 minutes. You then get your digital proof to approve. If you approve it after the deadline, your delivery date moves by one working day.

If you upload your file after checkout

You can also upload your file on the order confirmation page after checkout, or later from your order page. We then check your file and send you your digital proof to approve. Upload and approve before the deadline to keep your chosen delivery date.

If you want to change your file

You can replace your file until your order goes into production. We check the new file and create a new digital proof for you to approve.

A manual check only starts after we've received your payment, so pay in time to approve your digital proof before the deadline.
