What is Secured Delivery?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/what-is-secured-delivery

Category: Before ordering

Secured Delivery is an optional paid add-on you choose at checkout. It gives you priority support with delivery issues, an immediate reprint if your order is lost or damaged, and 20% of your order value back if it arrives late.

What Secured Delivery includes

  • Priority support: we handle delivery issues on your order first.
  • Immediate reprint: if your order is lost or arrives damaged, we reprint it straight away.
  • 20% back if your order is late: if your order arrives after the latest promised delivery date, you get 20% of the order value back.

Secured Delivery doesn't change how your parcel is packed, shipped or handled.

Add Secured Delivery to your order

Choose Secured Delivery at checkout, before you pay. To keep your delivery date, also upload your file and approve your digital proof before the deadline: whether your order is guaranteed to arrive on the date you chose explains how.

If your order with Secured Delivery is late, lost or damaged

We check your order and handle it with priority.

  • Late: we check the delivery date against the latest promised delivery date and refund 20% of the order value.
  • Damaged: see what to do when your order arrives damaged for what we need from you. Once we've checked it, we reprint your order straight away.
  • Lost: see what to do if your package hasn't been delivered. Once we've confirmed the parcel is lost, we reprint your order straight away.
