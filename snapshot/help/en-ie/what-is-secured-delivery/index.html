What is Secured Delivery?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/what-is-secured-delivery

Category: Before ordering

Secured Delivery is an optional paid add-on you choose at checkout: if your order arrives after the latest promised delivery date, you get 20% of your order value back.

What Secured Delivery covers

  • Secured Delivery covers late delivery: an order that arrives after the latest promised delivery date.
  • For a late order with Secured Delivery, you get 20% of the order value back.
  • Secured Delivery doesn't change how your parcel is packed, shipped or handled.

Add Secured Delivery to your order

Choose Secured Delivery at checkout, before you pay. To keep your delivery date, also upload your file and approve your digital proof before the deadline: whether your order is guaranteed to arrive on the date you chose explains how.

If your order with Secured Delivery arrives late

We check the delivery date of your order against the latest promised delivery date and look into your claim for you.

If your parcel is damaged or lost

Secured Delivery is about late delivery, so it doesn't cover a damaged or lost parcel. If your order arrived damaged, see what to do when your order arrives damaged. If your parcel hasn't arrived, see what to do if your package hasn't been delivered.
