Where do I start when preparing my design for printing?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/where-do-i-start-when-preparing-my-design-for-printing

Category: Creating your design

To prepare your design for printing, start on the product page: download the template for your product and follow its file requirements, because size, bleed and page count differ per product. For paper products, a print-ready file is one PDF in CMYK at 300 dpi with 3 mm bleed.

Prepare your design in 5 steps

  1. Download the template from the product page and design on it, with the same page count as you order.
  2. Set your colours to CMYK and use images with a resolution of 300 dpi.
  3. Add 3 mm bleed and keep text and logos inside the safety margin.
  4. Embed or outline your fonts and delete the template layers.
  5. Export your design as one PDF and upload the file. See which file type to use.

What happens next

Every order includes the free Basic design check: we check your file and send you a digital proof to approve. See how long a design check takes.

Details that can print badly

  • Small text and thin lines have a minimum size.
  • Small black text prints sharpest in 100% black: see rich black and 100% black.
  • Logos and icons stay sharp at any size as vector files.
  • Linked images must be embedded in your design.

If you design in Canva

Start your Canva design from the product page, so it opens at the right size. See how to find the right size in Canva.

If you don't have a design yet

We can't create designs from scratch, but we're happy to look at small changes to your file: see design help. On products that offer Canva, you can also design your product yourself in Canva from the product page.

We don't check spelling or typing errors, so check every word before you approve your digital proof.
