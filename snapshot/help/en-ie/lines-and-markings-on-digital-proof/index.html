What are the lines and markings on my digital proof?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/lines-and-markings-on-digital-proof

Category: Artwork check & digital proof

Lines and markings on your digital proof that you didn't put in your design are guides: they show where we cut, fold or perforate your product, and they are not printed. Check that nothing important in your design crosses them.

What each line shows

  • Cut marks in the corners: show where we trim your product to its final size. If your digital proof shows cut marks, your design includes bleed.
  • Magenta dotted lines on folded products: show where your product is folded, for example on a folded flyer.
  • Magenta lines on tickets: show where the perforation goes, for example on an admission ticket.
  • Magenta outlines on stickers: show the shape the machine cuts, for example around a round sticker.

What to check against the lines

  • Text and logos stay at least 5 mm inside the final size, so they stay clear of the cut line and inside the safety margin.
  • Backgrounds and images run past the cut line into the bleed, so no white edge appears after cutting.
  • Nothing important sits on a fold or perforation line.

If you see a grey line at the bottom of your digital proof

A grey line is not one of the guide lines above. Open your own file and check whether the line is part of your design. If it is in your file, remove it and replace your file. If it isn't in your file, don't approve your digital proof yet: click Chat about this artwork on your digital proof, and we look at it with you.

If something in your design crosses a line

Don't approve your digital proof yet. Move the element in your design, then replace your file. We create a new digital proof for you to check and approve.

Anything that crosses the cut line can be cut off, and once you approve your digital proof your file can't be changed.
