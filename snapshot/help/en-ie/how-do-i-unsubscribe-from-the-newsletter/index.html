How do I unsubscribe from the newsletter?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-do-i-unsubscribe-from-the-newsletter

Category: Account

Switch off marketing emails in My Account under Personal details → Communication preferences. You keep receiving emails about your orders, such as order confirmations.

Unsubscribe from the newsletter

  1. Log in and go to My Account.
  2. Open Personal details.
  3. In Communication preferences, switch off Marketing emails.

Your preference is saved for your account, and our newsletter and offers are switched off. Because you have one account for all Helloprint country websites, this applies to all of them.

If you still receive our newsletter

Check that you switched off marketing emails for the email address that receives the newsletter.

If you want to sign up again

Go to Personal details → Communication preferences in My Account and switch Marketing emails back on.
