What if I'm not happy with my order? (our satisfaction guarantee)

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/not-happy-with-my-order-satisfaction-guarantee

Category: After ordering

If something we're responsible for went wrong with your order, such as the print quality or the packaging, report it within 10 days of delivery with photos. Where the fault is ours, we'll put it right, usually with a reprint, refund or credit.

What the satisfaction guarantee covers

  • Design check: we check every file against the product's specifications with the free Basic design check. If something doesn't fit, we warn you on your digital proof.
  • Print quality: problems in how we printed or finished your order.
  • Packaging: damage caused by how we packed your order.
  • Dispatch: sending your order in time for the carrier to deliver it on your chosen date. See whether your order is guaranteed to arrive on the date you chose.

What the satisfaction guarantee doesn't cover

  • Errors in your design that were visible on the digital proof you approved, such as a spelling mistake or a layout problem. Check every page of your digital proof before you approve it. Until your order goes into production, you can still replace your file.
  • Carrier delays caused by extreme weather or the busy holiday period. If your order is delayed, chat with us so we can look together at what we can do.
  • Faster carrier transport: when you pay for faster delivery, you pay for a faster turnaround in production; the carrier's transport time stays the same.

Report a problem with your order

  1. Take clear photos of the problem, the packaging and the shipping label.
  2. Open your order in Orders and click Report a problem.
  3. Describe what went wrong, add your photos and send your message.

What happens next: our Customer Service team looks into your complaint and gets back to you. Where the fault is ours, we'll put it right, usually with a reprint, refund or credit. How to file a complaint lists what to include and which files you can attach.

If your order arrived damaged or incomplete

Report it the same way. See what to do with a damaged order or what to do if your order arrived incomplete.

If more than 10 days have passed since delivery

Report the problem with photos anyway. Our Customer Service team looks at what we can do, but we may no longer be able to check whether production or delivery caused it.

Once you approve your digital proof, errors in your design that were visible on it are no longer covered by the satisfaction guarantee.
