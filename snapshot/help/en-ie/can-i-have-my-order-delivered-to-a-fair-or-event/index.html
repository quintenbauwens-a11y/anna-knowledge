Can I have my order delivered to a fair or event?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/can-i-have-my-order-delivered-to-a-fair-or-event

Category: Delivery options

You can enter a fair or event as your delivery address, but we can't guarantee delivery there. We advise a residential or business address instead, so your order reaches you in time for your event.

Why we advise another address

Delivery to a fair or event can cause delays, especially if there is no named recipient to receive the parcel. A residential or business address where someone can receive your order is the safest choice.

Choose your delivery address and date

  1. Choose a delivery date on the product page that arrives well before your event.
  2. At checkout, enter a residential or business address where someone can receive the parcel.
  3. Upload and approve your file before the deadline shown next to your delivery option.

Once your order ships, you can track your order to see when it arrives.

If you still want delivery to the fair or event

Enter the full address of the venue and the name of a person who will be there to receive the parcel. Delivery there isn't guaranteed, so plan time for a possible delay.

If you choose Saturday delivery

Enter a consumer (home) address, not a business address or an event venue. We need a consumer address to deliver on Saturday.

If nobody can receive your parcel

See what happens after an unsuccessful delivery attempt.

If delivery to a fair or event is delayed, your products may arrive after your event has started.
