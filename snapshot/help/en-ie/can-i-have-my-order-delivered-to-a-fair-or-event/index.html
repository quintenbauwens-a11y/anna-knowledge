Can I have my order delivered to a fair or event?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/can-i-have-my-order-delivered-to-a-fair-or-event

Category: Delivery options

It is possible, but not recommended as it could cause delays, especially if there is no named recipient. We recommend that you provide a residential or business address to ensure prompt delivery.
