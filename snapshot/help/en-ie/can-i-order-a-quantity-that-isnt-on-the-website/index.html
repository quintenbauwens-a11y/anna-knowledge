Can I order a quantity that isn't on the website?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/can-i-order-a-quantity-that-isnt-on-the-website

Category: Before ordering

You can order every quantity shown on the product page, and you can combine two of them to reach a different total, for example 100 + 250 for 350. For any other quantity, email our bespoke team at bespoke@helloprint.com: they look at what we can do for you.

Combine quantities

  1. On the product page, choose the first quantity, add your design and add the product to your cart.
  2. Go back to the same product, choose the second quantity, add your design and add it to your cart.
  3. Check both items in your cart and pay for them in one order.

Each item gets its own digital proof to approve. Read how to add the same product twice to one order.

If you need fewer than the smallest quantity

The smallest quantity on the product page is the smallest print run you can order online. If you need fewer, email our bespoke team at bespoke@helloprint.com with the product and the quantity, and they look at what we can do. For a single test copy, see requesting a sample.

If you need another quantity or a special request

Email our bespoke team at bespoke@helloprint.com with the product, your options and the total quantity you need. They look at what we can do and email you a quote. You can also request a quote.

If you need a size that isn't listed

Some products can be printed in your own size. Read how to order a custom size.
