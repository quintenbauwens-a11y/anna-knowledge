How do I upload a double-sided or multi-page design?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-do-i-upload-a-double-sided-or-multi-page-design

Category: Before ordering

The easiest way is one PDF with every page in the order you want them printed; for a double-sided product that is one PDF with two pages. You can also upload several files for one product, and we combine them into one document for the check.

Upload one PDF

  1. Put all pages of your design in one PDF, in the order you want them printed.
  2. Upload the PDF on the product page, on the order confirmation page after checkout, or later from your order page.
  3. Check every page and both sides on your digital proof, then approve it.

Once you approve your digital proof, your order can go into production.

Upload several files for one product

If your pages are in separate files, you can upload several files for the same product on the product page, on the order confirmation page or on your order page. From your order page you can upload up to 10 files at once.

We combine your files into one document for the check. The total number of pages must not be more than the page count of the product you ordered. Check the page order on your digital proof before you approve it.

Example: a double-sided business card

For double-sided business cards, upload one PDF with two pages: page 1 is the front and page 2 is the back. We place each side automatically.

If you want different designs on the same product

Each design is ordered as its own product with its own file. See how to order products with different designs.

If you're making a stapled booklet

Stapled booklets have their own page count and page order rules. See the file requirements for stapled booklets.

If you need to change a file

You can replace your file until your order goes into production. We check the new file and send you a new digital proof to approve.

Once your order is in production, your file can no longer be changed.
