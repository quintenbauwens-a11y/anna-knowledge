What does the warning on my digital proof mean?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/what-does-the-warning-on-my-digital-proof-mean

Category: Creating your design

A warning on your digital proof is a note from our Basic design check: either a fix we already made to your file, or something only you can judge, such as a low-resolution image. Read every warning before you approve, because approving means you accept your file as the digital proof shows it.

Fixes we already made

Warnings marked Auto-applied are safe fixes we made for you. You don't need to do anything, but check that the digital proof still looks the way you want. Common ones:

  • RGB colours converted to CMYK. Read the difference between RGB and CMYK to see why colours can shift slightly.
  • Bleed extended to the edge. Read what bleed is.
  • Fonts embedded in your file, so your text prints as designed.

Warnings to check yourself

Warnings marked Check this point to something only you can judge. Look at each one on the digital proof:

  • Low-resolution image: the image may print blurry or pixelated. Read what resolution your images need.
  • Text or logo close to the trim line: it may be cut off. Read what safety margins are.

The coloured lines on the digital proof are not warnings; read what the lines and markings on your digital proof mean.

If the digital proof looks right

Approve your digital proof. Your order then goes into production. Approve before the deadline to keep your chosen delivery date.

If you want to fix something

Correct your design, then choose Upload new artwork and upload the new file. We check the new file and send you a new digital proof to approve. Read how to change your file after uploading for the steps and how long a design check takes.

If you're not sure what a warning means

Ask our chat assistant Anna, or contact our Customer Service team Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00, before you approve. We're happy to look at the warning with you.

Once you approve your digital proof, your order goes into production and you can't change your file.
