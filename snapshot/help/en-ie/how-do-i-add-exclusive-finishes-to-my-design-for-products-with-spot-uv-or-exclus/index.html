How do I add exclusive finishes to my design for products with spot-uv or exclusive foils?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-do-i-add-exclusive-finishes-to-my-design-for-products-with-spot-uv-or-exclus

Category: Creating your design

To ensure that the exclusive finishes are applied correctly, you need to provide two files:

  • One with your complete design (single or double-sided).
  • Another with only the elements of your design where the finish should be applied.

In the second file, make sure to indicate with 100% black where the exclusive finish should be applied. Keep in mind that the exclusive finish cannot be applied fully to the edge. For more information and templates, please refer to the product page of the desired product. There you will find a guide on how to correctly set up your files.
