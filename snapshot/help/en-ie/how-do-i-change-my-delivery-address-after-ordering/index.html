How do I change my delivery address after ordering?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-do-i-change-my-delivery-address-after-ordering

Category: Changes in delivery

  1. Open the order under My Account → Orders.
  2. Choose Change delivery address in the Self-service panel.
  3. Pick a saved address or enter a new one (include the Eircode), and save.

Two rules to know:

  • The new address must be in the same country as your order — each HelloPrint site delivers within its own country.
  • Once a parcel's delivery label is printed, the address is locked.
