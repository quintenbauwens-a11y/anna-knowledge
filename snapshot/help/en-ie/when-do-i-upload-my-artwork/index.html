When do I upload my artwork?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/when-do-i-upload-my-artwork

Category: Before ordering

You can upload your artwork either before or after payment by following the steps below.

If you would like to upload your artwork before payment:

  1. Add the product you would like to order to your cart.
  2. Proceed to the payment page.
  3. Select "Continue to Pay later, upload my artwork first".
  4. Proceed to upload your artwork.

If you would like to pay before uploading your artwork:

  1. Add the product you would like to order to your cart.
  2. Proceed to the payment page.
  3. Select any of the payment options to directly pay for your order (for more information about the payment options, check out this article).
  4. Proceed to upload your artwork.

Once your order is in production, you can't make any changes to your artwork, so please make sure to thoroughly check everything before approving.
