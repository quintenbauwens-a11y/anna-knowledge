When do I upload my artwork?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/when-do-i-upload-my-artwork

Category: Before ordering

You can upload your file before or after you pay. To get your chosen delivery date, upload your file and approve your digital proof before the deadline shown next to that delivery option.

Upload on the product page, before you pay

  1. Set your options and choose your delivery date on the product page.
  2. Upload your file.
  3. Go to your cart and pay with one of the payment methods.

What happens next: if your file passes the Basic design check, your digital proof is ready in about 5 minutes, before you pay. Check it and approve it to send your order to production.

Upload on the order confirmation page, after checkout

  1. Set your options, choose your delivery date and pay for your order.
  2. Upload your file on the order confirmation page that opens after checkout.

What happens next: we check your file and send you a digital proof to approve. Production only starts after you approve your digital proof.

Upload later from your order page

  1. Open the order in Orders.
  2. Upload your file on the order page.
  3. Check your digital proof and approve it.

What happens next: we check your file and send you a digital proof to approve. How long a design check takes tells you when to expect it.

Where to find your deadline

The deadline is at the bottom of the product page, next to each delivery option. After you order, it is also on your order page. Read how to make sure your order goes into production on time.

If you want to change your file after uploading

You can replace your file until your order goes into production. A new file means a new digital proof to approve. See how to change your file after uploading.

If you approve your digital proof after the deadline, your delivery date moves by one working day.
