How much ink coverage should I use for the best end result?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-much-ink-coverage-should-i-use-for-the-best-end-result

Category: Creating your design

Use a maximum ink coverage of 300% for most of our products. Ink coverage is the total of the cyan, magenta, yellow and black percentages in one spot of your design, and too much ink can cause smudging, bleeding or even damage to the paper.

How ink coverage is measured

Add up the percentages of the four CMYK inks in a colour. For example, a black made of 91% cyan, 79% magenta, 62% yellow and 97% black has an ink coverage of 329%, which is above the 300% maximum. Read more about ink absorption.

Check your darkest colours

  1. Find the darkest colours in your design, such as black backgrounds and deep blues or browns.
  2. Add up the cyan, magenta, yellow and black percentages of each dark colour.
  3. Lower the percentages of any colour that adds up to more than 300%, then export your file again.

If your design has small black text

Use 100% black for small black text, not a mix of four inks: a mix can make small text look blurry. See the difference between rich black and 100% black.

If you're not sure about your product

The 300% maximum applies to most of our products.

Above 300% ink coverage, the paper can't absorb all the ink, so your printed product can smudge.
