How much ink coverage should I use for the best end result?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-much-ink-coverage-should-i-use-for-the-best-end-result

Category: Creating your design

Ink coverage refers to the percentage of ink used on a printed product. The more ink used, the higher the coverage, which can affect the final appearance of the printed piece. For example, too much ink can cause smudging, bleeding, or even damage to the paper. This is why we recommend using a maximum ink coverage of 300% for most of our products.
