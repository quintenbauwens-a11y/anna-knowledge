When do I get free shipping?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/when-do-i-get-free-shipping

Category: Shipping information

Shipping is free when your order total is over €60 excluding VAT. Free shipping doesn't apply to custom-size products, such as banners, custom posters and custom stickers.

How free shipping works

  • Add your products to your cart. Once the total reaches €60 excluding VAT, shipping becomes free.
  • Below €60 excluding VAT, the shipping fee depends on which products and quantities you order.
  • Your cart shows the shipping fee before you pay.

If your order includes custom-size products

Free shipping doesn't apply to products in a custom size, such as banners, custom posters and custom stickers. For these products the shipping fee is shown in your cart.

If you pay extra for faster delivery

A faster delivery option is about production speed, not shipping. You pay separately for a faster turnaround for each design or product, because it costs more to produce. Shipping itself stays free above €60 excluding VAT. Getting your order delivered faster explains your options.
