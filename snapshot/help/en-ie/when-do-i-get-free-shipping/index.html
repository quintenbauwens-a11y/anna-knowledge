When do I get free shipping?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/when-do-i-get-free-shipping

Category: Shipping information

Want to save on shipping costs? At HelloPrint we make that easy for you. Spend over €60 excluding VAT and the shipping is on the house. Simply add your favourite business cards, flyers or stationery to your cart and once the total amount reaches €60 excluding VAT, shipping becomes free.

The shipping fees might differ, depending on which product and amounts you are ordering.

Please note: this offer does not apply to products of custom size, such as banners and custom posters, custom stickers etc.
