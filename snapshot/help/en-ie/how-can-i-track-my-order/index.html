Where is my order and how do I track it?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-can-i-track-my-order

Category: Tracking delivery

Your order page always shows where your order is: before shipping you see its status, and once it has shipped you see the track and trace link for each parcel. We also email you the tracking information as soon as your order ships.

Track your order

  1. Go to your orders.
  2. Open the order you want to track.
  3. Check the status of each product in the order.
  4. If a parcel has shipped, click Track to see the carrier's latest delivery date.

Products in one order can ship in separate parcels, each with its own link. See tracking a multi-parcel order.

If your order hasn't shipped yet

Your order page shows the current status, for example that your order is in production. See what your order status means. You receive the tracking email once your order ships.

If the track and trace link doesn't work

The link usually only works once the carrier has scanned your parcel. See track and trace code not working.

If you have a question about a parcel on its way

The carrier's tracking page shows the latest delivery information for your parcel. See also which carriers we use.

If your parcel is late or hasn't arrived

If the track and trace link shows a new date, see why your delivery date changed. If your parcel hasn't arrived, see package not delivered.
