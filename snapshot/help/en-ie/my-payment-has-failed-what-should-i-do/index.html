My payment has failed, what should I do?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/my-payment-has-failed-what-should-i-do

Category: Payment

If your payment failed, first check whether the amount was taken from your account.

If the amount wasn't taken from your account

  1. Place your order again.
  2. At checkout, choose a different payment method, such as PayPal or bank transfer.
  3. Complete your payment.

What happens next: once your order is placed, you receive an order confirmation email with your order number.

If you pay by bank transfer

Transfer the amount to our bank details and put your order number in the payment reference, so we can match your payment to your order. We start production planning once your payment arrives in our account.

If the amount was taken from your account

Don't pay again. Check your inbox for your order confirmation. If you paid twice, see what happens when you overpay.

We start production planning only once your payment arrives, so a late payment can affect your delivery date.
