What you can do in My Account

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/what-can-i-do-in-my-account

Category: Account

My Account is your home base after you order — track and manage every order, download invoices, and keep your details and company settings in one place. Here's what's inside:

  • Orders — live status, parcel tracking, proof approval, cancellations and reorders.
  • Invoices — every invoice and credit note as PDF.
  • Addresses — your address book and default delivery address.
  • Details & settings — password, email preferences, invoice email.
  • Company account — colleagues, VAT details, Pay on Invoice.
