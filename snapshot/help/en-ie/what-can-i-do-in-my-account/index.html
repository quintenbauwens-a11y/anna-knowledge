What can I do in My Account?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/what-can-i-do-in-my-account

Category: Account

My Account is where you manage everything after you order: your orders, invoices, addresses, personal details, quotes and complaints. You use one account for every Helloprint country website.

Orders

Open Orders to see the status of each order, track your parcel, approve your digital proof, replace your file, change your delivery address, cancel an order or repeat a previous order.

Invoices

Open Invoices to download every invoice and credit note as a PDF.

Addresses

Open Addresses to manage your address book and your default delivery address.

Personal details

Open Personal details to change your password, update your company and VAT details, change the email address for your invoices or set your email preferences under Communication preferences: see how to unsubscribe from the newsletter.

Quotes

Open Quotes to see the quotes you requested. To ask for a new one, see how to request a quote.

Complaints

If something is wrong with a delivered order, open the order in Orders and report the problem: how to file a complaint explains what to include.

If you can't sign in

Reset your password. We email you a link to choose a new password.
