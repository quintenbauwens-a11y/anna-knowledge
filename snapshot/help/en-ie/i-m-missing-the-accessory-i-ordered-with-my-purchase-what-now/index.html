I'm missing the accessory I ordered with my purchase. What now?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/i-m-missing-the-accessory-i-ordered-with-my-purchase-what-now

Category: After ordering

Accessories are sometimes shipped separately, so your missing accessory may still be on its way in another parcel. If no other parcel is on the way, file a complaint within 10 days of delivery and our Customer Service team looks into it.

Check whether the accessory is in another parcel

  1. Log in and open Orders.
  2. Click Order details on your order.
  3. Check the parcels on your order page: each parcel has its own tracking link.

If you received more than one tracking code, the accessory may be in the parcel that hasn't arrived yet. Follow that parcel with its tracking link. See tracking an order with several parcels.

If the tracking shows the parcel was delivered

Check with your neighbours and look carefully for safe places where the carrier may have left the parcel.

If you still can't find the accessory

  1. File a complaint within 10 days of delivery.
  2. Mention your order number, the items you received and the accessory that is missing.
  3. Add photos of the products you received, the box and the shipping label.

Our Customer Service team then looks into it and gets back to you. Where the fault is ours, we'll put it right, usually with a reprint, refund or credit.

If other products are missing too

See what to do if your order arrived incomplete.

Report a missing accessory within 10 days of delivery: after that, the complaint term has passed.
