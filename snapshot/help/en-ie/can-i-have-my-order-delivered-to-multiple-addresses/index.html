Can I have my order delivered to multiple addresses?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/can-i-have-my-order-delivered-to-multiple-addresses

Category: Delivery address

No: all products in one order go to the same delivery address. To send products to different addresses, place a separate order for each address.

Send products to different addresses

  1. Add the products for the first address to your cart.
  2. At checkout, choose or enter that delivery address and pay for your order.
  3. Place a new order for each other address in the same way.

Each order is delivered to its own address. Before you split your products over several orders, check when you get free shipping.

If you want the same design at a second address

Repeat your previous order and choose the other delivery address at checkout.

If you choose Saturday delivery

Enter a consumer (home) address, not a business address: we need a consumer address to deliver on Saturday.

If an address is in another country

An order is delivered within the country you ordered for. Place the order for that address on the Helloprint site for that country: see delivery to another country.

If you've already ordered

You can change the delivery address of your order until it goes into production: see changing your delivery address.

If you're sending a large order to many addresses

Email our bespoke team at bespoke@helloprint.com or request a quote before you order, and we look at the possibilities with you.
