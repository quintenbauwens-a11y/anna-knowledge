Can I use a PO Box as a delivery address?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/can-i-use-a-po-box-as-a-delivery-address

Category: Delivery address

No, we deliver to residential and business addresses, not to a PO Box. Enter a street address where your parcel can be handed over, and if you choose Saturday delivery, use a home address.

Enter your delivery address

At checkout, enter a residential or business address as your delivery address. Check the house number, unit and Eircode, so the carrier can find you. To use the same address again, save it in Addresses in My Account: see how to manage your saved addresses.

If you choose Saturday delivery

Enter a consumer (home) address, not a business address. We need a consumer address to deliver your order on a Saturday.

If you already entered a PO Box

Change the delivery address on your order page while you still can: changing your delivery address explains until when and how. Your order is then delivered to the new address.

If nobody is there to receive your parcel

The carrier has several options, such as a new attempt or a pick-up point: see what happens after an unsuccessful delivery attempt.

If you choose Saturday delivery and enter a business address, we can't deliver your order on the Saturday.
