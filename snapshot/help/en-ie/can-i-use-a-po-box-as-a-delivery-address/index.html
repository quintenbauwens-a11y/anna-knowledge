Can I use a PO Box as a delivery address?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/can-i-use-a-po-box-as-a-delivery-address

Category: Delivery address

Unfortunately, this isn’t an option. Please always provide a residential or business address.

Please note: Have you opted for Saturday delivery? Then do not provide a business address. To ensure that your delivery actually takes place on Saturday, we need a consumer address."
