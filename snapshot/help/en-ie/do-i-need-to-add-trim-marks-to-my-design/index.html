Do I need to add trim marks to my design?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/do-i-need-to-add-trim-marks-to-my-design

Category: Design definitions

No. Upload your file without trim marks (also called crop marks): we add them during our design check.

What your file needs instead

Instead of trim marks, your file needs bleed and a safety margin. File requirements for paper products lists the exact sizes and settings.

Where trim marks appear

Your digital proof shows the lines where we cut your product. The lines and markings on your digital proof explains what each one means.

If your file already has trim marks

Export your file again with crop marks turned off, then upload it. If you have already uploaded it, replace your file and we create a new digital proof for you to check.

Registration colour

Registration colour prints 100% of each CMYK ink and is only meant for printer's marks. Don't use it for text or shapes in your design, because it can make text blurry or leave smudges on your product.
