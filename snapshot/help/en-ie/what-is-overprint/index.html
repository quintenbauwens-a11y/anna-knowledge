What is overprint?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/what-is-overprint

Category: Design definitions

Overprint is a print setting where one colour is printed on top of another, so the inks mix where they overlap instead of the top colour replacing the one underneath. We print the overprint settings in your file as they are: overprint is a design choice, so we don't check or correct it.

How overprint changes your colours

Picture a red circle on top of a yellow square. Without overprint, the red circle prints red. With overprint switched on for the red circle, the area where the circle and the square overlap prints orange, because the red and yellow inks mix.

Designers use overprint on purpose, for example for small black text on a coloured background. If you didn't set overprint on purpose, colours in your printed product can look different from your design.

Overprint and our design check

Overprint is a design choice, so we don't check or correct it in your design, also not in the Basic, Premium or Deluxe design check. Check the overprint settings in your file yourself before you upload it.

Check overprint before you upload

  • Turn on the overprint preview in your design software, for example View > Overprint Preview in Adobe Illustrator or InDesign.
  • Make sure white text and white shapes are not set to overprint.
  • Check light colours placed on top of darker colours: with overprint they mix with the colour underneath.
  • Leave overprint on only where you want the colours to mix.

If you spot an overprint problem

If you spot an overprint problem in your file or on your digital proof, don't approve your digital proof yet. Correct the overprint settings in your design and replace your file. We then create a new digital proof for you to check and approve.

White text or shapes set to overprint can disappear in print, so turn overprint off for every white element.
