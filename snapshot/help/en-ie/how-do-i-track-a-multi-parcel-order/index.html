How do I track a multi-parcel order?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-do-i-track-a-multi-parcel-order

Category: Tracking delivery

Each parcel in your order has its own tracking link: open the order in My Account and you see a card for every parcel, with the carrier, the tracking link, the expected date and the items inside. Orders with several products often ship in several parcels, sometimes on different days.

Track each parcel

  1. Go to Orders in My Account and open your order.
  2. Find the parcel cards under the shipped step of your order.
  3. Click the tracking link on a parcel card to open the carrier's tracking page.

The carrier's page shows the latest status and expected delivery date of that parcel. You also find a Track parcel link on each product in your order.

Why your order comes in several parcels

Products in one order can be made by different production partners and ship separately, on different days. That's normal: one parcel arriving doesn't mean the others aren't on their way. Check the card of each parcel.

If a tracking link doesn't work

The link usually works once the carrier has scanned your parcel. See track and trace code not working.

If a parcel hasn't arrived on its expected date

See what to do if your package hasn't been delivered.

If all parcels show delivered but something is missing

Check the items listed on each parcel card first. If a product is still missing, see what to do when your order arrived incomplete. Our Customer Service team looks at the possibilities with you.

Report a missing product within 10 days after delivery, because that is the complaint term.
