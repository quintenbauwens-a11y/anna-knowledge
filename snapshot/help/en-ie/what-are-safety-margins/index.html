What are safety margins?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/what-are-safety-margins

Category: Design definitions

A safety margin is the space between the edge of your printed product and the text, logos and other important parts of your design. Keeping important elements inside the safety margin means nothing gets cut off and your design looks balanced after cutting.

Why safety margins matter

We cut every product to its final size after printing, and the cut can vary slightly. Text or logos that sit too close to the edge can be cut off or look unbalanced on your printed product.

Keep your design inside the safety margin

  • Place text, logos and other important elements inside the safety margin.
  • For paper products, keep text and logos at least 5 mm inside the final size: see file requirements for paper products.
  • Let background colours and images run past the edge into the bleed, so there are no white edges.
  • Avoid a border or frame along the edge: see borders and frames in your design.

Other guides in our templates

Besides the safety margin, our templates can show other guides: folding lines, perforation lines, non-printable areas, non-visible areas and the places where glue is applied. Keep important parts of your design away from these guides too.

Check the safety margin on your digital proof

Your digital proof shows where we cut your product. Before you approve, check that no text or logo crosses or touches those lines: the lines and markings on your digital proof explains what each line means. If something is too close to the edge, replace your file; we check the new file and send you a new digital proof to approve.

Text or logos outside the safety margin can be cut off when we trim your product.
