Why is my order "On hold"?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/why-is-my-order-on-hold

Category: After ordering

On hold means your order is paused. Open it under My Account → Orders — the banner there shows which of the two kinds applies:

  • Action needed (from you) — usually a proof waiting for approval, new artwork needed, or a delivery detail to confirm. The banner names the action; doing it releases the order immediately.
  • On hold (with us) — we're resolving something on our side. No action needed from you, and if it affects your delivery date we'll contact you.
