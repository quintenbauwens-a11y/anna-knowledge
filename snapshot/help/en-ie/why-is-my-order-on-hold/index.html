Why is my order "On hold"?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/why-is-my-order-on-hold

Category: After ordering

On hold means your order is paused. Open your order in Orders in My Account: the label and banner there show whether you need to do something or whether we are sorting it out.

If your order shows Action needed

We need something from you before your order can continue, for example your approval of the digital proof, a new file, or a delivery detail to confirm. The banner on your order page names the action.

What happens next: once you have done the action, your order continues to the next stage.

If your order shows On hold

We are resolving something on our side, and you don't need to do anything.

See what your order status means for all other statuses.
