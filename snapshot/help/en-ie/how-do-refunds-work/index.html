How refunds work

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-do-refunds-work

Category: Payment

Refunds always go back to your original payment method, automatically.

  • After a cancellation — once your cancellation is confirmed you receive a credit note and the refund is triggered. Allow 3–8 working days depending on your bank.
  • Overpaid? We detect it and refund the difference without you asking.
  • After an issue — once your issue is resolved and a refund agreed, allow 3–8 working days for it to reach your account, depending on your bank.
