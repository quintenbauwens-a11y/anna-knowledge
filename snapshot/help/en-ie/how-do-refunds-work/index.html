When will I get my refund?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-do-refunds-work

Category: Payment

Your refund goes back automatically to the payment method you used and usually reaches your account within 3 to 8 working days, depending on your bank. The refund starts as soon as your cancellation is confirmed or a refund has been agreed.

When a refund starts

  • After a cancellation: once your cancellation is confirmed, you receive a credit note and the refund starts.
  • After paying too much: we refund the difference without you having to ask. See overpaid orders.
  • After a complaint: once your complaint is resolved and a refund is agreed, the refund starts.

If your refund hasn't arrived

  • Check the account you paid with. The refund goes back to the original payment method, not to another card or bank account.
  • Check whether a new order replaced the cancelled one. Your payment may have been moved to the replacement order instead of being refunded.
  • Check your open invoices. If an invoice in your invoices is still unpaid, the refund can be held until that payment is settled.
