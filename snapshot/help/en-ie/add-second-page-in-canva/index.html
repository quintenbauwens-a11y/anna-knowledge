How do I add a second page in Canva?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/add-second-page-in-canva

Category: Canva

Click + Add page below your design in Canva to add a second page. For a double-sided product, page 1 is printed on the front and page 2 on the back.

Add a second page

  1. On the product page, click Design for free with Canva, or open your design from your cart with Edit your design.
  2. Click + Add page below your first page.
  3. Design the back of your product on page 2.
  4. Scroll between the pages to check both sides, then finish your design as usual.

What happens next

Your design goes to your cart with both pages. We check your design and create a digital proof. Check the front and the back on your digital proof, then approve it to start production.

If you want to change your design

Until you pay, click Edit your design in your cart to go back to the design editor.

If you want only one side printed

Choose a single-sided option on the product page and keep your Canva design to one page.

If you need more than two pages

Multi-page products such as booklets have their own page order rules. See how to upload a double-sided or multi-page design and the file requirements for stapled booklets.

You can change your Canva design only until you pay, so check both pages in your cart before you pay.
