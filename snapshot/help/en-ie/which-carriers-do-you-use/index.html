Which carriers do you use?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/which-carriers-do-you-use

Category: Shipping information

We work with several carriers, and the carrier can differ per order and per parcel. Once your order has shipped, your order page shows the carrier and the tracking link for each parcel.

Find the carrier for your order

  1. Go to your orders.
  2. Open the order.
  3. Find the carrier on the card of each parcel and click Track.

The carrier's tracking page opens with the latest delivery status of that parcel. Tracking your order explains what you see before and after shipping.

Your carrier and delivery date

We arrange the carrier for each parcel. What you choose when you order is your delivery date and delivery option on the product page: how long delivery takes explains the options.

If your order arrives in more than one parcel

Orders with several products can ship in several parcels, sometimes on different days and with different carriers. Each parcel has its own tracking link: tracking a multi-parcel order shows how to follow them all.

If you have a question about a parcel on its way

Check the carrier's tracking page first: it shows the delivery status and any delivery attempt.
