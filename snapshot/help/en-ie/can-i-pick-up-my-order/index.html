Can I pick up my order?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/can-i-pick-up-my-order

Category: Delivery options

No, picking up your order isn't possible. We print at many production locations across Europe, so there is no central pick-up point: we deliver every order to the address you choose.

Why there is no pick-up option

After you order, we choose the production location and carrier that get your order to you most efficiently. Each production partner has its own working methods and logistics and is usually not set up to receive customers on site. Fixed pick-up points would stop us from switching suppliers and carriers when that gets your order to you faster or at a better price.

Get your order where you need it

  • Workplace or another address: choose the address at checkout. To change the address of an order you have already placed, see how to change your delivery address.
  • More than one address: delivery to multiple addresses explains your options.
  • A fair or event: we can't guarantee delivery to a fair or event, so we advise a home or business address. Delivery to a fair or event explains why.

If you need your order sooner

Before you order, choose your delivery date on the product page. Faster delivery explains your options.
