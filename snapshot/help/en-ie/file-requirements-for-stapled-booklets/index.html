What are the file requirements for stapled booklets?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/file-requirements-for-stapled-booklets

Category: Creating your design

A file for stapled booklets is one continuous PDF with 8 to 64 pages, in print order. Page 1 is the front cover and the last page is the back cover.

Page order

  • Page 1 of your PDF becomes the front cover.
  • Page 2 of your PDF is the first inside page, on the left when the booklet is open.
  • The last page of your PDF becomes the back cover.

Check before you export

  • Page count. Make sure it matches your order.
  • Colours. Matte and gloss paper show colours differently, so keep your chosen paper in mind.
  • Fonts and images. Embed everything in the PDF.
  • Overprint. Check your layers for unwanted overprint settings.

For bleed, safe zone and colour mode, follow the file requirements for paper products.

After you upload

We check your file and create your digital proof. Check every page before you approve it.
