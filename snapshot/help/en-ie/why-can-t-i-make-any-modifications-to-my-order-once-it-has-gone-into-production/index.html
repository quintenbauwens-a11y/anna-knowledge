Why can't I make any modifications to my order once it has gone into production?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/why-can-t-i-make-any-modifications-to-my-order-once-it-has-gone-into-production

Category: After ordering

Because once you approve your digital proof, your order goes straight into production: we start printing your file immediately, so the file can no longer be changed and the print run can't be paused or sped up. Until you approve, you can still replace your file.

What happens when you approve

By clicking Approve & send to production, you confirm that your digital proof is exactly how your print should look. Production starts immediately and your file goes to print as it is on your digital proof. How to approve your digital proof explains each step.

What to check before you approve

  • Text, images and logos are all there and not cut off.
  • Spelling and typing: we print your text exactly as it is in your file.
  • Every page and both sides of your design.
  • The notes on your digital proof.

If you spot a mistake before you approve

Don't approve your digital proof yet. Click Upload new artwork to replace your file. We check your new file and send you a new digital proof to approve. How to change your file after uploading explains the steps.

If you want to change something else before production

In most cases, as long as your order isn't in production, we can still make changes. See what you can change in your order.

If your order is already in production

Your file can no longer be changed, and the print run can't be paused or sped up. What your order status means shows whether your order is in production.

If something is wrong when your order arrives

You can file a complaint up to 10 days after delivery. See how to file a complaint.
