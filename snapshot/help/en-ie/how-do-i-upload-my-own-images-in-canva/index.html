How do I upload my own images in Canva?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-do-i-upload-my-own-images-in-canva

Category: Canva

You can upload your own images to use in your designs. From the editor side panel, click the tab “uploads”. Select and drag the image to upload it or click the “upload” button. Once it’s been uploaded, you can click on the image to add it to your design. You can then easily resize your images, adjust their settings or apply special filters and effects within Canva!
