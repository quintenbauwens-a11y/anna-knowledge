Why is there a payment from Airwallex on my bank statement?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/why-is-there-a-payment-from-airwallex-on-my-bank-statement

Category: Payment

Airwallex is our payment partner. Some refunds and returned bank transfers from HelloPrint show Airwallex as the sender on your bank statement, for example "Airwallex (UK) Limited". The payment is from us.

Who the payment is from

  • Sender: HelloPrint or Airwallex, depending on how we send the payment.
  • Payment details: many banks also show Helloprint B.V., the company the payment is made for.
  • Description: a reference from us.

Why you received money from us

  • A refund by bank transfer: after a cancellation, an overpayment or a complaint. See how refunds work for when it arrives.
  • A payment we couldn't match: if we can't link your bank transfer to an invoice or order, we send it back to the account it came from.

If your payment was sent back

  1. Find your invoice number in Invoices or your order number in Orders, in My Account.
  2. Transfer the amount again to the bank account on your invoice. See HelloPrint's bank details.
  3. Put your invoice or order number in the payment reference.

Always pay to the bank details on your invoice

Never send a payment to the account a refund or returned payment came from. We don't receive payments on that account.
