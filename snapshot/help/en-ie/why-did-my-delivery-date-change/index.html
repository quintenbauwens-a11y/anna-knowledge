Why did my delivery date change?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/why-did-my-delivery-date-change

Category: After ordering

Most often, your order shows the latest date of the range from the product page, or your digital proof was approved after the deadline. If something delayed your order, we email you.

If you're seeing a different date in the range

The product page shows a date range, from the earliest to the latest possible delivery date, and your order can show the latest date of that range. Once your order has shipped, your track and trace link has the most accurate date: track your order.

If your digital proof was approved after the deadline

You'll see this if you approved your digital proof after the deadline shown next to your delivery option on the product page, or on your order page. In that case your delivery date automatically moves by one working day, and we can't change that afterwards. For your next order, upload and approve before the deadline: see making sure your order goes into production on time.

If something delayed your order

You'll see this if we emailed you about stock, shipping or your file. Follow the steps in the email. If you'd like to talk it through, chat with our chat assistant Anna, or contact our Customer Service team (Monday to Friday, 09:00–17:00), and we look together at what we can do. You can also see whether faster delivery is possible.
