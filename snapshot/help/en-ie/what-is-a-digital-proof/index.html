What is a digital proof?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/what-is-a-digital-proof

Category: Artwork check & digital proof

A digital proof is a preview of your design exactly as we will print it. You check it on your order page, and once you approve it, your order goes into production.

Get your digital proof

Every order gets a free Basic design check. If your file passes the Basic design check, you get your digital proof before payment. If your file needs a manual check, that check starts once we receive your payment and takes longer. How long a design check takes gives the current times.

Want our specialists to look at your design as well? Choose a Premium or Deluxe design check in your cart.

What to check before you approve

  • Text, images and logos are all there and not cut off.
  • Every page and both sides, if your product is double-sided.
  • Cut marks. If your digital proof shows cut marks, it includes bleed.
  • Notes on your digital proof. A note means something was changed in your file or the file isn't fully suitable for printing, so take an extra look.

Approve your digital proof

When everything looks right, approve your digital proof on your order page. How to approve your digital proof shows the steps. Approve before the deadline on your order page: if you approve after the deadline, your delivery date moves by one working day.

If something is wrong on your digital proof

Leave the digital proof unapproved and replace your file. We then create a new digital proof for you to check.

Once you approve, printing starts and your file can't be changed, so approve only when you are 100% happy.
