How can I find the right size in Canva?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-can-i-find-the-right-size-in-canva

Category: Canva

Your Canva design already has the right size, because the Canva editor opens at the size you chose on our product page. To check the size, click File in the top-left corner of the Canva editor.

Start your Canva design at the right size

  1. On the product page, choose your size and your other options first.
  2. Click Design for free with Canva.
  3. Create your design in the Canva editor, which opens at the size you chose.

Place text and logos well inside the edges so nothing important is cut off: see safety margins.

Check the size of your design

  1. Open your design in the Canva editor.
  2. Click File in the top-left corner.
  3. Read the dimensions of your design in the menu that opens.

If you need a different size

Custom sizes aren't available in Canva x Helloprint yet, so choose one of the standard sizes on the product page and start your Canva design from there. For a size we don't offer as standard, create your design in other design software and upload your own file: see ordering a custom size.

If your Canva design is already in your cart

Until you pay, click Edit your design in your cart to go back to the design editor. After payment, see how to change your Canva design after you accept it.
