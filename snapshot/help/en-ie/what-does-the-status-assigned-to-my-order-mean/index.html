What does my order status mean?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/what-does-the-status-assigned-to-my-order-mean

Category: Before ordering

Your order status shows which stage your order is in: Submitted, Artwork, Production, Shipped or Delivered. You find it in Orders in your account, next to each order.

Find your order status

  1. Log in and open Orders.
  2. Find your order in the list. Your most recent order is at the top, and the status is shown next to it.
  3. Click Order details to see the status of each product in your order.

The status updates automatically at every stage. If we need something from you, such as a file or your approval, your order page says what to do.

What each status means

  • Submitted: we have received your order. If you haven't uploaded your file yet, you can upload it from your order page.
  • Artwork: we are checking your file and preparing your digital proof. Once your digital proof is ready, approve it to send your order to production.
  • Production: your order is being printed, finished and packed.
  • Shipped: your order is on its way. Your order page shows the track and trace link, so you can track your order.
  • Delivered: the carrier has delivered your order.

If your order shows On hold

Your order is paused until something is resolved. The banner on your order page says whether you need to do something or whether we are resolving it. Why an order is On hold explains both situations.

If your order shows Cancelled

Your order, or a product in it, has been cancelled and will not be printed. How refunds work explains when you get your money back.

If your order stays in Artwork

Check whether your order page asks you to upload a new file or approve your digital proof. How long a design check takes tells you when to expect your digital proof.
