What does the status assigned to my order mean?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/what-does-the-status-assigned-to-my-order-mean

Category: Before ordering

Your order moves through five simple stages. Here's what each one means:

  • Submitted — we've received your order.
  • Artwork — we're checking your files and preparing your proof. We'll let you know if we need anything from you, or once your proof is ready to approve.
  • Production — your approved files are being prepared and printed.
  • Shipped — your order is on its way, and you'll have a tracking link to follow it.
  • Delivered — your order has arrived.

Two other states can appear alongside these:

  • On hold — occasionally an order pauses, for example if we need something from you. We'll show what's holding it up and what's needed to continue.
  • Cancelled — the order, or an item in it, has been cancelled.
