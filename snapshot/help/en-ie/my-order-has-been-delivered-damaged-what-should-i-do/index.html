My order has been delivered damaged. What should I do?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/my-order-has-been-delivered-damaged-what-should-i-do

Category: After delivery

We're sorry your order arrived damaged. File a complaint within 10 days of delivery and add clear photos or a video of the damage, the packaging and the shipping label, so our Customer Service team can look into it.

Take photos of the damage

  • The damaged products, clearly visible.
  • The box or packaging.
  • The shipping label on the packaging.

A short video works too, as long as the damage, the packaging and the shipping label can be seen.

Report the damage

Send your complaint with your photos or video from your order. How to file a complaint shows the steps and what to include.

What happens next

Our Customer Service team looks at your complaint and your photos. Where the fault is ours, we'll put it right, usually with a reprint, refund or credit.

If you need your products urgently

A complaint must be filed within 10 days of delivery.
