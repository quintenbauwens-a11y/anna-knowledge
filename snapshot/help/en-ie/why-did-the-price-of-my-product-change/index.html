Why did the price of my product change?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/why-did-the-price-of-my-product-change

Category: Payment

The price of your print product changes when you change an option, because every combination of size, paper, quantity, page count and delivery date has its own price. If you changed nothing, our prices may have been updated since you last looked.

If you changed an option

You'll see this when the price changes straight after you change the size, paper, finishing, quantity, page count or delivery date on the product page.

What you can do: compare a few options on the product page. The price updates each time you change an option, so you can see what each choice costs before you order.

If a bigger quantity or fewer pages costs more

You'll see this when a larger quantity costs more per piece than a smaller one, or when a booklet with fewer pages costs more than the same booklet with more pages.

This happens because we work with several printing partners across Europe. For each combination of options, our system picks the partner with the best price for exactly that combination, and each partner has its own price structure.

What you can do: try a quantity or page count close to the one you need and pick the best price. Need a quantity that isn't listed? See ordering a quantity that isn't on the website.

If your booklet is landscape

You'll see this when the same booklet costs more in landscape than in portrait.

This happens because landscape pages fit less efficiently on standard print sheets, so more paper is used. Many machines are set up for portrait, and binding a landscape booklet takes extra work.

What you can do: if your design also works in portrait, compare the portrait price on the product page.

If you chose a faster delivery date

A faster delivery date means a faster turnaround in production, and that has its own price for each product. See getting your order delivered faster.

If the price changed since you last looked

You'll see this when the same product with the same options costs more or less than it did on an earlier day.

This happens because we update our prices from time to time. The product page always shows the current price.

What you can do: for a larger order or a special project, request a quote and our team prepares a price for you.

If the total in your cart is different

You'll see this when the product price is the same, but the total in your cart is higher or lower.

What you can do: check the lines in your cart. The total can include shipping costs, extra services and VAT. Shipping is free above €60 excluding VAT. A discount code that no longer works is removed from the total.
