please note:

  • For optimal results, we recommend using the template specific to the product you're designing. Despite similar dimensions across products, nuances in trim, bleed, and safety margins may exist.
  • Designing with our on-site Canva tool? No need for separate templates. Canva will guide you with the correct trim, bleed, and safety margins in the final steps as you hand in your artwork.
