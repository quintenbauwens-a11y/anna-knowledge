Where can I find the template for my design?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/where-can-i-find-the-templates-for-my-design

Category: Creating your design

You find the template for your product on its product page, under Design Guidelines. The design guide for that product is in the same place.

Download your template

  1. Open the product page of the product you want to order.
  2. Scroll to Design Guidelines.
  3. Download the template for your product.

Place your design in the template, then save it as one PDF and upload it to your order. Our file requirements for paper products list what your file needs.

What the template shows

  • Trim line: the final size of your product, where we cut.
  • Bleed: the extra area outside the trim line that your background must fill.
  • Safety margin: the area inside the trim line where your text and logos must stay.

Download the design guide

The design guide for your product is in the same place, under Design Guidelines on the product page. The design guide explains how to prepare a design for that product.

If you design in Canva

You don't need a template. Canva sets the right size, bleed and safety margin for your product in the final steps before you hand in your design. Finding the right size in Canva explains how.

If you need help with your design

We don't create designs from scratch. Can we create a design for you? explains more.

Use the template for the exact product and size you order: a template from a similar product can have a different bleed or safety margin, and parts of your design can be cut off.
