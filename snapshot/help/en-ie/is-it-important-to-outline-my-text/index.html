Is it important to outline my text?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/is-it-important-to-outline-my-text

Category: Design dos and don'ts

Yes. Outline your text or embed your fonts before you save your file, so your text prints exactly as you designed it. If a font isn't outlined or embedded, text can go missing or be replaced by a different font.

Outlining and embedding

Outlining turns your text into shapes, so your file no longer needs the font to print it. Embedding includes all the information about a font or image within your file, so it is reproduced correctly during printing. Both keep your text looking the same in print as on your screen.

Prepare your text

  1. Finish your design and check your text: outlined text can no longer be edited as text.
  2. Save a copy of your design with editable text, so you can still make changes later.
  3. Convert your text to outlines in your design program, or embed all fonts when you export your PDF.

What happens next
