Can I order a custom product that isn't in your catalogue?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/custom-print-product-not-in-catalogue

Category: Before ordering

You can ask for one: request a quote with the details of the custom product you need, and our team looks at whether a printer in our production network can make it. You only order once you have our quote and accept the price.

What to include in your quote request

  • The type of product and what you will use it for.
  • The size in mm or cm.
  • The material and finish you want, if you know them.
  • The quantity, or several quantities if you want to compare prices.
  • The date you need the products delivered.
  • A reference photo or an example of a similar product, if you have one.

Send your quote request

  1. Open the quote request form.
  2. Fill in the details of your custom product.
  3. Send the form.

What happens next

Our team looks at whether a printer in our production network can make your custom product and, if so, sends you a quote with the price. See how to request a quote for what happens once you accept it. If we can't make this exact product, our quotes team looks at the possibilities with you, for example one of our standard products in a custom size or quantity.

For sales questions, special projects and large orders, you can also email bespoke@helloprint.com.

If you only need a different size or quantity

You don't need a quote for everything. Many products can be ordered in a custom size straight from the product page, and you can also ask for a quantity that isn't on the website.

A custom product needs time to be sourced and priced before production can start, so a quote requested close to your deadline may not make your delivery date.
