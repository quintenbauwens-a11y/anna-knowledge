Why am I paying shipping more than once?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/why-am-i-paying-shipping-more-than-once

Category: Shipping information

What you pay more than once is usually a faster turnaround, not shipping: shipping is free above €60 excluding VAT, but for each design or product you choose a faster turnaround for, you pay for that turnaround separately, because each one is produced separately.

Shipping costs

Shipping is free above €60 excluding VAT. See when you get free shipping for the conditions.

Faster turnaround per design or product

A faster delivery date is about turnaround: how quickly we produce your order. Each design or product is produced on its own, so a faster turnaround costs extra for each design or product, because of the production costs. See whether you can get your order delivered faster.

Example: three flyer designs

You order three different flyer designs in one checkout and choose a faster delivery date for each. Each design is produced separately, so you see the cost of the faster turnaround three times: once for each design.

If you want to pay less

  • Choose a later delivery date on the product page: the standard turnaround costs less than a faster one.
  • For large orders or many designs, email bespoke@helloprint.com or request a quote before you order.

If you have already ordered

As long as your order isn't in production, they look at the possibilities with you.
