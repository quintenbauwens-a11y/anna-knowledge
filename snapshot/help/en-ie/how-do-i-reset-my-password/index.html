How do I reset my password?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-do-i-reset-my-password

Category: Account

Choose Forgotten password? on the login page and enter the email address of your account: we email you a link to set a new password. If you can still log in and only want a new password, change it in your personal details instead.

Reset your password

  1. Go to the login page and choose Forgotten password?.
  2. Enter the email address you registered with. We send you a reset email within a few minutes.
  3. Open the email and click the link in it. A page opens where you can set your new password.
  4. Enter your new password and save it. You can now log in with your new password.

You have one Helloprint account for every country site, so your new password works on all of them.

If no reset email arrives

Check your spam folder first. If the reset email isn't there, you may have registered with a different email address: try the address on your order confirmation and request a new reset link.

Change your password while you're logged in

  1. Go to Personal details in My Account.
  2. In the Password section, click Change password.
  3. Enter your current password and your new password, and save.

What happens next: you use your new password from your next login.
