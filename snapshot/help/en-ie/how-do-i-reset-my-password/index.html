Reset your password or get back into your account

Category: Account

Can't get into your account? Here's how to get back in:

  1. Choose Forgotten password? on the login page — the reset link arrives by email within minutes (check your spam folder too).
  2. No email coming through? You may have registered with a different address — try the one on your order confirmation.
  3. Still locked out? Check you're on the right country shop — your account is linked to the country site you registered on.

None of that worked? Chat with us and we'll recover access together.

Want to change your password while you're logged in? Go to My Account → Details → Change password.
