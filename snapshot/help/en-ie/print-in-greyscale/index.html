How do I print in greyscale?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/print-in-greyscale

Category: Creating your design

To print in greyscale, set up your design using only black (K) and order your product in full colour (CMYK). Greys made from black only print neutral, without a colour tint.

Set up your design in greyscale

  • Create every element in your design with the K (black) channel only.
  • Don't use cyan (C), magenta (M) or yellow (Y) in any grey or black area.
  • Make lighter or darker greys by changing the K percentage only, for example 20% K, 50% K or 80% K.
  • For a darker grey, raise the K percentage instead of adding C, M or Y.

Why greys mixed from colour can look tinted

We print in CMYK. A grey that is mixed from cyan, magenta and yellow can shift slightly during printing and come out with a blue, red or green tint, or with greys that vary across the print. A grey made from K only stays neutral.

If your design has large black areas

For large black backgrounds, rich black and 100% black explains which black to choose for your design.

What happens next

Upload your file: it goes through our Basic design check and you get a digital proof. Check that all greys look neutral before you approve it. If they don't, correct your design and replace your file; we then create a new digital proof.
