How can I download designs from my previous orders?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-can-i-download-designs-from-my-previous-orders

Category: After ordering

Open the order in My Account: on the order page you can download the original file you uploaded and the print-ready PDF we used for printing.

Download your design

  1. Go to Orders in My Account.
  2. Open the order with the design you need.
  3. On the product in that order, download the file you uploaded, or the digital proof with Download proof PDF.

The file is saved to your device, so you can reuse it, edit it or send it to a designer.

When the print-ready PDF is available

You can download the print-ready PDF of your design once you have approved your digital proof. Before that, you can download the file you uploaded.

If you want to print the same design again

You don't need to download and upload your design again. Repeat your previous order straight from your order page.

If you want to change the design for a new order

Download your original file, make your changes and upload the new file with your new order. We check it and send you a new digital proof to approve.
