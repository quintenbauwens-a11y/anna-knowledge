Why does my Canva design look so blurry?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/why-does-my-canva-design-look-so-blurry

Category: Canva

A Canva design usually looks blurry because of low-quality images or because a filter softens your design. Use high-quality images and remove softening filters, and you can change your Canva design yourself until you pay for your order.

Low-quality images

You'll see this if a photo or logo looks pixelated or soft when you zoom in on your design. What you can do: replace the image with a higher-quality version, ideally 300 dpi at the size you print it. See what resolution is for how this works.

Filters that soften your design

You'll see this if you added a filter to an image or to your design and it looks less sharp than the original. What you can do: remove the filter and check whether the quality improves without it.

If your design is in your cart

  1. Open your cart.
  2. Click Edit your design to go back to the design editor.
  3. Replace the image or remove the filter, and save your design.

Your changed design replaces the one in your cart, and you can carry on to checkout.

If you have already paid for your order

You can no longer change your Canva design yourself after payment.

Once you pay, you can no longer edit your Canva design yourself, so check the sharpness of your images before checkout.
