Why does my Canva design look so blurry?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/why-does-my-canva-design-look-so-blurry

Category: Canva

If your design looks blurry or pixelated, there are two common factors that could cause this.

- Using low quality images can result in blurry or pixelated images. Where possible, upload only high quality images in 300 DPI.

- Have you used filters? Some filters have a ‘softening’ effect when applied to an image or design. Simply remove the filter and see if the quality improves without it.
