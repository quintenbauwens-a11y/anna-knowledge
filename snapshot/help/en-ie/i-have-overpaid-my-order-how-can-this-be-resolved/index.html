I have overpaid my order, how can this be resolved?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/i-have-overpaid-my-order-how-can-this-be-resolved

Category: Payment

If you paid twice for the same order, our system detects the extra payment and refunds it automatically. This can take up to 5 working days.

What happens next

The extra amount goes back to you automatically: you don't need to do anything. For how refunds reach your account, see when you get your refund.
