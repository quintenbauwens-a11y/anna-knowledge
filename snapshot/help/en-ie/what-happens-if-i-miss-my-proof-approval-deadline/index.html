What happens if I miss my proof approval deadline?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/what-happens-if-i-miss-my-proof-approval-deadline

Category: Artwork check & digital proof

Nothing dramatic. Your order isn't cancelled, and we never approve on your behalf — your file only prints when you approve it.

The one thing that changes is your delivery date: it moves back, usually by one working day, and keeps moving until you approve. Your order page shows both the original and the updated delivery date, plus the new deadline that locks it in.
