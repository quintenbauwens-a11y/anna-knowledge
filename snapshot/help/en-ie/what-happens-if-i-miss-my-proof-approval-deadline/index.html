What happens if I miss my proof approval deadline?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/what-happens-if-i-miss-my-proof-approval-deadline

Category: Artwork check & digital proof

Your delivery date automatically moves by one working day, and we can't change that. Your order isn't cancelled: it waits until you approve your digital proof.

What changes on your order

  • Your delivery date moves by one working day. If you still haven't approved your digital proof by the new deadline, the delivery date moves again.
  • Your order page shows your original delivery date, your updated delivery date and the new deadline.
  • We never approve your digital proof for you: your file is only printed once you approve it.

Approve your digital proof

Open your order and approve your digital proof. How to approve your digital proof explains each step. Once you approve, your order goes into production.

Where to find your deadline

Before you order, the deadline is at the bottom of the product page, next to each delivery option. After you order, it's on your order page. How to make sure your order goes into production on time explains how to make your deadline.

If you need your order sooner

See also getting your order delivered faster.
