Do I need to pay VAT?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/do-i-need-to-pay-vat

Category: Payment

VAT is included where it applies, and the exact amount is always shown itemised in your basket and on your invoice.

Whether VAT applies — and how much — can depend on the product and how it's used, so it's calculated for your specific order at checkout rather than quoted as a single rate. You'll always see the VAT before you complete your order.
