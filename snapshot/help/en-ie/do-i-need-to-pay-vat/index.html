Do I need to pay VAT?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/do-i-need-to-pay-vat

Category: Payment

Yes, when VAT applies to your product, we add VAT to your print order, and you see the exact amount in your cart before you pay. The VAT rate can differ per product, so we calculate it for your specific order.

Where you see the VAT

  • In your cart and at checkout, as a separate line before you pay.
  • On your invoice, itemised per product. See when you receive your invoice.

Why the VAT rate differs per product

Whether VAT applies, and at which rate, depends on the type of product and how it is used. That's why we work out the VAT for each order at checkout instead of using one rate for everything.

If you order for a business

Add your company name and VAT number in My Account, so they appear on your invoices. See whether you can order without VAT.

If your VAT details are wrong

Update your VAT number in the Company info card under Personal details in My Account: see updating your company and VAT details.
