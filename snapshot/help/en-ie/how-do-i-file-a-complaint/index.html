How do I file a complaint?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-do-i-file-a-complaint

Category: Complaints

If something isn't right with your order, you can let us know in a couple of minutes.

Open the order in My Account and select “Report a problem”, or go to My Account → Support and start a new message, choosing the category that fits.

Tell us what went wrong and we'll investigate. Where the fault is ours, we'll put it right — usually a reprint, refund or credit. You can follow your message, its status and our reply under My Account → Support; each one has its own reference (for example C-A3F9C2D1).
