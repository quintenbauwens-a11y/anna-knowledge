How do I add elements, borders and images in Canva?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-do-i-add-elements-borders-etc-when-using-canva

Category: Canva

You add elements, borders and images in Canva from the side panel of the editor: Elements for shapes, lines, frames and borders, Photos for stock images and Uploads for your own images. Click an item and it appears on your design straight away.

Add elements and borders

  1. In the side panel, click Elements.
  2. Type what you need in the search bar, for example "borders", or click See all to browse shapes, lines, illustrations, icons, frames, grids, stickers, charts and gradients.
  3. Click the element to add it to your design.

The element appears on your design. Drag it to move it and drag its corners to resize it.

Add stock images

  1. In the side panel, click Photos.
  2. Type keywords in the search bar or click one of the suggested categories.
  3. Click the image to add it to your design.

Images you have used are grouped under Recently Used on the Photos tab. To use an image as your background, right-click the image and choose the background option.

Upload your own images

  1. In the side panel, click Uploads.
  2. Drag your image into the panel, or click Upload and select it.
  3. Click the uploaded image to add it to your design.

Once your image is on your design, you can resize it, adjust its settings or apply filters and effects in Canva. If your own image looks soft or pixelated, read why a Canva design can look blurry.

If you add a border

Printing with borders or frames is at your own risk, because trimming can vary slightly and a border close to the edge can end up uneven. Before you place one, check how to use borders or frames in your design.
