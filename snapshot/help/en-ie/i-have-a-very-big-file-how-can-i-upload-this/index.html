I have a very big file, how can I upload this?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/i-have-a-very-big-file-how-can-i-upload-this

Category: Creating your design

You can upload files of up to 500 MB each, and you can upload several files for one product. If a single file is larger than 500 MB, make it smaller, split it into several files or send it to us via WeTransfer.

Upload files of up to 500 MB

Upload your file on the product page, on the order confirmation page after checkout, or later from your order page. Each file can be up to 500 MB. We check your file and send you a digital proof to approve; how long a design check takes tells you when to expect it.

Upload several files for one product

You can upload several files for one product, for example one file per page. From your order page you can upload up to 10 files at once. We combine your files into one document for the check, so the total number of pages can't be more than the product's page count. See uploading a double-sided or multi-page design.

Make your file smaller

  • Save your design as a PDF.
  • Use images at the resolution your print needs. Images at a higher resolution than the file requirements for paper products ask for make your file bigger, not your print sharper.
  • Delete hidden layers and elements you don't use in your design.
  • Split a multi-page design into several files of up to 500 MB each.

Don't compress your file into a ZIP or RAR file: we don't accept those. See which file type to use.

If your file is still larger than 500 MB

  1. Go to wetransfer.com and add your file.
  2. Enter hello@helloprint.com as the recipient.
  3. Enter your own email address.
  4. Add your order number in the message.
  5. Start the transfer.

We use your order number to link the file to your order.

If your file won't upload for another reason

If your file is under 500 MB and still won't upload, see what to do when your file won't upload.

If you approve your digital proof after the deadline, your delivery date moves by one working day, so send large files early.
