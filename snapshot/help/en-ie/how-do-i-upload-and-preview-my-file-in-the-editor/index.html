How do I upload and preview my file in the editor?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-do-i-upload-and-preview-my-file-in-the-editor

Category: Creating your design

On supported products, you can upload your file in the online editor on the product page and see your design placed on the product before you add it to your cart. The preview stays with the item in your cart and at checkout, so you always know which design belongs to which item.

Upload and preview your file

  1. Open the product page and choose your options.
  2. Choose the option to upload and preview your own file in the editor.
  3. Upload your file.
  4. Check the preview: the position, size and colours of your design on the product.
  5. Add the product to your cart.

What happens next: your preview shows with the item in your cart and at checkout. For most gift products, the preview is a mock-up of your design on the product.

What happens after you order

The editor preview is a quick visual check. After you order, your file goes through the free Basic design check and you get a digital proof: a preview of your design exactly as we will print it. How long a design check takes explains when your digital proof is ready. Check it and approve your digital proof to send your order to production.

If the preview doesn't look right

Before you add the product to your cart, adjust your design and upload the new file in the editor. After you order, you can still replace your file until your order goes into production: go to Orders → Order details → Change submitted files. A new file means a new digital proof to approve. See how to change your file after uploading.

If your product has no editor preview

Upload your file on the product page, on the order confirmation page after checkout, or later from your order page. Your digital proof is then your preview of the product.

Once you approve your digital proof, your order goes into production and your file can no longer be changed.
