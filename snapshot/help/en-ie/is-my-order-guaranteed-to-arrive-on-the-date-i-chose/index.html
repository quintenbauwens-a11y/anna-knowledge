Is my order guaranteed to arrive on the date I chose?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/is-my-order-guaranteed-to-arrive-on-the-date-i-chose

Category: Before ordering

Almost always: 98% of Helloprint orders arrive on the chosen date. Yours stays valid when you upload your file and approve your digital proof before the deadline on the product page.

Keep your delivery date

  1. Find your deadline at the bottom of the product page, next to each delivery option.
  2. Upload your file. You can do this before or after you pay.
  3. Approve your digital proof before the deadline: open your order and click Approve.

Once you approve, your order goes into production and keeps its delivery date.

What can still cause a delay

  • A problem with your file. We email you what to fix. Upload a corrected file and approve the new digital proof before your deadline.
  • The carrier. Weather or other events can delay a parcel. Your track and trace link shows the latest date. For extra certainty on your next order, add Secured Delivery in your cart.

If you approve your digital proof after the deadline, your delivery moves by one working day.
