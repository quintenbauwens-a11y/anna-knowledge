My file won't upload, what can I do?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/file-wont-upload

Category: Creating your design

A file that won't upload is usually larger than 500 MB, a file type we don't accept, or blocked by your browser or connection. You can also order now and upload your file later from your order page, as long as you upload and approve before the deadline.

The file is too large

You'll see this if your file is larger than 500 MB: you get a message that the file is too large, or the upload stops partway.

What you can do: make your file smaller, for example by saving your design as one PDF with images at the resolution your print needs. Uploading a very big file explains how, and what to do if your file is still too large.

The file type isn't accepted

You'll see this if your file can't be selected or you get a message about the file format. ZIP, RAR and GIF files are not accepted.

What you can do: export your design as a PDF, or save it as another file type we accept. Which file type to use lists all accepted file types.

You're uploading several files

You'll see this if some of your files don't appear when you upload many files at once from your order page: you can upload up to 10 files at once there.

What you can do: upload your files in groups of 10 or fewer. We combine your files into one document for the check, so the total number of pages must not be more than the page count of your product.

The PDF is password-protected or damaged

You'll see this if the upload fails straight away, even though your file is small and a PDF.

What you can do: open your design in your design program and export a new PDF without a password or editing restrictions, then upload the new PDF.

Your browser or connection is blocking the upload

You'll see this if the progress bar stops, or nothing happens when you upload, even with a small PDF.

What you can do: refresh the page and try again, or try another browser. Switch off a VPN and use a stable connection. If you have already ordered, your order stays saved: you can upload from your orders at any time before the deadline.

If you see "Preview not available"

Office files (DOC, DOCX, XLS, XLSX, PPT and PPTX) may show "Preview not available" after you upload them. Check your design on the digital proof we send you before you approve it.

If your file still won't upload

They look at the possibilities with you to get your file to us.

If you approve your digital proof after the deadline, your delivery date moves by one working day.
