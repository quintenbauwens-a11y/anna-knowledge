How do I pay on invoice?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-do-i-pay-on-invoice

Category: Invoice

We offer all customers an option to pay on invoice. To be eligible, you must have placed and completed at least three orders with us.

Payment terms  Once this option is available, you can place an order and pay for it within 14 days of receiving the invoice. This is our standard payment term.

Credit limit  Our standard credit limit is 250 euros. If you reach this limit, the option to pay by invoice will be temporarily disabled until outstanding invoices are paid.

We review requests on a case-by-case basis and will inform you if we can accommodate your needs. Payment reminders We'll send you a friendly reminder 3 days before your invoice is due. If an invoice is still unpaid after the due date, we'll send you a statement at most once every 7 days. From the second reminder, the option to pay on invoice is suspended until your open invoices are paid.
