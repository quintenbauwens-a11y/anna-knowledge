How do I pay on invoice?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-do-i-pay-on-invoice

Category: Invoice

You can choose Pay on invoice at checkout once you've placed and completed at least three orders with us. You then pay within 14 days of receiving the invoice, and your order goes into production as soon as you approve your digital proof.

Who can pay on invoice

  • You have placed and completed at least three orders with us.
  • Your open invoices stay within your credit limit of €250.
  • You have no invoices that are past their second payment reminder.

Pay on invoice comes with an extra charge: why there is an extra charge for Pay on invoice explains it.

Pay on invoice at checkout

  1. Add your products to your basket and go to checkout.
  2. Choose Pay on invoice as your payment method.
  3. Place your order.
  4. Upload your file and approve your digital proof.

Your order goes into production as soon as you approve your digital proof, without waiting for your payment. If you pay by bank transfer instead, we start production planning once your payment arrives.

Pay your invoice

We email your invoice once your order has shipped; when you'll receive your invoice explains the timing. Your 14-day payment term starts when you receive the invoice. Pay by bank transfer using the details on the invoice, or see our bank details.

If Pay on invoice isn't shown at checkout

Pay on invoice isn't shown if you've completed fewer than three orders, if your open invoices have reached your credit limit, or if an invoice is past its second payment reminder. You can choose another payment method for this order. For a higher credit limit, other payment terms or Pay on invoice before your third order, see how to increase your credit limit: our Customer Service team looks at the possibilities with you.

If an invoice reaches its second payment reminder, Pay on invoice is switched off until all your open invoices are paid.
