Where will they cut my final product?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/where-will-they-cut-my-final-product

Category: Creating your design

We cut your product along the trim line, which is the final size you chose on the product page. We add the crop marks that show where to cut ourselves, so you don't need to add them to your file.

What crop marks show

Crop marks are short lines just outside the corners of your design. They show the printer where to trim the material to its final size. Leave crop marks off your file: we add them for you.

Set up your file for cutting

  • Extend your background colours and images 3 mm past the final size on every side. This is the bleed.
  • Keep text and logos at least 5 mm inside the final size. This is the safety margin.
  • Save your file without crop marks.

The full list is in the file requirements for paper products.

If your design has a border or frame

Printing with a border or frame is at your own risk, because trimming can vary slightly and a border can end up thicker on one side. Using borders or frames in your design explains how to place one as safely as possible.

Check the cut on your digital proof

Your digital proof shows lines and markings for where we cut. Check that no text or logo sits too close to them before you approve. If something is too close, replace your file before your order goes into production. You then get a new digital proof to approve.

Text or logos closer than 5 mm to the final size can be cut off.
