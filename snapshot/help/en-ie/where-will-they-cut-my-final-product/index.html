Where will they cut my final product?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/where-will-they-cut-my-final-product

Category: Creating your design

We will know where to cut thanks to crop marks.

Crop marks are lines added to the artwork's edges to indicate where the piece will be trimmed after printing. These marks show the printer where to cut the material to achieve the desired finished size.
