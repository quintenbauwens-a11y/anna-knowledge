Creating a company account

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/creating-a-company-account

Category: Company account

To create a company account, go to “Create company account” in My Account. Add your company name to set up the account. You can also add your VAT number and Chamber of Commerce information.

You will be the first user of your company account and you can invite more users once the account is created.
