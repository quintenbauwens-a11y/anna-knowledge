How do I make sure my order goes into production on time?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-do-i-make-sure-my-order-goes-into-production-on-time

Category: Artwork check & digital proof

Upload your file and approve your digital proof before the deadline for your delivery date. Do both early to be safe.

Steps to make your deadline

  1. Find your deadline. It's at the bottom of the product page, next to each delivery option. If you already ordered, it's on your order page.
  2. Upload your file: on the product page, on the order confirmation page after checkout, or later from your order page. See how long a design check takes.
  3. Open your order and click Approve proof.
  4. Check your digital proof and click Approve & send to production.

Your order goes into production straight away.

If you miss the deadline

Your delivery date automatically moves by one working day; we can't change that. See what happens if you miss your proof approval deadline.

If your design isn't ready yet

Order now and upload your file at any time from your order page. Upload your file and approve your digital proof before the deadline to make your chosen delivery date.

Once your order is in production, you can't change your file.
