Can I change my delivery date?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/can-i-change-my-delivery-date

Category: Changes in delivery

Before you order, yes: you choose your delivery date yourself on the product page.

If you haven't ordered yet

Choose your delivery date on the product page. The deadline for uploading your file and approving your digital proof is at the bottom of the product page, next to each delivery option. A faster delivery date means a faster turnaround, which costs extra: see getting your order delivered faster.

If you've ordered and want your order sooner

They look at the possibilities for a faster delivery with you, including any extra cost, while your order isn't in production yet. Getting your order delivered faster explains each situation.

If you've ordered and want your order later

They look at the possibilities with you while your order isn't in production yet. You can also receive your order on the planned date and keep it until you need it.

If your order is already in production

Once you approve your digital proof, your order goes into production straight away. Your track and trace link shows the latest delivery date once your order has shipped.

If you won't be there on the delivery day

See what happens after an unsuccessful delivery attempt. To have your order delivered somewhere else, see changing your delivery address.

If your delivery date has changed

See why your delivery date changed.

If you approve your digital proof after the deadline, your delivery date automatically moves by one working day.
