Can I order a custom size?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-do-i-order-a-custom-size

Category: Before ordering

Yes, for selected products, currently stickers and panels: choose Custom size on the product page and enter your width and height in mm. For a product without the Custom size option, our bespoke team looks at what we can do for your size.

Order a custom size

  1. Open the product page of your sticker or panel.
  2. Choose Custom size in the product options.
  3. Enter the width and height in mm. The price updates straight away.
  4. Choose your other options and add the product to your cart.

What happens next

Set up your design at exactly the width and height you entered. See what bleed is and whether you need it. When you upload your file, we check it and send you a digital proof to approve.

If your product has no Custom size option

We're adding the Custom size option to more products over time, so check the product page of the product you want. If the option isn't there yet, email our bespoke team at bespoke@helloprint.com with the product, the size in mm and the quantity you need. The bespoke team looks at what we can do and emails you a quote.

For a product that isn't in our catalogue at all, see ordering a custom product.

If you need a different quantity

For a quantity that isn't on the product page, see ordering a quantity that isn't on the website.
