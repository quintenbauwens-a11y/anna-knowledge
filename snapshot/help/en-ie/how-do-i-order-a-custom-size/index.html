Ordering a custom size

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-do-i-order-a-custom-size

Category: Before ordering

Selected products — currently stickers and panels — accept custom dimensions: choose Custom size in the configurator, enter width and height in mm, and the price updates instantly.

Don't see it on the product you want? Look for the Custom size option in the configurator — we're adding it to more products over time.
