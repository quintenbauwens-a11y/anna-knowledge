Can I get a mock-up of my logo on a product before ordering?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/mock-up-of-my-logo-on-a-product

Category: Before ordering

Yes, for most gift products: the editor tool on the product page shows a mock-up of your design on the product before you order. For other products, your digital proof shows exactly what we'll print, and we only start production once you approve it.

See your logo on a gift product

  1. Open the product page and choose your options.
  2. Open the editor tool and upload your logo or design.
  3. Check the mock-up: position, size and colours of your logo on the product.
  4. Add the product to your cart when you're happy with it.

What happens next: after you order, you still get a digital proof to approve before we print. See how to upload and preview your file in the editor.

Check your design on the digital proof

For products without a mock-up, the digital proof is your preview: it shows your design exactly as we'll print it.

  1. Order the product with the options you want.
  2. Upload your logo on the product page, on the order confirmation page, or later from your order page in Orders.
  3. Check the position, size and colours of your logo on the digital proof.
  4. Approve your digital proof if everything looks right.

What happens next: production starts once you approve. Approve before the deadline to keep your chosen delivery date; if you approve after it, your delivery moves by one working day.

If the digital proof isn't what you expected

Upload a new file before you approve: go to Orders → Order details → Change submitted files. You get a new digital proof to approve. See how to change your file after uploading. If you decide not to go ahead, see whether you can cancel your order.

If you want to see the product itself before you order

In certain situations we can send a sample: request a quote and tell us which product you'd like to see. See also how to request a sample.

If you are planning a large or custom order

Request a quote and tell us what you want to print and where your logo should go. Our Quotes team replies with an offer.

Once you approve your digital proof, production starts and you can no longer change your file.
