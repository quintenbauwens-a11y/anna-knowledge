What is the difference between rich black and 100% black?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/what-is-the-difference-between-rich-black-and-100-black

Category: Design definitions

100% black is printed with black ink only, while rich black is a mix of black with other ink colours that prints a deeper, darker black. Use 100% black for text and rich black for large areas such as backgrounds.

Rich black

Rich black mixes several ink colours to print a deeper and darker black than black ink alone. Rich black works best for larger objects such as backgrounds and large shapes.

100% black

100% black uses black ink only. Text in 100% black prints sharp and easy to read, also at small font sizes.

Why rich black isn't recommended for text

Rich black text is printed with a mixture of different ink colours. Especially with smaller fonts, that mixture can make text less legible and blurry, so it's harder to read. See also printing small text.

Choose the right black in your design

  • Text, especially small text: 100% black.
  • Backgrounds and other large areas: rich black.
  • Set up your design in CMYK, so you control which inks make up your black. See the difference between RGB and CMYK.

Check the colour settings of your text and backgrounds in your design software before you save your file. The file requirements for paper products list everything else a print-ready file needs.
