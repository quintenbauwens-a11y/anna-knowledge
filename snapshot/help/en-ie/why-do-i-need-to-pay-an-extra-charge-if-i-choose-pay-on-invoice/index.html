Why do I need to pay an extra charge if I choose Pay on invoice?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/why-do-i-need-to-pay-an-extra-charge-if-i-choose-pay-on-invoice

Category: Payment

When you choose Pay on invoice, we add an extra charge of 2% of the order amount, with a minimum of €1.99. We charge it because invoicing takes more administrative work than an instant electronic payment.

How the extra charge is calculated

  • The extra charge is 2% of the order amount.
  • The minimum extra charge is €1.99.
  • For an order amount of €50, 2% is €1.00, so you pay the minimum extra charge of €1.99.
  • For an order amount of €200, the extra charge is €4.00.

Why we ask for an extra charge

With an instant electronic payment, your payment is completed when you order. With Pay on invoice, we send you an invoice and follow up the payment afterwards, which takes more administrative work.

If you'd rather not pay the extra charge

Choose another payment method at checkout. Which payment methods you can use lists the options.
