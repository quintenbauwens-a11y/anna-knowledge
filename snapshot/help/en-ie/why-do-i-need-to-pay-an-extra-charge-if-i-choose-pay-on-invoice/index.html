Why do I need to pay an extra charge if I choose 'Pay on Invoice'?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/why-do-i-need-to-pay-an-extra-charge-if-i-choose-pay-on-invoice

Category: Payment

For the payment option 'Pay on invoice', we are asking for an extra charge of 2% of the order amount (with a minimum charge of 1.99€), as invoicing requires more administrative work than instant electronic payments.
