Why have elements in my design moved after downloading it from Canva?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-come-elements-in-my-design-have-moved-after-downloading-it-from-canva

Category: Canva

This usually happens because browsers can export some Canva elements differently. Download your design again in another browser, such as Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Safari or Microsoft Edge.

Why elements move

You'll see this if text, images or shapes in your downloaded design sit in a different place or have a different size than in Canva. Different browsers can export some elements differently, so the same Canva design can come out slightly different.

Download your design again

  1. Open Canva in another browser: Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Safari or Microsoft Edge.
  2. Open your design and download it again.
  3. Compare the new download with your design in Canva and check that every element is in place.

Use the new download as your file. If you've already uploaded the old file, replace your file before your order goes into production. You then get a new digital proof to approve.

If you designed in Canva on our website

Until you pay, you can go back to your design: in your cart, click Edit your design to open the design editor, check the elements and save your design. Changing your Canva design after you've accepted it explains more.

What to check on your digital proof

Your digital proof is a preview of your design exactly as we will print it. Check that every element is in the right place before you approve. If something has moved, don't approve yet: replace your file or fix your design first.

Once your order is in production, your file can't be changed, so check every element on your digital proof before you approve.
