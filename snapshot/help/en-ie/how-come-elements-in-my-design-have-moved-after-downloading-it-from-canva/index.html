How come elements in my design have moved after downloading it from Canva?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-come-elements-in-my-design-have-moved-after-downloading-it-from-canva

Category: Canva

Different browsers can export some elements differently. If elements in your design appear to have moved or changed size during download, try to download your design again on a different browser. The best browsers for Canva are Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Safari and Microsoft Edge.
