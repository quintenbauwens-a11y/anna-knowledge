What is and what is not included in a Premium/Deluxe Artwork Check?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/what-is-and-what-is-not-included-in-a-premium-deluxe-artwork-check

Category: Artwork check & digital proof

If you select the Premium Artwork Check, this means that:

  • We will do basic resolution, size and colour checks.
  • Your files will be converted into a verified PDF.
  • You will receive a print proof (digital proof) before production.

If you select the Deluxe Artwork Check, it means that:

  • We will do everything that is included in the premium design check.
  • In addition, one of our experts will review your design.
  • Your files will be optimised using AI (if possible).

However, we're never able to check:

  • Spelling mistakes.
  • Overprint.
