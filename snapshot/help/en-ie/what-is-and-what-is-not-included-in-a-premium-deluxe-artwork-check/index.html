What is included in the Basic, Premium and Deluxe design check?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/what-is-and-what-is-not-included-in-a-premium-deluxe-artwork-check

Category: Artwork check & digital proof

Every order includes the free Basic design check, which checks your file and creates a digital proof for you to approve. The Premium and Deluxe design checks are paid options with extra checks; no design check covers spelling or overprint.

Basic design check

  • The Basic design check is free with every order.
  • The Basic design check checks your file for print and creates your digital proof.
  • If your file passes the Basic design check, you see your digital proof before you pay: see seeing a proof before paying.

Premium design check

  • The Premium design check checks the resolution, size and colours of your file.
  • The Premium design check converts your files into a verified PDF.
  • The Premium design check includes a digital proof before production.

Deluxe design check

  • The Deluxe design check includes everything in the Premium design check.
  • In the Deluxe design check, one of our experts also reviews your design.
  • In the Deluxe design check, we optimise your files with AI where possible.

Choose your design check

You choose the Premium or Deluxe design check in your cart, where you also see its price. If you don't choose one, your order gets the Basic design check.

What no design check covers

  • Spelling mistakes in your text.
  • Overprint settings in your file.

Check these yourself on your digital proof before you approve it.

What happens next

After you upload your file, we check it and create your digital proof. See how long a design check takes. Approve your digital proof to send your order to production. If you approve after the deadline, your delivery date moves by one working day.

Spelling mistakes are printed exactly as they are in your file, because no design check includes a spelling check.
