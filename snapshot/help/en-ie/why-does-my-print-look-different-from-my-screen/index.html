Why do printed colours look different from my screen?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/why-does-my-print-look-different-from-my-screen

Category: Creating your design

Your screen shows colours with light (RGB), while we print with four inks (CMYK), so some bright screen colours print slightly more muted. Your digital proof shows your design in print colours, so judge the colours there before you approve.

Why screen and print colours differ

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. A printing press mixes cyan, magenta, yellow and black ink, which reproduces colour consistently on paper, the same way on every press. Some bright screen colours, such as vivid greens and electric blues, can't be mixed in ink, so they print a little darker and more muted. This is how ink works, not a printing error. Read more about the difference between RGB and CMYK.

What happens to an RGB file

If your file is in RGB, we convert it to CMYK automatically using the FOGRA standard printing profile. Your digital proof then mentions the conversion in its notes, so you can see the result before you approve. Read what the notes on your digital proof mean.

Get printed colours closer to your design

  • Set up your design in CMYK from the start, so you choose colours that ink can print.
  • Avoid relying on very bright greens, blues or oranges for important brand colours.
  • Check the colours on your digital proof before you approve it.

If the colours on your digital proof aren't what you want

Adjust your design in CMYK and replace your file before your order goes into production. We check the new file and send you a new digital proof to approve.

If your printed order looks different from your digital proof

File a complaint within 10 days after delivery, with photos of your products. Our Customer Service team looks at the possibilities with you.

Once your order is in production, the colours in your file can no longer be changed.
