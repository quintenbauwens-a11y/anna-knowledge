Can I print white or light colours on transparent products?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/print-white-on-transparent-products

Category: Design dos and don'ts

It differs per product: the product page shows what is possible for white or light colours on that transparent product. Without a special option, white isn't printed and light colours are barely visible on transparent material.

Check the product page

Open the product page of the transparent product you want and look at the printing options. Some products offer an option for printing white or solid colours, for example a white layer under your design or PMS (Pantone) colours. Such an option isn't standard, so you have to select it yourself. If the product page doesn't offer one, white and light colours can't be printed clearly on that product.

Why white and light colours disappear

  • White: in standard full-colour printing, white is the colour of the material itself, so nothing is printed there. On a transparent product, white parts of your design come out clear.
  • Light and pastel colours: ink is printed, but so lightly that the colour is barely visible on transparent material.
  • Strong colours: transparent material lets light through, so even strong colours can look lighter than on your screen.

If the product you want has no option for white

Choose darker colours for your design, or request a quote and we look at what's possible.

What to check on your digital proof

Look at every white or light area of your design on your digital proof. A new file means a new digital proof.

If you don't select an option for white when you order, the white parts of your design won't be printed.
