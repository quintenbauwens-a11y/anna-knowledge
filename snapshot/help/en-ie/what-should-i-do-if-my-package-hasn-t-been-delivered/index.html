What should I do if my package hasn't been delivered?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/what-should-i-do-if-my-package-hasn-t-been-delivered

Category: After delivery

Check the track and trace link for the latest delivery status first: carriers are sometimes delayed, and your order is then normally delivered on the next working day.

Check where your parcel is

  1. Open the track and trace email we sent you when your order shipped, or open your order in Orders.
  2. Open the track and trace link for your parcel.
  3. Check the latest status and delivery date from the carrier.

If your order was shipped in more than one parcel, each parcel has its own link. See how to track your order.

If the carrier is delayed

A delivery driver can sometimes be delayed. Your order is then normally delivered on the next working day. You don't need to do anything yet: keep an eye on the track and trace link.

If your order is more than two working days late

We look at the delivery with you and at what we can do.

If the delivery attempt failed

The carrier may have tried to deliver when nobody was available. See what happens after an unsuccessful delivery attempt, including what to do if your parcel comes back to us.

If the track and trace link doesn't work

The link usually only works once the carrier has scanned your parcel. See track and trace code not working.
