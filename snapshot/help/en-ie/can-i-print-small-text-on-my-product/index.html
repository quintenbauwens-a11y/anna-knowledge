Can I print small text on my product?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/can-i-print-small-text-on-my-product

Category: Design dos and don'ts

Thin lines and small text can be challenging to print clearly, especially if they are too close together or not bold enough. Therefore, it's important to check that these elements have the minimum size required for the given print technique and material so they will be legible in the final product.

On every product page, within the section 'Design Guidelines' we mention the minimum font size or line thickness in order for you to design it in a correct way.
