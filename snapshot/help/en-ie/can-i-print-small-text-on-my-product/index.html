Can I print small text on my product?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/can-i-print-small-text-on-my-product

Category: Design dos and don'ts

Yes, as long as your text is at least the minimum font size for your product. You find the minimum font size and line thickness for each product under Design Guidelines on its product page.

Find the minimum size for your product

  1. Open the product page of the product you want to order.
  2. Go to the Design Guidelines section.
  3. Note the minimum font size and line thickness, and use at least that size in your design.

The minimum size depends on the print technique and the material, so it can differ between products.

What makes small text hard to read

  • Letters that are smaller than the minimum font size.
  • Letters or lines that are too thin, or not bold enough.
  • Letters or lines that sit too close together.

For lines in your design, see whether you can print thin lines.

What happens next

Once you upload your file, you get a digital proof. Zoom in on your smallest text and check that it is clear before you approve.

If your text is smaller than the minimum

Make the text larger or bolder in your design, or put more space between the letters. If you have already uploaded your file, you can replace your file until your order goes into production. A new file means a new digital proof to approve.

Text below the minimum font size may not be legible on your printed product.
