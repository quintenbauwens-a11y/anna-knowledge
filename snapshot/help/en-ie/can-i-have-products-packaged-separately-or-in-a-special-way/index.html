Can I have products packaged separately or in a special way?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/can-i-have-products-packaged-separately-or-in-a-special-way

Category: Before ordering

Only on a quote basis: packaging your products separately or in a special way isn't a standard option when you order, so request a quote before you order.

Request a quote for special packaging

  1. Open our quote form, or email our bespoke team at bespoke@helloprint.com.
  2. Describe the products and quantities you want to order.
  3. Explain how you want them packaged, for example each product or each set packed separately.
  4. Send your request.

What happens next

Our bespoke team looks at whether the packaging you want is possible for your order and whether extra costs apply, and sends you a quote. How to request a quote explains the process.
