How can I change the email address where I receive my invoices?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-can-i-change-the-email-address-where-i-receive-my-invoices

Category: Invoice

You change the email address for your invoices in My Account, under Personal details, in the Company info card. Enter the address in the Invoice email field and save it.

Change your invoice email

  1. Go to Personal details in My Account.
  2. Click Edit in the Company info card.
  3. Enter the email address in the Invoice email field.
  4. Click Save.

We then send your invoices to this email address. You can still download all your invoices at any time from Invoices in My Account.

If you need an invoice that went to your old email address

Download it from Invoices in My Account. See how to download your invoice or credit note.

If you want to change the email address you log in with

That is a different setting: change it in the Email card on the same Personal details page. We send a confirmation link to the new email address.

If your company name or VAT number is wrong

You update those in the same Company info card. See updating your company and VAT details.
