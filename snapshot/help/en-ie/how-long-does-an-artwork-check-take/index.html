How long does a design check take?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-long-does-an-artwork-check-take

Category: Artwork check & digital proof

The Basic design check takes about 5 minutes. If your file needs a manual check, it always takes about 60 minutes and starts once we have received your payment; after either check you get your digital proof to approve.

Basic design check

Every order includes the free Basic design check. If your file passes it, your digital proof is ready in about 5 minutes, before you pay. Check it and approve it to send your order to production.

Manual check

A manual check always takes about 60 minutes. It starts only once we have received your payment. Want to know what our team checks in the Premium and Deluxe design check? See what each design check includes.

If your digital proof shows a warning, see what the warning on your digital proof means.

Make your delivery date

Upload your file early enough to receive and approve your digital proof before the deadline. The deadline is at the bottom of the product page next to each delivery option, and on your order page after ordering. Getting your order into production on time explains the steps.

If your digital proof hasn't arrived

Open Orders and check whether your order page asks you for a new file or for payment. If it doesn't and more than 60 minutes have passed, contact our Customer Service team with your order number and we look at it with you.

If you approve your digital proof after the deadline, your delivery moves by one working day.
