Can I change my delivery address?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/can-i-change-my-delivery-address

Category: Changes in delivery

In most cases yes, as long as your order hasn't gone into production: change the address yourself on your order page. The new address must be in the same country as the address on your order.

Change the address on an order you've placed

  1. Go to Orders in My Account and open the order.
  2. Click Change delivery address.
  3. Pick a saved address from your address book, or enter a new address including the Eircode.
  4. Save your changes.

Your order page then shows the new delivery address, and your order is delivered there. If you enter a new address, you can also choose to save it for future orders.

If you don't see Change delivery address

Your order has gone into production, so the address can no longer be changed on your order page.

If the new address is in another country

The delivery address of an order always stays in the country you ordered for. To send products to another country, place a new order: see delivery to another country.

Change your default address for future orders

You choose the delivery address for each order at checkout. To change your default delivery address, see managing your saved addresses.

Once your order is in production, you can no longer change the delivery address yourself.
