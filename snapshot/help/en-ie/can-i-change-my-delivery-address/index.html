Can I change my delivery address?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/can-i-change-my-delivery-address

Category: Changes in delivery

Manage and update your delivery addresses for future orders through your account's 'My Addresses' page. Access this page by hovering over your name in the top right corner of your screen and selecting 'My Addresses.' On this page, you have three options for making changes:

1. Edit the default billing and delivery address:

  • Click 'Edit Address' in the box labeled 'Standard Billing Address & Default Delivery Address.'
  • Make any necessary changes.
  • Click 'Edit Address' to save your updates.

2. Add a new address to your address book:

  • Click the green 'Add Address' button.
  • Fill out the new address details.
  • Click 'Save Address' to add it to your address book.

3. Edit a saved address in your address book:

  • If you have multiple addresses in your address book, they will appear under 'My Saved Addresses' on the 'My Addresses' page, each with its own row. On the right side of each row, you will find three dots. Click on these dots.
  • Select 'Edit Address.'
  • Make the necessary changes.
  • Click 'Edit Address' to save your updates.

Need to update the address for an existing order? Please refer to this article for more information!
