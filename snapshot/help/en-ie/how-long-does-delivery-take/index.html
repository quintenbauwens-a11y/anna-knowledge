How long does delivery take?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/how-long-does-delivery-take

Category: Delivery time

Your print delivery time is the date shown next to each delivery option on the product page, and that date already includes production and transport. It holds if you upload your file and approve your digital proof before the deadline shown next to that option.

What the delivery time includes

  • Production time: the time we need to print your order after you approve your digital proof.
  • Transport: the time the carrier needs to bring your order to your address.
  • The delivery date on the product page adds both together, so you don't need to add anything yourself.

Find the delivery date for your product

  1. Open the product page and choose your options, such as size, quantity and finish.
  2. Look at the delivery options at the bottom of the page. Each option shows a delivery date, a price and the deadline to upload your file and approve your digital proof.
  3. Choose the option with the date you need. That date appears in your cart and on your order page after you order.

What changes the delivery time

  • Your product options: quantity, paper and finishes can affect how long production takes.
  • Your approval: approving your digital proof after the deadline moves delivery by one working day. See what happens if you miss your proof approval deadline.
  • Your delivery option: a faster option means a faster production time (turnaround), which costs more because of production costs.

If you have already ordered

Your delivery date is on your order page. Once your order has shipped, track your order with the carrier's link. If the date has moved, read why did my delivery date change?
