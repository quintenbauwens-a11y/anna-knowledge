Do I need to embed the images in my design?

Help centre article on Helloprint Ireland: https://www.helloprint.com/en-ie/help-centre/do-i-need-to-embed-the-images-in-my-design

Category: Design dos and don'ts

Yes. Embedding puts all the information about an image inside your file, so the image prints correctly; an image that is only linked to another file on your computer can be missing when we print your design.

Embedded and linked images

  • Embedded: the image is saved inside your file. It travels with your file wherever you upload it.
  • Linked: your file only points to an image stored somewhere else on your computer. Without that image, your file can't show it.

Embed your images before you upload

  1. Place or embed every image in your design instead of linking to it. Most design programs show which images are linked.
  2. Export your design as a PDF. See which file type to use for printing.
  3. Open the PDF on your computer and check that every image is there.

Also check the other file requirements for paper products, such as resolution and outlined text.

What happens after you upload

We check your file and send you a digital proof. Check that every image is in the right place before you approve your digital proof.

If an image is missing on your digital proof

Embed the image, export your file again and replace the file you uploaded. You can replace your file until your order goes into production, and a new file means a new digital proof. See how to change your file after uploading.

Once your order is in production, your file can no longer be changed, so check every image on your digital proof before you approve it.
