What techniques are used to print bodywarmers jackets?

Embroidery: This method involves sewing thread directly onto a garment to create your designs or logos. Embroidery is the most durable customization technique; it highlights your design, adds texture, and provides a high-quality appearance. The embroidered motif can include up to 6 colors.

Screen print: This is a popular method for clothing and textiles where ink is pushed through a mesh screen onto the fabric, creating vibrant and long-lasting prints. It's cost-effective available from a quantity of 100 pieces and allows for up to 4 colours.
