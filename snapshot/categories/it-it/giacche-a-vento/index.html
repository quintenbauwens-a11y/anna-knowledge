Windbreaker Jackets printing

Windbreaker jackets seamlessly blend style and functionality. Lightweight and wind-resistant, they offer comfort for various activities. Ideal for walks, hikes, or camping, their breathability ensures mobility. These jackets shield against wind and light rain, making them perfect for unpredictable weather. Their unified appearance makes them an excellent canvas for team branding or promotional merchandise. By customising windbreaker jackets with your logo or design, you add a personal touch that enhances your brand's image.

What techniques are used to print windbreaker jackets?

Embroidery: This method involves sewing thread directly onto a garment to create your designs or logos. Embroidery is the most durable customization technique; it highlights your design, adds texture, and provides a high-quality appearance. The embroidered motif can include up to 6 colours.

Screen print: This is a popular method for clothing and textiles where ink is pushed through a mesh screen onto the fabric, creating vibrant and long-lasting prints. It's cost-effective available from a quantity of 100 pieces and allows for up to 4 colours.
