FAQs about Event Programs

What types of event programs can I print?

You can print all kinds of event programs to match your occasion: wedding programs, funeral and memorial order-of-service booklets, church and worship bulletins, concert and recital programs, and graduation or ceremony programs. We support a range of sizes, binding types and finishes to suit your event.

What bindings are available for event programs?

Not sure which binding suits your program? Compare the four options and pick the one that fits your event: - Saddle-Stitched Booklets — stapled spine; the most economical and popular choice for wedding and funeral programs up to ~64 pages. - Perfect Bound Booklets — glued flat spine for a premium, book-like keepsake; best for thicker programs. - Wire-O Booklets — twin-loop wire binding that lies flat; great for order-of-service booklets held open during a ceremony. - Spiral Bound Booklets — coil binding that lies completely flat and rotates 360°; ideal for songbooks and hands-on programs.

What is the best binding for event programs printing?

For most event programs under ~64 pages, saddle-stitch binding is the popular, economical choice. Wire-o or spiral (coil) binding lets an order-of-service booklet lie flat during a ceremony, and perfect binding suits thicker keepsake programs.

What paper is best for event programs?

Uncoated is easy to read and write on, making it a popular choice for order-of-service and ceremony programs. Glossy or matte paper works well for programs with photos and full-color graphics, such as wedding or concert programs.

Can I print event programs in color or black and white?

Yes. We offer full-color event programs printing for detailed graphics and branding, or black-and-white options for cost-effective, text-heavy manuals.
