Catalog Printing FAQs

What are catalogs used for?

Catalogs are printed booklets used to showcase products, services, or detailed business information. They're ideal for retail, wholesale, real estate, hospitality, and corporate marketing.

What sizes and binding options are available?

We offer standard sizes like 5.5" x 8.5", 8.5" x 11", and more. You can choose from saddle stitch, perfect bound, or wire-o binding depending on your page count and finish preference.

Do you offer cheap catalog printing for bulk orders?

Yes, our pricing is designed for both small and large runs. The more you print, the lower your cost per unit, making it easy to stay within budget.

Can I print custom catalogs with my own design?

Absolutely. Upload your own print-ready file or use our templates to create a custom catalog that reflects your brand’s style and layout preferences.

Is catalog printing available near me?

We offer online ordering with shipping across the U.S., so even if we’re not local, you’ll get fast and reliable catalog printing near you with nationwide delivery.

What’s the minimum and maximum page count for catalogs?

Most catalogs start at 8 pages and can go up to 96 or more, depending on the binding type. Perfect bound catalogs are better for higher page counts.
