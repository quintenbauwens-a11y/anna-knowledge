Custom USB FAQs

Is a flash drive the same as a USB drive?

Yes, flash drive, USB drive, USB stick and thumb drive all describe the same portable storage that plugs into a USB port. Custom USB flash drives and custom flash drives are therefore the same product, just with a different name.

Should I pick full color print or laser engraving?

Choose custom printed USB drives in full color when your logo has several colors, gradients or photo elements and needs to pop. Go for laser engraving when you want a clean, tone-on-tone mark on aluminum that stays put for the life of the drive.

What capacity should I choose for promotional USB drives?

Choose 2 GB for documents, brochures and slide decks, 4 GB when you add image-rich files, and 16 GB for video or large catalogs. Matching the capacity to what you share keeps your custom USB budget working and your custom flash drives useful.

What is the minimum order for custom USB drives?

Custom USB drives on this page start from 30 pieces per design. That works for a single team as well as a larger event run of promotional USB flash drives, and the exact minimum for each model is shown in its price table.

Can I order several logos in one batch?

Yes, you can order custom flash drives with logos for different brands, departments or events, as long as each design meets the minimum of 30 pieces. Every version is printed or engraved as its own run, so each one looks right.

All-aluminum or aluminum and ABS: which flash drive suits my brand?

The all-aluminum two-tone drive gives branded USB flash drives a sleek, solid feel that fits tech, finance and professional services. The aluminum and ABS plastic Rotate drives come in several colors, so you can match promotional USB drives to your brand palette at trade shows.

Are personalized flash drives a good gift for clients?

Yes, personalized flash drives make a useful thank-you because clients keep using them long after the meeting. Custom printed USB drives in full color or with a crisp engraving keep your logo in sight every time they are plugged in.

What is a swivel flash drive?

A swivel flash drive has a cover that rotates around the body to protect the USB connector when it is not in use. All Rotate models here use this design, so these custom USB sticks have no separate cap to lose at a busy event.

Why are custom USB sticks popular at trade shows?

Custom USB sticks give visitors something useful to take home along with your catalog, price list or presentation. Compared with paper handouts, a branded USB flash drive tends to stay in a laptop bag and keeps your name close at hand.
