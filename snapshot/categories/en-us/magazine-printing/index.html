What is the size of a standard magazine?

The standard size for a magazine is typically 8.5 x 11 inches. This size is commonly used for general publications, including lifestyle, fashion, and business magazines. However, some magazines may use different sizes, such as Digest size (5.5 x 8.5 inches), used for smaller, more compact magazines like some niche or specialized publications.

The size choice often depends on the type of content and the target audience.

What is the best binding option for magazines?

Saddle stitching is great for magazines with fewer pages (typically under 92), while perfect binding is ideal for thicker magazines with a more premium, book-like finish. We offer both options to match your needs. For Perfect Bound Magazines, explore our selection here.

How many pages can my magazine have?

Your magazine must have at least 8 pages, and the total must be a multiple of 4. The maximum number of pages depends on the binding type and paper weight you choose. With saddle stitching, you can have up to 92 pages. For more pages, explore our selection of perfect bound booklets.

Is the number of pages the same as the number of sheets?

No, pages and sheets are not the same when it comes to saddle stitched booklets. Each sheet of paper is printed on both sides and then folded in half, which creates 4 pages per sheet (2 on the front and 2 on the back).

Example: If you order a 5.5 x 8.5" booklet with 8 pages, we print your design on two large 8.5 x 11" sheets. After folding and stapling, those two sheets become 8 pages in total.

So remember: the number of printed pages in your booklet will always be four times the number of folded sheets. This is why your total page count must be a multiple of 4!
