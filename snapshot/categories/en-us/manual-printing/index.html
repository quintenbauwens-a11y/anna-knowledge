FAQs about Manuals

What types of manuals can I print?

You can print all sorts of manuals based on your needs, from product manuals, training manuals, instruction booklets, to reference guides. We support a variety of sizes, binding types, and finishes to suit your needs.

What is the best binding for manual printing?

For manuals under 64 pages, saddle-stitch binding works well. For manuals that need to lay flat, wire-o binding is ideal. Perfect bound manuals are best for thicker training or product guides.

Can I print manuals in color or black and white?

Yes. We offer full-color manual printing for detailed graphics and branding, or black-and-white options for cost-effective, text-heavy manuals.

What paper is best for product manuals?

Uncoated or matte paper is often used for instruction manuals because it’s easy to read and can be written on. Glossy paper works well for manuals with images and diagrams.
