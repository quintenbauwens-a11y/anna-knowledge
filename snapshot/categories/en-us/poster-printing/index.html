Poster Printing FAQs

What is the best size for poster printing?

The best size depends on your purpose. Popular formats include 11 x 17 for handouts or indoor notices, 18 x 24 for promotions and signage, and 24 x 36 for maximum visibility in storefronts or events. We also offer custom sizes for more tailored displays.

How do I print a custom poster online?

Simply upload your design, select your preferred size, paper type, and finish, then place your order through our easy-to-use online poster printing platform. You can preview your file and choose quantities from a single print to bulk orders.

Can I print posters in full color?

Yes, all posters are printed in vibrant full color using high-resolution digital or offset printing. Whether you're printing photography, illustrations, or promotional graphics, your design will come out crisp and eye-catching.

What finishes are available for poster printing?

Choose from gloss, matte, or uncoated finishes. Gloss is great for vibrant, photo-heavy designs. Matte offers a glare-free, professional look. Uncoated is best for posters that need to be written on or used in high-touch environments.

What materials are best for printing posters?

We offer a range of poster paper types including standard glossy paper or photo quality gloss, premium matte, and waterproof options. The material you choose should depend on where and how long your poster will be displayed. You can also pick uncoated paper stock to write down information directly on your posters.

Do you offer waterproof or outdoor poster printing?

Yes, we offer waterproof and weatherproof posters printed on durable synthetic materials like EndurACE. These are ideal for outdoor advertising, construction sites, or areas exposed to moisture.

How fast is the turnaround time for poster printing services?

Depending on the options you choose, delivery can vary from next day to several business days. Standard production usually takes 2–4 business days. Rush printing options are available if you need faster delivery, and shipping timelines are displayed on the product page.

Can I order just one poster, or do I need to buy in bulk?

Depending on the options you choose, you can print as few or as many posters as you need. We offer flexible quantities so you can order a single custom poster or hundreds for a larger campaign.

Most posters are printed single-sided, but double-sided printing is available on select materials. Reach out to our support team if you need a specific double-sided setup.

What file format and resolution should I use for poster printing?

For the best print results, upload your artwork as a PDF or high-resolution JPG or PNG. Aim for at least 150 DPI at full print size, with 300 DPI recommended for smaller formats like 11x17. CMYK color mode gives the most accurate color reproduction. HelloPrint accepts all common file formats and provides artwork templates and guidelines to help you get print-ready files right the first time.

What is the difference between matte and glossy poster printing?

Glossy poster printing has a shiny, high-contrast finish that makes colors vibrant and photos pop, making it the popular choice for event promotions, retail displays, and eye-catching visuals. Matte poster printing has a flat, non-reflective surface that reduces glare, which works better for text-heavy designs, fine art reproductions, and environments with strong ambient light. Both paper types are available at HelloPrint across all standard poster sizes.
