Which Custom Notepads Fit Your Office, Events and Clients?

Think first about where the pad will sit. For meeting notes on an office desk, the 8.5 x 11 in pad gives the most writing space. At a front desk, the 4.25 x 5.5 in pad takes phone messages. In a warehouse, the narrow 3.5 x 8.5 in pad holds packing lists. At trade shows, the 4 x 6 in pad works as a handout.

Paper matters most for pads that leave the building. For client gifts, the 70lb linen text paper has a woven texture. For staff pads, the 60lb or 70lb uncoated text paper is a plain choice.

Sheet count follows the use. Hand out pads of 25 sheets at events, and keep pads of 50 sheets on desks.

All five sizes are standard notepads, with your design in full color.
