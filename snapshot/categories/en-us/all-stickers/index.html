What’s the difference between die cut and kiss cut stickers?

  • Die cut stickers are precisely cut to the shape of your design.
  • Kiss cut stickers are cut through the sticker layer but not the backing paper, making them easier to peel.

Both options are popular for custom printed stickers and branding.
