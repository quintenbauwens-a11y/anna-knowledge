What is the difference between Vinyl banners and Mesh banners?

Vinyl banners are made from durable plastic that is strong, water-resistant, and durable. This printed banners are designed to stand out at any indoor or outdoor event. Mesh banners, on the other hand, have a perforated design that allows wind to pass through, preventing tearing. They are ideal for outdoor advertising and construction sites where wind resistance is needed. These personalized banners are the perfect outdoor signage solution for any weather condition.

Visually, Mesh banners have a semi-transparent look due to their woven design, making them great for large fonts and images. Vinyl banners provide a more vibrant and solid finish, perfect for detailed graphics and indoor use.
