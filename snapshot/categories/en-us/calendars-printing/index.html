Frequently Asked Questions About Custom Calendars

How do I order personalised calendars online?

Choose your calendar format and options, such as size and paper, then upload your print-ready design. On wall, desk and folded calendars, include the months and dates in your file. We check your file for free and send a digital proof before printing.

Can I make a photo calendar with my own pictures?

Yes. On a wall or desk calendar you can place a different photo on each page, such as your products, projects or team, and add your logo. Months and dates aren't preset on these, so include them in your design along with the photos.

Which types of calendars can I choose from?

You can choose wall calendars in portrait or landscape, desk calendars and folded calendars, all from 1 piece. For planning ahead there are 3-month calendars in memo, desk and classic versions, plus a 4-month calendar with a printable header strip.

What's the difference between a wall and a desk calendar?

A wall calendar hangs in an office, workshop or reception where the whole team sees it. A desk calendar has 13 pages and stands next to the keyboard, which makes it a handy gift for clients. Many businesses order both.

Can I print my company logo on a calendar?

Yes. On wall, desk and folded calendars your design covers every page, so you can add your logo, brand colours, contact details and your own dates, like trade fairs. On the memo and classic 3-month calendars and the 4-month calendar, your logo goes on the header strip.

What is the minimum order for calendars?

Wall, desk and folded calendars can be ordered from 1 piece, so a single sample or a small batch is possible. The 4-month calendar starts at 50 pieces. Each product shows its own minimum and price per piece before you order.

Can I add our own company dates to the calendar?

Yes, on wall, desk and folded calendars. Because you design every page yourself, you can mark trade fairs, launches, company holidays or birthdays wherever you like, together with the months and dates.

Are the months and dates already printed on the calendar?

It depends on the calendar. Wall, desk and folded calendars have no preset months or dates, so include them in your artwork. The memo and classic 3-month calendars and the 4-month calendar come with the calendar grid printed; you design the header strip. Each product page explains the details.

Why are branded calendars a good business gift?

A calendar gets looked at every day for a whole year, so your logo stays in sight far longer than a flyer or email. It's useful rather than throwaway, which makes it a welcome end-of-year thank-you for clients and staff.

Do I need to design the calendar myself?

Yes, you supply a print-ready file. On wall, desk and folded calendars that includes the months and dates; each product page has design guidelines. We always do a free design check and send a digital proof, and our team is available via chat if you get stuck.
