FAQs About Custom Drawstring Bags

What are drawstring bags with a logo best used for?

They work well as trade show giveaways, race packets, orientation kits, team bags and onboarding gifts. People keep using them for the gym and errands, so your logo stays visible long after the event is over.

Which drawstring bag material should I choose?

Choose polyester or polycanvas for team gear, air mesh for gym and swim kit, and non-woven or polypropylene for large giveaways. Recycled and RPET styles suit brands that prefer recycled materials.

Can I order drawstring bags in bulk?

Yes. Printed drawstring bags are made for volume, from booth giveaways to company-wide events. Choose your style, add your logo and set the quantity you need on the product page to see the price for that run.

Do you offer recycled drawstring bags?

Yes. The range includes recycled and RPET drawstring bags, a recycled polyester model and recycled cotton options. Each product page shows exactly which recycled material is used for that style.

What is the difference between a drawstring bag and a drawstring backpack?

The terms are used for the same product: a light sack closed with cords that double as shoulder straps. Some styles, like the polycanvas sportspack, use a sturdier fabric for heavier gear.

Are personalized drawstring bags good for sports teams?

Yes. A drawstring backpack in team colors holds shoes, a water bottle and a towel, and is easy to carry to practice. Teams, leagues and camps often order them in large numbers as part of their season kit.

Do you have insulated drawstring bags?

Yes. The range includes insulated drawstring bags that keep snacks and drinks cool, which makes them a good fit for picnics, outdoor events and company outings where a practical giveaway is welcome.

How do you carry a personalized drawstring bag?

The cords close the bag and double as shoulder straps, so you can carry it on your back like a light backpack. That keeps your hands free at events, on campus or on the way to the gym.

Are promotional drawstring bags a good trade show giveaway?

Yes. Visitors use drawstring bags with your logo right away to carry brochures and samples around the floor, which keeps your logo moving through the show. After the event, the bag goes to the gym or on errands with your brand.
