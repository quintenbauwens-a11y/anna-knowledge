Give Your Folded Brochures The Perfect Finishing Touch

  • Glossy – A glossy finish is perfect for when you have a lot of images in your artwork and want to add an extra shine to your design.
  • Matte – Matte is the best finish for artwork featuring both text and images, as it maintains readability without excessive shine.
  • Dull – A smooth, low-sheen finish that reduces glare for a clean, professional look—ideal for designs with both text and images, without the shine of gloss or the softness of matte.
  • Writable – Opt for writable if you want to be able to write on your folded brochure. Remember that images will be less vibrant on this paper.
