FAQs about Custom Cards

What are custom cards and invitations used for?

Custom cards and invitations are perfect for any and every occasion. Make your friends and family feel special with custom printed cards or make your brand stick in the minds of your customers with uniquely designed invitations.

What paper options are available for card printing?

Our cards are printed on thick, high-quality paper stock with uncoated, matte, or gloss finishes. All options are durable, professional, and suitable for mailing or display.

What if the size I want is not available?

The standard size for card printing is 4 in x 6 in cards. We offer multiple sizes in portrait, landscape or square designs. If you'd like a different size to what we offer, please get in touch with our quotes team.

Can I add punch holes or tear marks to my gift vouchers?

Our gift vouchers don't standardly have the option to include punch holes or tear marks. Please contact us via our quotes team and enquire if this is something we can arrange for you.

Can my custom loyalty cards and other cards be printed on both sides?

Yes! Most of our cards and personalized vouchers can be printed double-sided. Please check the product page for further information.

What types of cards can I print?

We offer printing for folded cards, flat cards, thank-you cards, invitations, and promotional handouts. You can fully customize the design, size, and finish to suit your needs.

Do you offer card printing near me?

While we operate online, our delivery service covers the entire U.S., making it easy to get professional card printing near you, right to your door.

Can I print cards in small or large quantities?

Yes, whether you need a small batch or a high-volume run, our card printing services are built to scale with your needs and budget.
