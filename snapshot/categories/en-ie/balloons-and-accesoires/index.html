Choosing the Right Printed Balloon


Go for printed latex balloons when you need plenty of them. They are 30.5 cm and round, in nine colours, and orders start at 25. Metallic balloons are 33 cm latex with a pearlised sheen, with gold and silver among the nine colours. For an engagement or a wedding, heart-shaped balloons come in red, pink and white from just 10. A single big balloon of 60 or 90 cm marks a stand, and you can order one on its own. If that big balloon should be metallic, look at giant metallic balloons. For children, choose punch balloons, which are 45 cm and have an elastic band.


Printed Balloons for Weddings, Staff Dos and Anniversaries


Venues and planners preparing a wedding reception can have heart-shaped or round latex balloons printed with both first names. For a company anniversary, pick metallic balloons in gold, a warm colour with a pearlised sheen. For a staff do, tie round latex balloons to the tables with 110 cm ribbons.


Printing Balloons With Your Company Logo


Start from the design you already have, such as your shop name or a club crest. Every balloon is printed in one colour, so plan a one-colour version of a logo that has several. On white latex every print colour shows up, while black, dark blue, red and green balloons need a white or light print. The round print area is 16 cm on latex and metallic balloons, 15 cm on a heart and 20 cm on a punch balloon.

Frequently asked questions

What fits in the print on each balloon type?

A heart has room for a logo or a few short words in its centre. A big balloon takes an icon with one or two lines of text, such as your shop name above and the event below. On a punch balloon, a logo with a short line under it fits well.

How few custom balloons can I order?

One, if it is a big balloon. Heart-shaped balloons start at 10, and round latex and metallic balloons at 25 per colour. Punch balloons start at 25, or at 100 in white and green.
