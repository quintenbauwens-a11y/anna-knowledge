Frequently Asked Questions for Personalised Mepal Products

What Mepal products can I personalise?

The range covers lunch boxes, bento boxes, insulated lunch pots, snack pots, the EasyClip food storage box, the Cirqula multi bowl, the Ellipse cutlery set and foldable spoon, water bottles, sport bottles and mugs. Pick a model and choose its colour and print option on the product page.

Do personalised Mepal products suit sports clubs?

Yes. Sport bottles like the Pull in stainless steel, the Twist insulated sport bottle and the Shaker are made for training, and a lunch box or snack pot is handy on match days. Your crest or logo stays visible on the sideline all season.

Which Mepal lunch box is best for hot meals?

The Ellipse insulated lunch pot in stainless steel. It has a 500 ml and a 200 ml container, each with its own lid, so ingredients stay separate. For cold lunches, the Take a Break lunch box midi or the large bento lunch box are good picks.

Is there a sustainable Mepal water bottle?

Yes, the Mepal Vita bottles in recycled stainless steel. The plastic Vita water bottle is also a good choice: it is BPA-free, leak-proof and dishwasher safe, so it can be used for years.

How many colours can my logo have?

It depends on the model. The Mepal Vita 500 ml water bottle, for instance, can be printed in 1 to 4 colours or in full colour, so multi-colour crests work too. Each product page lists the print options.

Can I put my logo on Mepal cutlery?

Yes. The Ellipse cutlery set, with knife, fork and spoon in a compact holder, and the Ellipse foldable spoon can both carry your logo. They are handy for lunch at the desk or a day out with the team.
