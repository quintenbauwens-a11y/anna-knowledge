When do we consider a project 'special'?

A project is 'special' when standard solutions are not sufficient. For example:

✔︎ Unusual formats, materials, or volumes

✔︎ Combination of multiple printing techniques

✔︎ Finishing such as foil stamping, embossing, varnishes, or die-cutting

✔︎ Multiple products within one project

✔︎ International production and distribution

✔︎ Sustainable or certified productions

Frequently Asked Questions for Special Print Projects

What do you define as a special project?

A special project is any print assignment where standard solutions fall short. This typically includes unusual formats, materials, or volumes, combinations of multiple printing techniques, complex finishing. If your project requires customization, technical expertise, or tight coordination across multiple parties, it's likely a good fit for Special Projects.

Is there a minimum volume?

There is no fixed minimum volume, as every special project is assessed individually. That said, this service is best suited to organizations running complex or large-scale productions where a tailored approach genuinely adds value. Get in touch and we'll let you know whether your project is a good fit.

Can you coordinate international projects?

Yes. HelloPrint Special Projects is active in 13 countries and supported by a European network of specialized printing partners. We regularly manage productions that span multiple countries, including international roll-outs for retail and franchise organizations. Your dedicated project team handles coordination across all locations from a single point of contact.

Do you work with clients' existing suppliers?

This depends on the project. Our starting point is our own European network of vetted, specialized printing partners, which allows us to guarantee quality, speed, and transparency. If you have existing supplier relationships, we're happy to discuss how these might fit within your project setup during the initial briefing.

How does pricing work for custom projects?

Because every special project is unique, pricing is tailored to the specific requirements of your brief, including format, materials, finishing, volume, and distribution scope. Once we understand your project, we'll put together a clear, transparent quote. Our goal is to offer the quality of a specialist printer with the commercial transparency of an international platform.
