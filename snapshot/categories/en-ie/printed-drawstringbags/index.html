Printed Drawstring Bags for Clubs, Schools and Events

Why promotional drawstring bags work

A drawstring bag is light, roomy and carried on the back, so your logo travels to training, the pool and the school gate. People keep reusing them, which makes promotional drawstring bags great value as a giveaway.

Pick your material

  • Polyester for bright colours and big giveaways
  • Cotton and recycled cotton for a natural feel
  • RPET and other recycled fabrics
  • Mesh and non-woven bags for specific uses

Popular across Ireland

GAA and rugby clubs use personalised drawstring bags as kit bags, schools hand them out on open days, and companies fill them with other giveaways for conferences and staff events.

Questions About Branded Drawstring Bags

Which material should I choose for a printed drawstring bag?

Choose polyester for bright colours and large giveaways, or cotton and recycled cotton for a more natural look. RPET and other recycled fabrics suit brands that prefer recycled materials. Each product page lists the exact material of every personalised drawstring bag.

Are printed drawstring bags a good fit for sports clubs?

Yes. Clubs use them as kit bags for boots, gumshields and water bottles, or sell them to supporters. A bag in club colours with the crest is light, practical and easy to carry to training and matches.

Can I use promotional drawstring bags as conference goody bags?

Yes. A drawstring bag holds leaflets, samples and small gifts, and visitors carry it on their back while they walk around. After the event, it goes to the gym or the pool, taking your logo with it.

Do you have recycled drawstring bags?

Yes. The range includes RPET drawstring bags, recycled cotton drawstring bags and other models made with recycled fabrics. Check the product page of each bag to see exactly which recycled material is used.

Can I design my drawstring bag online?

Yes, many printed drawstring bags can be designed online. Choose your model, add your logo or text and set the quantity. If you already have artwork, you can use your own file instead. Our team is always here to help.

What are personalised drawstring bags used for?

Schools use them for PE kit, clubs for training gear, festivals as merchandise and companies hand out branded drawstring bags as welcome packs or event giveaways. They are light enough to post and easy to fill with other branded items.

How do you carry a personalised drawstring bag?

The cords close the bag and double as shoulder straps, so you can carry it on your back like a light rucksack. That keeps your hands free on the way to training, the office or an event.

What is the difference between a drawstring bag and a backpack?

A drawstring bag is a light sack closed with cords that double as straps, ideal for giveaways and sports kit. A backpack has padded straps, zips and compartments, which suits heavier daily use.
