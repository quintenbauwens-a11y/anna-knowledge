Your Advent Calendar Questions Answered

How many corporate advent calendars do I need to order?

You can order from just 5 calendars per design, which suits a small team or a test run. Prices are per design, so a version for clients and another for your own team count as two items, and the price per calendar drops as quantities grow.

Which treats can go inside a branded calendar?

Choose from Kinder, Celebrations, Milka, Toblerone, premium mini sticks, Lindt Lindor, merci Petits or Ritter Sport. For a classic look, the standard calendars hold fair trade Gubor milk chocolate with at least 35% cocoa.

Is a custom advent calendar the same as a personalised advent calendar?

Both terms describe a calendar with 24 doors and a front printed single-sided in full colour with your logo or own design. What changes is the filling: classic Gubor milk chocolate or a well-known branded treat.

What size is a custom advent calendar for business gifting?

Large landscape and large portrait both measure 348 x 248 mm and give your artwork the most space, ideal for a standout client gift. Small landscape (207 x 146 mm, about A5) suits longer lists of clients or staff.

What file should I send for printing?

Send a print-ready PDF made on the template for your format, with 3 mm bleed on every side. Keeping logos and text well away from the edges means nothing important is cut off when the calendars are trimmed.

Does the chocolate contain allergens?

Yes, and they differ per filling: the classic Gubor chocolate and each branded treat have their own ingredients, and some branded fillings contain nuts. Each product page lists the full ingredients and allergens. For a gift without the 24 doors, have a look at our personalised chocolate range.
