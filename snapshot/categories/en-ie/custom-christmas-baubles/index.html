Custom Christmas Baubles with Your Own Design

Create custom glass Christmas baubles with your own design and turn them into an eye-catching Christmas gift that perfectly matches your brand. Choose from a wide range of colours, sizes and printing techniques, and complete your baubles with matching packaging.

Your logo or design on a glass Christmas bauble

Turn your Christmas baubles into a distinctive branded gift with your own logo or design. Choose screen printing for simple logos and bold designs, or pad printing when your artwork contains finer details or multiple colours. Depending on the printing technique, your design can be applied to one side, two sides or all around the bauble.

Choose from more than 100 colours

Match your Christmas baubles to your brand or festive campaign. The Basic Plus collection offers around 120 colours, including glossy, matt and metallic finishes. Choose from classic Christmas shades such as red, green, gold and silver, or opt for blue, pink, purple, copper and a variety of pastel colours. For the most popular standard colours, printing is available from 240 baubles. Colours from the extended Basic Plus collection are available from 500 pieces per colour.
