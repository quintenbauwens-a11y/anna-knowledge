Our Commitment to a better planet

At HelloPrint, our dedication to fostering a healthier planet is woven into the very fabric of our business. Through a carefully curated selection of certifications, we not only promise superior quality in our clothing range but also ensure our practices contribute positively to environmental sustainability and ethical labor standards. Each certificate represents a step forward in our journey towards a more responsible and sustainable future. Below, we share the essence of these commitments, highlighting how they reflect our core values and our promise to you and the planet. More about our certificates.

  • OEKO-TEX®: Ensuring Safety in Textiles – This label guarantees that textiles have been rigorously tested for harmful substances, exceeding global safety standards. It's a mark of consumer safety and manufacturer commitment to minimizing health risks.
  • Aware: Advancing Sustainability with Technology – Aware uses tracer and blockchain technology to verify the sustainability of textiles, enhancing transparency and authenticity in environmental claims, and leading the way towards a more accountable textile industry.
  • GOTS: Setting the Bar for Organic Textiles – GOTS certifies textiles made from organic fibers, ensuring they meet strict environmental and social criteria throughout the supply chain, promoting organic farming and ethical manufacturing practices.
  • amfori BSCI: Advocating for Sustainable Trade – Amfori and its ‘Trace with Purpose’ initiative are transforming the textile industry by promoting transparency and sustainability in the supply chain, ensuring quality textile products that respect workers and the environment.
  • Fair Wear: Elevating Labor Standards in Fashion – Adhering to eight core labor standards, Fair Wear works towards ethical labor practices in the fashion industry, emphasising voluntary employment, fair compensation, and safe working conditions.
  • Better Cotton: Ensuring Sustainable Cotton Farming – Focused on improving the sustainability of cotton farming, Better Cotton trains farmers in eco-friendly practices, promoting soil health, water efficiency, and fair labor conditions to enhance the sector's sustainability.
