    Frequently asked questions about Canva print

    What is Canva?

    Canva is an online design tool used to empower small to medium businesses bringing marketing dreams come to life. This customer-friendly interface enables users to design and create beautiful products like posters, flyers, business cards, stickers and many more.

    How to print from Canva?

    To print with Canva and HelloPrint you just have to select the product you want to print, choose the template that best suits your needs, customise your design by changing the text, colours or images of the template you chose and finally check that everything is correct and send it to print. We will take care of the rest!

    Can I upload my logo using Canva?

    Yes! You can upload and edit (scale, rotate) your logo to fit your needs as brand or business. Once you open the editor, it's possible to upload from the left side menu-bar. Just click the upload button and edit away.

    What HelloPrint products can be designed with Canva?

    The majority of HelloPrint products can be designed with Canva, but not all of them. Just find the label 'design online' with the product and you're good to go, or use the Canva button in the header (top of the website) to start with products that can be designed and printed.

    Can I make changes to my design after designing?

    Yes, it's always possible to return to the Canva editor and make changes to your design. Just hit the 'edit' link from your cart, or if you're still customizing your product (e.g. finish, quantity) then it's possible to go back to your design from the right sidebar.

    Do you have any tips for me?

    Our greatest tip we can offer is to have a play around with the designs and templates. The design tool is completely free and has many different ideas and toolkits that are easily personalisable. So, take your time and try out several different angles and designs, until you find the right one.

    What if I'm not happy with my design?

    If you're unhappy with your design please contact our customer experience team who will be more than happy to assist you.
