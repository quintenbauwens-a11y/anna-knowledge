The Legacy of BIC:<br> Iconic Pens, Endless Possibilities

BIC’s story began in 1944 when Marcel Bich saw the untapped potential of the ballpoint pen. With a vision to create a smooth, effortless writing tool, he launched the BIC® Cristal® in 1950—an instant success that would revolutionise writing forever.

More than 75 years later, BIC remains one of the world's most recognised and trusted brands for writing instruments. While the classic BIC® Cristal® remains an icon, the brand has expanded its range to include a variety of innovative and customisable pens, designed to meet every writing need.
