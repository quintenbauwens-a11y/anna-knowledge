Glossy vs. Matte: Which Paper Type is right for your High Quality Photo Prints?

  • Glossy Prints – Want vibrant colours and deep contrasts? Glossy paper enhances sharpness and makes colours pop, giving your photos a sleek, polished look. It's perfect for high-contrast images, portraits, and landscapes. However, glossy prints can reflect light and show fingerprints, so they’re best suited for albums or frames behind glass.

  • Matte Prints – Prefer a soft, elegant finish? Matte paper reduces glare and fingerprints, making it ideal for black-and-white photography, artistic shots, and display prints. The subtle texture gives a premium feel and ensures your images look stunning under any lighting conditions.

Not sure which one to choose? Think about where and how you'll use your prints—glossy for bold, eye-catching visuals, matte for a timeless, sophisticated look. Either way, you’ll get high-quality, long-lasting prints to cherish.
