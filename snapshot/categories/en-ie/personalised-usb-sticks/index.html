USB Stick FAQs

Where can I order branded USB memory sticks in Ireland?

You can order them right here: choose the model, capacity, print method and quantity online, and your branded USB sticks are made with your logo. The delivery estimate for Ireland is shown on each product page when you configure your order.

How do I get a good USB stick design?

Keep the USB stick design simple and bold so it reads well at a small size. A clear logo in up to four fixed colours prints crisply, while a single-tone mark works best for laser engraving on metal or bamboo.

Are custom and personalised USB sticks the same thing?

Yes, custom USB sticks and personalised USB sticks mean the same: standard USB storage with your own logo or design added. Branded USB sticks is the name most often used when the stick carries a company logo for marketing.

How many USB sticks do I need to order?

Orders for custom USB sticks start from 10 pieces on the Rotate models, so you can keep things small for one team or go bigger for a conference. The exact minimum for each model is shown in its price table.

Which storage size suits USB sticks for Irish events?

Documents, brochures and slide decks fit comfortably on 4 GB. If you want to share video, photo libraries or large catalogues, the bamboo model also comes in 8 and 16 GB. The metal model is available in 4 GB.

Metal or bamboo: which fits my company best?

Metal gives a sleek, solid finish that suits tech firms, finance and professional services. Bamboo brings a warmer, natural look and more capacity options. Both have a swivel cover and take a 1 to 4 colour print or laser engraving.

Is full colour printing available?

Full colour printing is not offered on our personalised USB sticks in Ireland. Logos are applied with a 1 to 4 colour print or laser engraving, which suits clean logos in fixed brand colours better than photos or colour gradients.

Can I add other branded tech to my order?

Yes, a 4-port USB hub and the Incognito privacy kit sit on this page next to the USB sticks. Together they make a practical desk set for new starters, hybrid teams or valued clients, all carrying your brand.
