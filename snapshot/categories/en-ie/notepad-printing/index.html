Choosing Custom Notepads for Office, Events and Gifts

For a welcome pack, choose notepads with cover, so your design sits on the outside. The cover comes in 170 gsm silk, 250 gsm silk or 170 gsm recycled, and the print is full colour. The same pad suits a client gift.

In the office, standard notepads are the pads staff use every day. With the grey cardboard back, you can write on the pad while standing. By the till, a square pad takes quick messages. On a desk, an A4 pad leaves room for longer notes. Standard notepads also offer PMS colours, print on both sides and an optional printed cover.

At a training session, notes often need to stay together. Spiral-bound notebooks keep every page bound.
