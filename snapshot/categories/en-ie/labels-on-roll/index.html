With or without dispenser, what's the difference?

Labels on roll are a powerful tool to provide information, customise packaging or simply put your logo wherever you like. An important thing to consider when ordering your printed labels on roll is if you need a dispenser or not, so what's the difference?

  • With dispenser – Labels on roll with a dispenser are your go-to if you need to manually place your labels on your product. Thanks to the convenient box that separates each label when you pull them out, you can simply place your labels wherever you need. Pull and stick, that's it!
  • Without dispenser – Labels on a roll without dispenser are the option if you have a labelling machine. They are ideal if need to label a great amount of products automatically without having to remove the label manually from its roll.
