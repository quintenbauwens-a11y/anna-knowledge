Booklet or book, which one fits your project?

Choose a booklet when you need something quick to hand out and easy to scan. Great for events, sales visits, short menus, price lists, and product highlights. It is light to carry and easy to share. Add a QR code or contact details so people can act fast. Keep it simple: one message, clear sections, and a strong call to action.

Choose book printing when the content is longer or needs to feel more official. A book holds up better and sits nicely on a desk or in a client folder. Ideal for catalogues, company handbooks, onboarding packs, training guides, and client presentations. If you want people to keep it and use it later, this is the better pick.
