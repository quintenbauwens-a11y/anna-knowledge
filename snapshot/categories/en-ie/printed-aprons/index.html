Custom Aprons: Questions from Irish Businesses

Can I order just one apron?

Many models can be ordered from 1 piece, while some start at 5 or 10. The product page shows the minimum for each apron, and the price per piece goes down as you order more, which suits larger teams.

Is embroidery or print better for a pub or café logo?

Embroidery is the best match for logos and small text, giving a durable, textured look that lasts. Print works better for colourful or larger designs. Which techniques you can use depends on the model, and each product page lists them clearly.

Which fabrics can I choose from, and are there greener options?

Printed aprons in our range come in cotton, organic cotton, recycled cotton, a recycled cotton-polyester blend, polyester, denim, canvas and hemp. Organic cotton, recycled cotton, recycled canvas and hemp are the more sustainable picks, and there is a chef apron with leather details too.

Do I need my logo ready before I order?

Not necessarily. You can upload your logo straight away, or choose to upload it later after adding the apron to your cart. Either way, a sharp, high-resolution file gives the cleanest result on custom aprons.

Are there aprons for a club barbecue or summer event?

Two BBQ aprons come as a set with tools: the Donau East BBQ Apron with Tools and the PET BBQ Apron Set. Add your club or company logo and they make a handy giveaway or prize at summer events.
