Questions About Branded Keyrings

Which branded keyrings are best for events?

Light, practical keyrings work best for events because visitors happily take them home. Printed keyrings in PET suit large numbers, while a bottle opener or carabiner keyring stands out more. Choose the model that fits your audience and the size of the event.

Which materials can I choose from?

You can choose from wood, bamboo, aluminium, PET, RPET felt and leather-look keyrings, among others. Natural materials suit a warm brand, while aluminium gives a modern finish. Each product page lists the exact material of that model.

Can a keyring do more than hold keys?

Yes. Many models have a practical extra, such as a bottle opener, a carabiner, an LED torch or a tape measure. A keyring with a useful function is used more often, so your logo is seen more often too.

Are promotional keyrings a good choice for estate agents?

Yes, they are a natural fit. Handing over the keys to a new home on a keyring with your logo leaves a lasting impression. Buyers and tenants see your brand every time they open the front door.

Can I design my keyring online?

Yes, many keyrings can be designed online. Choose your model, add your logo or text and set the quantity. If you already have artwork, you can use your own file instead. Our team is always here to help if you have questions.

Are keyrings with a logo good staff gifts?

Yes. A keyring with your company logo is a small, useful welcome gift for new starters or a thank-you after a busy season. Choose a leather-look or wooden model for a more premium feel.

What makes keyrings effective advertising?

Keyrings are used every day and travel everywhere with their owner. That gives your logo far more views than most other giveaways. They are also small and light, so they are easy to hand out or add to an order.

Can sports clubs order keyrings with their crest?

Yes. Clubs often use custom keyrings as fundraisers, membership gifts or merchandise for supporters. A keyring in club colours with the crest is affordable to order and easy to sell at matches and events.

Are there keyrings made from recycled materials?

Yes. The range includes keyrings in RPET felt and other recycled materials, as well as wood and bamboo models. Check the product page of each keyring to see exactly which material is used.
