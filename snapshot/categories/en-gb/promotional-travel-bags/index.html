Personalised travel bags: branding that travels

Personalised travel bags for business and events

Branding that leaves the office

A travel bag is one of the few branded items that goes on the trip with the person carrying it. Your logo turns up at airports, in hotel lobbies and at client sites, on every trip, for as long as the bag lasts.

Choosing the right size

Weekend bags, holdalls and wheeled models

Compact weekend bags suit staff gifts and short trips. Mid size holdalls carry a few days of luggage and work well for conferences and client visits. Larger wheeled travel bags suit people who are on the road most weeks. Look for reinforced handles, a separate shoe compartment and an adjustable shoulder strap.

Print or embroidery

Two ways to apply your logo

Printing gives you full colour and works well for larger artwork across a panel. Embroidery gives a more premium finish and holds up to repeated packing, which suits corporate gifts. Upload your artwork and we will tell you which method suits it best.

Ordering and delivery

A proof before anything is produced

Order a small run for one team or larger quantities for an event. You receive a digital proof to approve before production starts, so you can see exactly where the branding sits and how it will look on the finished bag.
