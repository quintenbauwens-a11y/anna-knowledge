Frequently Asked Questions

What material are the custom shirts made from?

Our range of personalised shirts are made from a mixture of polyester, cotton and a sprinkle of love.

What technology suits best for colourful and detailed logos?

The best technology for colourful and detailed logos is screenprinting, it is very durable and great for printing vibrant colours. The washability is also better with screenprinting compared to DTG (direct to garment).

How should I care for my custom shirt?

Our custom shirts and blouses are made of a high quality materials that will serve you well for years if taken care of. To maintain the quality of your personalised dress shirts, we recommend not to iron the printed areas. In addition, to preserve the coloured areas in the best way possible, we advise to wash your shirt inside out and at temperature of 30 degrees.
