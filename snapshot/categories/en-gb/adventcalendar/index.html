Why Personalised Advent Calendars Work as a Business Gift

24 Brand Moments Instead of One

A Christmas card gets one glance. Personalised advent calendars stay on a desk or in the office kitchen and get opened 24 times, so your logo or design on the full-colour front stays in view all through December. That makes corporate advent calendars a natural fit for end-of-year relationship marketing, staff thank-yous and Christmas campaigns or events.

Choosing Format and Filling

  • Classic personalised chocolate advent calendars: fair trade Gubor milk chocolate (at least 35% cocoa) in large landscape or large portrait (both 348 x 248 mm) or small landscape (207 x 146 mm, about A5), with a shelf life of about 6 months.
  • Branded advent calendars: Kinder, Celebrations, Milka, Toblerone, premium mini sticks or Lindt Lindor. The Kinder version has a sturdy inlay in pressed paperboard and keeps for about 3 months.
  • Quantities: order from just 5 calendars per design, ideal for a small team or a test run, or scale up to bulk advent calendars for a full client list or several offices. The price per calendar drops as the quantity grows.

Artwork Tips for Printed Advent Calendars

For smooth advent calendar printing, start from the template for your format, add 3 mm bleed and upload a print-ready PDF. Use one strong image or colour across the front, keep logos and text well inside the edges and leave room for a short Christmas message. Need different personalised advent calendars for teams, regions or client groups? Create one design per version, each from just 5 pieces.

Questions About Corporate Advent Calendars

What is the minimum order for corporate advent calendars?

You can order from just 5 calendars per design, so a small team, one client group or a test run is easy to cover. Prices are set per design, so separate versions for teams or regions each become their own item, and the price per calendar drops as quantities grow.

What is the difference between a classic and a branded advent calendar?

A classic calendar holds fair trade Gubor milk chocolate and comes in three formats, while a branded advent calendar is filled with well-known treats such as Kinder, Celebrations, Milka, Toblerone or Lindt Lindor. Both carry your logo or design on a full-colour printed front.

Which format suits personalised advent calendars best?

Large landscape and large portrait (both 348 x 248 mm) give your artwork the most room, so they suit image-led designs and hero gifts. Small landscape (207 x 146 mm, about A5) is the compact choice when you are sending to a long client list.

Can I order personalised chocolate advent calendars in bulk?

Yes. Orders start from 5 calendars per design and you can order bulk advent calendars of one design in any larger quantity. Personalised chocolate advent calendars with Gubor milk chocolate are a practical pick for big runs, as they keep for about six months.

Do you supply wholesale advent calendars for large companies?

Wholesale advent calendars are ordered per design, from 5 pieces up to large quantities, so corporate advent calendars for several offices or a national client base are simple to set up. Add one design per audience, such as clients and staff, and order each in the quantity you need.

Which part of printed advent calendars carries my design?

The front carries your design, printed single-sided in full colour with your logo or own artwork. Behind it sit 24 doors, so the printed front is what people see on their desk every day until Christmas.

How should I prepare artwork for advent calendar printing?

Download the template for your chosen format, add 3 mm bleed on all sides and save your design as a print-ready PDF. Keep logos and text well inside the edges so nothing important is lost when the calendars are trimmed.

Why choose promotional advent calendars over a Christmas card?

A Christmas card is read once, while promotional advent calendars give people 24 doors to open in the run-up to Christmas. That adds up to 24 small brand moments for clients, employees and partners, ideal for end-of-year relationship marketing.

How long does the chocolate in the calendars keep?

Classic calendars with Gubor milk chocolate have a shelf life of about six months. Kinder calendars keep for about three months and come with a sturdy inlay in pressed paperboard, so plan your order early if you choose that filling.

Which allergens does the chocolate contain?

Allergens differ per filling. The classic Gubor chocolate and each branded treat have their own ingredients, and some branded fillings contain nuts. Check the full ingredients and allergens on the product page of the calendar you choose, especially before you order for staff or clients with allergies.

Are business advent calendars suitable for staff as well as clients?

Yes, business advent calendars work as a Christmas thank-you for employees, a gift for clients and partners, or a giveaway at Christmas events. A custom advent calendar for business use can carry a different design for each group, each from just 5 pieces.
