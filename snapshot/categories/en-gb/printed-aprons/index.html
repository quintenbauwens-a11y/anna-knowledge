Apron Printing or Embroidery?

Embroidery gives a durable, textured finish and suits logos and small text, which makes an embroidered apron with logo a classic choice for front-of-house teams. Apron printing in full colour, or 1 to 4 colour screen print, is the better fit for larger or more colourful artwork. Each product page shows which techniques are available.
