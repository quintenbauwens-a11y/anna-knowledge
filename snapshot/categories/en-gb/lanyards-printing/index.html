Frequently Asked Questions for Lanyard Printing

Which attachment (clip or hook) is needed for personalised lanyards?

We offer so many attachment options to choose from, with our custom lanyards available to order with metal clips, buckles, buckle and safety clip combinations, retractable clips, roller clips, detachable buckles, and metal hooks.

Deciding which is right for you depends on the overall use of your lanyard, with some attachments more suitable for holding certain items than others. A clip or hook that’s meant for carrying a PVC badge or key bundle for instance may not be suitable for the carrying of heavy tools, devices or mobile phones. The frequency of use will also influence which attachment will work better for your lanyard.

Many of our custom printed lanyards feature a trigger clip for easy attachment and detachment, while ensuring optimum security. This attachment is the safest option for general, lightweight items, including ID badges, fobs, keys, small torches and cameras. We also stock and supply roller clip attachments for keys and IDs.

Please note, these attachments are not suitable for heavier devices like smartphones. Please check out our Kubi Phone Lanyard or AMESTE Phone Holder Lanyard.

Can I add a safety breakaway clip to my lanyard printing design?

Here at HelloPrint, our customers’ safety is our priority. That’s why many of our custom lanyards feature breakaway closures. Our breakaway clips are safe and sturdy delivering the reassurance you need when using our custom print lanyards at your event or workplace.

Breakaway clips are integral to safeguarding your workers, visitors and guests from preventable injuries and even fatalities. They primarily prevent choking hazards, and can be lifesaving in high-risk environments where moving machinery or aggressive clients/patients are regular occurrences. Safety breakaway clips can also improve the comfort of your company lanyards, with their length able to be adjusted simply, and the lanyard itself removed and reattached just as easily.

While the addition of safety breakaway clips to your lanyard design isn’t a legal requirement, it is a best practice safety measure and comes strongly recommended in settings like schools, local authorities and healthcare facilities.

Make sure your work environment is as safe as it can be by choosing our custom lanyards with breakaway clips.

What width do you require for the straps?

The width of your lanyards is more important than you might think. As well as defining the appearance of your event and company lanyards, the right lanyard width can directly influence comfort. If the lanyard strap is too thin for example, this may be uncomfortable when worn for longer periods of time. Again, this customisable option can be determined by your lanyard’s purpose.

For the carrying of lightweight items, like ID cards and key fobs, or for short-term use, a narrow lanyard strap of 10 to 15mm is recommended. This is great for visitors to your school or workplace, or for lanyards destined for souvenir use.

The most popular width for lanyards is 20mm. This medium width lanyard is typically relied on for everyday use. Our wider lanyard widths (25mm) are great for drawing attention to eye-catching and engaging promotional material, and are top choices for trade shows, events, conferences or any opportunity where you want your branded lanyards to stand out. These wider lanyards are also more comfortable to wear, especially for longer periods.

To explore the strap width options of our custom lanyard designs, please visit individual product pages for details, including pricing.

Do you provide recycled PET eco-friendly straps?

Yes, we do! Our commitment to sustainability, and our status as an official B Corp company, means we offer several eco-friendly custom lanyard options. Lanyards printed on RPET (recycled polyethylene terephthalate) tick many of these eco-friendly boxes, ensuring a sustainable printed product for you, a demonstration of your eco-credentials, and the seal of approval from us!

For those of you unfamiliar with RPET material, this is a fibre produced using PET plastic. Unlike virgin PET plastic, RPET is a post-consumer material that has been collected, cleaned and repurposed as something new, which in this case is our custom print lanyards.

Are there eco-friendly bamboo lanyard options?

Our Bamboo Lanyards can be used to demonstrate your commitment to sustainability every day, offering great value and even better convenience when keeping keys and ID passes close too.

As a material, bamboo is up there with the eco-friendliest fibres. It grows incredibly fast and is highly renewable as a result, with resources replenished in as little as 3 to 5 years. What are the differences between personalised lanyard printing methods?

To print lanyards here at HelloPrint, we call on two printing methods, namely screen printing and dye sublimation. The print technique selected depends on the number of colours featured in your design (1, 2, 3, 4 or full colour) and the quantity ordered.

Sublimation is a print method generally reserved for full colour custom print lanyards. This method sees printing conducted at high temperature using eco-friendly, water-based ink, which seeps into the material for a flexible, waterproof finish. For personalized ID lace manufacturing in 1 to 4 colours, screen printing is typically the method of choice.

The screen printing process utilises a woven mesh screen and ink-blocking stencil to print the necessary imagery. This produces high quality results for large scale orders in particular, and does so very cost effectively.
