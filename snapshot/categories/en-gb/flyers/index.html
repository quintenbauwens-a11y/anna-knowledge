Best Practice for Eye-Catching Leaflet Design

Making sure your brand looks great on paper is easier said than done. With the right advice however, your printed hand outs can tick all the right boxes to deliver an eye-catching look, engaging experience and highly effective marketing strategy. Use the following tips to create custom printed flyers that are extra special…

  • Start with a great headline: it should grab attention and focus on what the reader could gain to have the impact you’re looking for. Keep your headline concise and to the point to improve engagement.

  • Don’t overcomplicate your offering: your custom printed flyers have to be scannable as well as suggestable. Make what you are advertising obvious so nothing is missed.

  • Let your images do the talking: including visuals is a must for leaflets of all sizes and styles. Make sure they are relevant and high resolution – no stock or generic images please!

  • Keep text short and sweet: if text is part of your plan to woo customers, make sure the content you include is short and skimmable. Many use bullet points, icons and subheads to keep content engaging.

  • Close with a solid CTA: a call-to-action (CTA) will help you close the deal. Ask yourself what action you want the reader to take – call, book, visit your website, head to your store or redeem an offer? Choose one to keep things simple and get straight to the point.

What paper weight (gsm) do you require?

Here at HelloPrint, we take the quality of our custom printed flyers very seriously. Along with the material, size and design, the thickness is a key indication of quality, and it plays a bigger part than you think when you print flyers online.

The thickness or paper weight (gsm) essentially dictates the feel of your business leaflets, with thicker, higher gsm products sporting a sturdier, more premium finish. We offer several paper weight options so you can choose the perfect foundation for your custom printed flyers. To satisfy budget and quality requirements, we always recommend our standard 170 gsm paper weight when shopping our print leaflets.

Select super budget (90 gsm), budget (135 gsm), standard (170 gsm), premium (250 gsm), premium plus 300 gsm or premium plus 400 gsm in a finish you love and discover printed hand outs that fit your brief. With our eco flyers, paper weights of between 80 and 300 gsm are available depending on the material you choose.

Customise your budget, standard or luxury leaflet design further by exploring our folded options. When picking the right design for you, there are several factors that could influence your decision.

For simple, direct messages that require clear visibility at high volume, such as sale promotions, limited-time deals and opening events, single sheet custom printed flyers are generally the best choice. Folded flyers are highly engaging and readable too, with the design of your front cover offering the hook, the inside panels delivering the details and the back page closing the deal.

Can I upload my own leaflet design?

You certainly can! To ensure the quality of your artwork is print-ready however, there are a number of guidelines you should be aware of. We recommend uploading your artwork as a print-ready PDF with CMYK as the colour mode. Any imagery used in your leaflet design should be at least 300 dpi, while text should be legible with a minimum font size of 6pt and line thickness of at least 0.25pt. Beware of the bleed too. For all our custom printed leaflets, we suggest adding a bleed of 3mm and a safety margin of 4mm. Don’t worry if your file size is too big for our standard upload process.
