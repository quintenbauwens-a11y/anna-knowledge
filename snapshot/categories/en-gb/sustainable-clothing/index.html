Wear your values

  • Transparency – Iqoniq's belief is that the foundation for recyclability lies in transparency. We're open about our endeavours to bring about positive changes, both at home and throughout our supply chain.
  • Traceability – Iqoniq firmly believes that traceability and sustainability are intricately linked, which is why we collaborated with Aware™, a traceability platform that makes it possible to track the impact of sustainable clothing from origin to you.
  • Giving back – Iqoniq is committed to giving back to the planet. We want to entice our customers to shop consciously and bring about change, which is why we've partnered with selected environmental NGOs that work hard towards a sustainable future.
  • Made2Flow – Iqoniq has collaborated with Made2Flow, a company that specialises in impact calculations for the fashion industry, to calculate the GHG (Greenhouse Gas/CO2 Footprint), water and land footprints of our products.

Sustainable Stanley/Stella Clothing

Clothing that puts people and the planet first.

Sustainable fashion for a better tomorrow

  • 100% Organic & recycled – Stanley/Stella prioritises environmentally and socially responsible practices, beginning with the sourcing of raw materials. We work closely with our suppliers to ensure that the materials we use are sustainably grown or produced, without harm to people, animals, or the environment.
  • Certified – Stanley/Stella has obtained certifications from several independent bodies for our supply chain, demonstrating our commitment to developing healthy environmental practices and promoting greater respect for the planet throughout our production process.
  • Transparency – At Stanley/Stella, transparency is a core value, and we uphold it by openly sharing the names and contact information of all our suppliers. We work with a small number of partner factories - five in Bangladesh and one in China - fostering strong, long-term, and collaborative relationships with each of them.
  • Environmental impact – To reduce our environmental impact, Stanley/Stella gathers data from our entire supply chain to gain insights and identify areas for improvement. All of our partner factories have advanced Effluent Treatment Plants, which eliminate harmful substances from water, making it safe to reuse.
