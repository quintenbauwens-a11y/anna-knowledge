Create the perfect custom poster for your business

As a business owner, creating the perfect poster can be a powerful tool for marketing and promotion. Whether it’s to advertise a new product, a sale, or a special event, a well-designed poster will grab attention and leave a lasting impression. In this guide, we'll walk you through the steps to create the ideal poster for your business, with key considerations such as design, format, and budget. Ready to step up your poster printing?

1. Start with a Clear Purpose for your Poster Printing

Before diving into design, it’s crucial to define the purpose of your poster. In a first step, ask yourself:

  • What message do you want to communicate?

  • Who is your target audience?

  • What action do you want viewers to take?

Whether you're aiming to boost foot traffic to your store, promote an upcoming event, or increase brand awareness, having a clear objective will guide every design decision you make.

2. Choose the Right Poster Size for your Custom Posters

Choosing the right size for your poster depends on where it will be displayed and how much information you need to include. For smaller spaces like cafes or bulletin boards, A3 poster printing may be ideal. For larger spaces such as shop windows or event venues, consider A1 poster printing or A2 poster printing.

Here’s a quick guide:

  • A3 posters (297 x 420 mm): Perfect for smaller spaces, suitable for promotional offers and quick-read information.

  • A2 posters (420 x 594 mm): Great for medium-sized spaces with more content.

  • A1 posters (594 x 841 mm): Ideal for large spaces where visibility from a distance is essential.

If you have a unique requirement, custom size poster printing is a great option to tailor the poster exactly to your needs.

3. Opt for Eye-Catching Design Elements

To create a compelling poster, make sure it stands out visually. Use high-quality images that are relevant to your message, and opt for bold, legible fonts that are easy to read from a distance. Consider using contrasting colours to make the text pop against the background.

For photo-heavy designs, photo poster printing ensures that your images come out crisp and clear. If you’re promoting a sale or event with a time limit, add a bold CTA (Call to Action) like “Limited Time Offer” or “Visit Us Today” to create urgency.

5. Customise Posters to Your Business Needs

Customising your posters ensures that they reflect your brand and target audience. With custom poster printing, you can tailor everything from the size and format to the paper quality and finish.

For high-end retail stores, you may want to use glossy photo paper for a sleek, polished look. For events or concerts, matte finishes can create a more artistic, modern vibe.

If you’re in a rush, next day poster printing services are available to get your posters out quickly without compromising on quality.
