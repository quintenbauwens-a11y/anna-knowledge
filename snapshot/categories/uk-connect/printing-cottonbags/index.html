The Golden Rules for Designing Branded Tote Bags

Whether uploading your artwork or using our editor to design a tote bag, there are various artwork requirements you need to fulfil to ensure you achieve the finish you desire and deserve. Here are our recommendations for elevating your design to print-ready status:

  • Take a hint from us: getting the design of your branded tote bags just right for printing is as important to us as it is to you. This is why we provide a range of downloadable design templates and guides to lend a hand. Access them via our templates section.

  • Select the right palette: if, like many of the customers who design a tote bag for printing, you are including graphics in your artwork, you’ll want to make sure those colours pop. When printing custom tote bags with solid shades, be sure to supply your design with Pantone Solid colours. Full colour printed tote bags must be supplied in CMYK.

  • Size and space text correctly: if text forms part of your design, this requires just as much precision. Make sure each and every word is legible, with a font size of at least 12pt. Lines should be spaced by at least 3pt to improve readability further. Small black text should also be 100% black, i.e. Cyan: 0, Magenta: 0, Yellow: 0 and Black: 100. When exporting your artwork file, embed or outline all fonts first - and don’t forget to check for spelling and grammar errors!

  • Keep the resolution high: the higher the resolution, the better, with any images and graphics included in your artwork needing to be at least 300 dpi as a rule of thumb.

Which printing methods are used for tote bags, and how do they affect the final product's quality?

At HelloPrint Connect, we call on the latest and greatest print techniques to bring our personalised tote bags to life. Our UK custom tote bag printing service uses screen printing or digital transfer to guarantee the high quality finish, vibrancy and longevity our customers have come to know and love.

For designs with up to 4 colours, our screen printed tote bags are the best choice. While this method may not be suitable for intricate motifs with lots of different colours, it’s the most efficient and effective for simpler designs with fewer shades.

Screen printed tote bags are applauded for their brightness, with striking finishes coming as standard. Their durability is also impressive, with less fading and cracking over time, even with extended or heavy use. We use screen printing to print your custom designs on a range of materials, including cotton and non-woven fibres.

If the design of your custom tote bags is a little more complex, our experts will use digital transfer to achieve the same high quality look and durable feel. Also referred to as ‘UV bag printing’, the digital transfer technique uses UV ink and printers to ensure top quality results across numerous products, including branded tote bags, with fantastic results in full technicolour.
