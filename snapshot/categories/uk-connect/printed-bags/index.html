How do you remove a printed logo from a personalised bag?

If it’s time for a change and you want to remove a printed logo from your personalised bags, don’t panic. While DIY removal isn’t ideal, it is possible. The results however depend largely on the fabric of your custom bags and the print method used to adhere the logo. Our jute bags for example are very difficult to remove logos from after printing due to the absorbent, textured nature of the fibres.

If the logo is applied using digital transfer or vinyl, removal can be as simple as applying nail varnish remover or rubbing alcohol with a cotton wool ball to the logo and peeling it away. Dab, don’t soak, the area for the best results. Heat can also be used to encourage peeling, but this can often leave residue behind. Again, acetone or rubbing alcohol can be used to remove any logo leftovers.

If the material of your personalised bags allows it, wash according to the manufacturer’s instructions after logo removal and before use.

How can you remove screen printing from a custom backpack?

If your custom shopping bags or backpacks are screen printed, your task may be a little trickier. You should however use the methods mentioned above for DIY removal.

You can also use a sugar scrub, which is particularly effective on prints that have become cracked or worn over time. For the best results, combine with rubbing alcohol or acetone for full removal. You should avoid this method if you have opted for a lighter weight fabric as this can damage thinner cotton canvas satchels or similar designs.
