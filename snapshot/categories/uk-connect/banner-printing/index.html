How to Design Your Own Custom Banner with Canva

Don’t let the quality of our custom banners go to waste! At HelloPrint Connect, we understand that being able to print a banner solves only part of the problem. Use the following tips to make this editor even easier to use and reap all the rewards of our top-quality printing services…

Think high-contrast when choosing colours

The colours you choose and use to create your custom banners with Canva should stand out for all the right reasons. As well as making sure they align with your brand, they should ideally be high contrast. It’ll make your promotional banners even more eye-catching, interesting and readable.

Use simplicity to your advantage

A simple design is often better received by audiences, which means you should avoid clutter when designing your banners. Steer clear of including too much information or too many images. Instead, keep things clear, simple and concise. The same rules apply with your font, which should be large and legible.

Don’t forget the CTA

The call-to-action, or CTA, is an important aspect of any promotional campaign. Without one, your banner would be rendered pretty useless, giving customers who want to find out more nowhere to go. Use your CTA to tell them what to do next, whether that’s “Visit our store today”, “Sign up now” or “Learn more on our website”.

Keep your imagery high quality

No banner would be complete without appropriate imagery. Any visuals you use – whether that’s imagery, icons or illustrations - must be high quality and high resolution. They should also align with any key messages and complement your overall colour scheme. If images appear blurry or pixelated, don’t use them. They could negatively affect the credibility and visual appeal of your business banners.

Consider the ‘where’ and ‘when’

The final placement of your promotional banners should always be considered, even early on during the banner design process! Where your banner will be displayed could influence its material, design and size. Outdoor banners for example require larger fonts and more durable materials (such as vinyl) to withstand adverse weather conditions.

How do PVC vinyl and mesh printed banners differ?

When we say the possibilities are endless with custom banners, they really are! Our most popular banner types are PVC vinyl banners and mesh banners, but what is the difference between the two?

PVC vinyl banners are crafted using polyvinyl chloride, a durable plastic that’s already widely used in the creation of printed banners. It’s known for its outstanding strength, water resistance, and fire-retardant properties. Both standard PVC canvas and mesh canvas are made from PVC material, but the difference lies in the design. A mesh canvas for instance has a perforated design, making it particularly wind resistant and robust. As a result, mesh banners are ideal for use in outdoor settings, even in harsher weather.

Appearance-wise, mesh banners have small woven holes upon closer inspection. This provides a slightly transparent, less vibrant finish in comparison to vinyl alternatives. Mesh banners work well with larger fonts and images, while vinyl is the preferred banner type for complex, more intricate designs.

Which material is the best fit for an outdoor banner design?

Thanks to their vibrancy and durability in all weather conditions, standard PVC vinyl banners (510 gsm PVC) are highly recommended for use outdoors. Our vinyl banners can be printed using UV-resistant inks, which means they maintain their colour integrity even when exposed to the sun’s powerful rays. Vinyl banners are also applauded for their high contrast, with everything from graphics and logos to text able to standout successfully.

Mesh outdoor banners

Mesh banners are great choices for outdoor use too. The mesh material is extremely wind resistant due to its open structure and perforated surface. Its water repellence and scratch resistance only add to this robustness. Mesh banners are slightly transparent, which is seen as a positive and a negative. Those looking for a vibrant banner design should opt for PVC vinyl, while mesh’s natural transparency is the preferred choice on construction sites and scaffolding.

Any tips for hanging my promotional banners?

Deciding on the right location for your custom banners is just the beginning. You now need to hang it properly and securely to unlock its full potential. When hanging, use metal rings, placing them at the corners or every 30cm for the best results. The rings are placed approximately 15mm from the edge, and have an internal diameter of 12mm. Ropes, zip ties or hooks can then be threaded through to secure your banner further.

We’d also recommend the tunnel option, which allows for the insertion of poles or ropes to make hanging your banner a breeze and ensure a neat display. When opting for the tunnel option, please consider tunnel positions when submitting your artwork.
