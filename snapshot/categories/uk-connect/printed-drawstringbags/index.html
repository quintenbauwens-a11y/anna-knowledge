Personalised Drawstring Bags for Events, Teams and Giveaways

Why branded drawstring bags work

A drawstring bag is light, roomy and easy to carry on the back or shoulder, so people keep using it long after your event. Every trip to the gym, the pool or the office puts your logo back on show, which makes branded drawstring bags a smart pick for welcome packs, conference goody bags and team kit.

Choose the right material

  • Polyester: light and colourful, ideal for large giveaways
  • Cotton and recycled cotton: a natural look and feel
  • RPET and other recycled fabrics
  • Non-woven, mesh and transparent bags for specific uses

Drawstring bag printing to suit your design

Polyester drawstring bags with a logo are printed in 1, 2 or 3 colours, with a print area of up to 240 x 230 mm. Our cotton drawstring bag can be printed in full colour with a DTF transfer of up to 230 x 280 mm, so detailed logos stay sharp.

From small runs to bulk orders

Our best-selling polyester and cotton bags start from 10 pieces, and the price per piece drops as your quantity grows. Planning a big event? Fill your promotional drawstring bags with other giveaways for a complete goody bag.

Questions About Custom Drawstring Bags

What is the minimum order for printed drawstring bags?

Our best-selling polyester and cotton drawstring bags start from 10 pieces. The price per piece drops as you order more, so larger orders for events and giveaways work out cheaper per bag. Each product page shows all available quantities.

Which material is best for a branded drawstring bag?

It depends on your goal. Polyester is light and colourful, perfect for promotional drawstring bags at big giveaways. Cotton and recycled cotton give a more natural look and feel. RPET and other recycled fabrics suit brands that prefer recycled materials.

How many colours can I print on a personalised drawstring bag?

Drawstring bag printing on our polyester bags is done in 1, 2 or 3 colours. Our cotton drawstring bag can also be printed in full colour with a DTF transfer, which works well for detailed logos, gradients and photos.

How big is the print area on printed drawstring bags?

The print area depends on the bag. On our polyester drawstring bags it is up to 240 x 230 mm, and on the cotton drawstring bag up to 230 x 280 mm. Each product page shows the exact print area for that model.

Are personalised drawstring bags good for events?

Yes. Promotional drawstring bags are light, easy to hand out and practical to carry, so visitors use them straight away for leaflets and samples. After the event, the bag goes to the gym, the pool or school, taking your logo with it.

Can I order printed drawstring bags in bulk?

Yes. Drawstring bags are popular for large runs at trade shows, festivals, races and school events. The more you order, the lower the price per bag, so a large order gives your budget the most reach.

Do you offer recycled drawstring bags?

Yes. You can choose from RPET drawstring bags, recycled cotton drawstring bags and other models made with recycled fabrics. Each product page shows exactly which recycled material is used for that bag.

What can I use a drawstring bag for?

Drawstring bags work as goody bags at events, kit bags for sports teams, swim bags, welcome packs for new staff or students and merchandise for clubs. Drawstring bags with your logo are easy to carry, as the cords double as shoulder straps.

Can I wash a polyester drawstring bag?

Yes, but not above 40 degrees, as stated on the product page of our polyester drawstring bags. A gentle wash keeps the fabric and your printed logo looking good for longer, so the bag stays in use.

What is the difference between a drawstring bag and a backpack?

A drawstring bag is a light sack closed with cords that double as straps, ideal for giveaways and sports kit. A backpack has padded straps, zips and more compartments, which suits heavier everyday use.
