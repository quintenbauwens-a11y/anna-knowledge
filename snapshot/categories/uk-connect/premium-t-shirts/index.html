Choose the material that suits you best

Discover the essence of our materials: breathable cotton, sustainable polyester, and their eco-friendly variants. Each material, from pure to an innovative blend, has been carefully chosen to unite comfort, style and sustainability. Our commitment to environmental awareness is reflected in every fiber, for a conscious and future-oriented collection.

  • Cotton: Breathable, soft, and perfect for all kinds of clothes – The go-to fiber for comfortable, cool clothing. Its natural fibers offer unparalleled airiness, ideal for hot climates, ensuring you stay cool whether in summer attire. Its fibers promise a gentleness against the skin, making cotton essentials perfect for daily wear, relaxation, and sleep.
  • Polyester: Stay dry, move freely – Stay dry, move freely: explore the comfort and durability of lightweight polyester apparel. It is known for its ability to wick away moisture and dry quickly, which makes it comfortable to wear. With it's durability it can withstand a lot of wear and tear from activities and washing. In addition, it's a great choice for athletic wear as of the light weight.
  • Recycled Cotton: A circular fashion system – Recycled cotton reduces textile waste and conserves resources by utilizing post-consumer or post-industrial waste. It contributes to a circular fashion system.
  • Recycled Polyester: From bottles to apparel – Recycled polyester is created by processing used plastic bottles into fibers, which are then spun into yarn and woven into fabric for clothing. This process helps to reduce waste and conserve resources.
  • Organic Cotton: Less water and no chemicals – Organic cotton farming uses 91% less water, avoids synthetic chemicals, promotes soil health, biodiversity, and local ecosystems compared to conventional methods.
  • Blend: Discover strength and comfort – Our cotton blends are perfect for Activewear and Sportswear with durability, wrinkle resistance and stretch. The cotton-polyester blend marries durability with style, creating a robust fabric ideal for activewear that endures regular wear and washes.
