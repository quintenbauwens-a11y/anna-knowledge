How are flags actually printed at HelloPrint Connect?

With digitally printed flags available in any size and shape, our flag creators are among the best around. Our print on demand flags provide a quality that stands the test of time, with flags printed using two techniques: sublimation and UV print.

Sublimation is perhaps the most popular flag printing technique. The process uses heat to transfer your design onto your chosen material to produce a flexible yet waterproof finish. The ink itself is water-based and eco-friendly to ensure a conscious choice when creating event flags and corporate flags.

UV print uses a different type of ink that is also eco-friendly. When exposed to UV light, the curing process is activated instantly with specialist UV ink applied precisely to guarantee a quality print that puts sharpness first.

Both printing techniques ensure a high level of durability that’s suitable for use indoors and outdoors, ensuring a reliable flag printing choice for your brand.
