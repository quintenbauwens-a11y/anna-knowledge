Which Branded Balloon Should You Choose?


For a room full of colour, choose printed latex balloons. They are round, 30.5 cm across and come in 24 latex colours, from 25 pieces. For a festive look at a gala or an awards night, pick printed metallic balloons, 33 cm latex with a pearlised sheen in nine colours, gold and silver included. Printed heart balloons are 25 cm hearts in red, pink or white, and you can order them from 10. When one balloon has to mark a spot, order a giant printed balloon of 60 or 90 cm, even a single one. For a big balloon with a metallic finish, choose giant metallic balloons. Printed punch balloons are 45 cm and come with an elastic band, so children can take them home and play.


Promotional Balloons for Business Events and Weddings


At a trade fair, companies tie printed balloons to the stand and hand them out to visitors. For a company anniversary, metallic balloons give the room a festive shine. Planners and venues order them for weddings and engagement parties, with the couple's names and the date. Round latex, metallic, heart, giant and punch balloons are all printed in one colour, on one side or on both. Round latex, metallic and heart balloons can come with 110 cm ribbons or 30 cm paper sticks, and the giant balloon with a 110 cm ribbon.


Planning the Print on Your Balloons


The print area depends on the type. It is a circle of 16 cm on round latex and metallic balloons, 15 cm on a heart and 20 cm on a punch balloon. A logo or two short lines read best in that space. Choose a print colour that differs clearly from the balloon, such as white on black or dark blue, and a dark colour on yellow or light pink.

What does a printed logo look like on each balloon?

Our product photos show what works per type. On round latex balloons, a logo with a small icon, in white on red or in a dark print on yellow and green. On hearts, a few words in the centre. On giant balloons, an icon with your shop name above and the event below.
