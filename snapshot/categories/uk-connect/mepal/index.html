Frequently Asked Questions for Custom Mepal Products

Which Mepal products can I customise?

You can add your logo to Mepal lunch boxes, bento boxes, lunch pots, snack pots, the EasyClip food storage box, the Cirqula multi bowl, cutlery, water bottles, sport bottles, travel mugs and insulated mugs. Each product page shows the colours and print options for that model.

Are custom Mepal products a good corporate gift?

Yes. A lunch box or water bottle is used every working day, so your logo stays in view far longer than a one-off giveaway. Mepal is a well-known Dutch brand, which gives your gift a quality feel for clients and staff alike.

Which Mepal lunch box should I choose?

The Take a Break lunch box midi (900 ml) suits people who bring sandwiches. The large bento lunch box (1500 ml) with two removable trays works well for salads and snacks. For warm meals, the Ellipse insulated lunch pot in stainless steel is the best fit.

Which Mepal water bottle suits my team?

The Vita water bottle is leak-proof, BPA-free and dishwasher safe, which makes it a good everyday office bottle. Choose a Vita bottle in recycled stainless steel for a more sustainable option, or the Pull, Twist or Shaker for teams that train together.

Can my logo be printed in more than one colour?

That depends on the product. On the Mepal Vita 500 ml water bottle, for example, you choose a print in 1 to 4 colours or in full colour. The options for each model are shown on its product page.

Are Mepal products dishwasher safe?

Many are, including the Take a Break lunch box midi, the Vita water bottle, the Ellipse cutlery set, the Ellipse travel mug and the Cirqula multi bowl. The product description of each model tells you how to clean it.
