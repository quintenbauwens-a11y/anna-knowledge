How to remove a print from a cap

If you want to remove a printed element from your custom cap, then just soak a cotton ball in a nail polish remover and gently rub the design. In case you don't have any nail polish at hand, you can always easily remove the print with the help of an iron and paper bag. Simply put the paper bag on the top of your hat and melt the print using the iron. If the above doesn't work, you can always try another popular method and get rid of the cap print by scraping the design away using some sugar scrub.

What are the types of printing available for caps?

Depending on the cap's brand, you can either the screen printing and embroidery options are available.

How to care for your personalised caps

In order to keep your custom cap stylish and prevent colours from fading, we recommend avoiding machine wash. Therefore, if you want to make your cap look fresh again, we recommend a hand wash using warm water and a very mild textile detergent with no bleach in it. After you hand-washed your cap, fill it in with a dry towel, it will help to preserve the nice shape and prevent wrinkles. Do not put your cap to dry in the sun or next to radiators, otherwise the colour of your cap may quickly fade.
