Outdoor Signs for businesses

Panels are not limited to indoor use; we offer a comprehensive collection of material options suitable for withstanding outdoor elements. Personalised signage is a powerful solution to stand out in bustling environments, whether for storefront signs on a lively street or precision-designed business signs within the office.

Outdoor signs, due to their exposure to weather, need to be durable. Durability and longevity depend not only on the elements, such as sunlight and rain, but also on the material itself. The perfect balance between durability and cost-efficiency is Aluminium material. This weatherproof material will keep it's shape and look, making sure your outdoor sign keeps shining through the seasons.

Do you need a temporary outdoor sign for your event or business? Our foamex material is a popular choice for promotional uses. The material is robust, easy to transport, and mount, making it suitable for shop signs, information boards, and safety signage.

For those requiring sturdier and more hardwearing outdoor signs, Trespa material is an ideal selection. It is weather-resistant and can withstand exposure to harsh outdoor elements like rain, sun, and wind, making it suitable for various applications, including sports field signage.

Custom signs for a lasting impression

Panels create a professional appearance. Aluminum signs are lightweight and durable, while providing a sleek appearance. Featuring a strong core, they can handle windy weather and rough conditions. At HelloPrint Connect we have two types of aluminium signs, painted and brushed. Painted aluminium is ideal for retail shop fronts, making it a perfect option to use as outdoor sign. Brushed aluminium has a stylish metallic touch, perfect for unique decorations. Add glossy or matte lamination for a vibrant look and for extra protection.

Outdoor signage can be elegant and luxurious while maintaining durability. Resistant to rain, snow, sunlight, and extreme temperatures, plexiglass is an excellent choice for various options such as office signage and commercial signs. With versatile customisation options and cost-effectiveness, plexiglass is a reliable and attractive solution.
