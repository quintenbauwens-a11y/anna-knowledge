Personalised Gym & Sports Bags

Personalised gym and sports bags go out several times a week, which makes them one of the most seen items you can brand. Print or embroider your logo on the front panel, side pocket or strap and it travels to training, the gym and away days.

Choose compact gym bags for staff and members, mid size holdalls for clubs, or larger travel bags for tournaments. Every order gets a digital proof before production, so you see exactly how the branding sits.
