Your Questions About Branded USB Sticks

What are branded USB sticks?

Branded USB sticks, also called branded USB flash drives, are custom USB sticks that carry your company logo as a 1 to 4 colour print or laser engraving. Businesses hand them out at events, add them to onboarding packs and give them to clients.

Should USB sticks with logos be printed or laser engraved?

Choose a 1 to 4 colour print when your logo uses fixed brand colours and needs to stand out. Pick laser engraving for a subtle, tone-on-tone mark that suits metal and bamboo surfaces and stays put for the life of the stick.

Which capacity is right for personalised USB sticks?

For documents, brochures and presentations, 4 GB is usually plenty. Go for 8 or 16 GB when you want to share video, high-resolution images or large catalogues. The metal Rotate model comes in 4 GB, and the bamboo model in 4, 8 and 16 GB.

What is the minimum order for promotional USB sticks?

Promotional USB sticks on this page start from 10 pieces, which makes a small batch for one team or a single event easy to arrange. The exact minimum for each model is shown in its price table on the product page.

Is a bamboo or metal USB stick better for a corporate gift?

Bamboo suits brands that want a warm, natural look and offers the widest choice of capacities. Metal gives a sleek, solid feel that fits tech, finance and professional services. Both make branded USB flash drives with a swivel cover that shows off a logo well.

Is a USB memory stick the same as a flash drive?

Yes, USB memory stick, flash drive and USB drive all name the same portable storage that plugs into a USB port. Custom USB drives and custom USB sticks are therefore the same product; in the UK, stick is simply the more common word.

Can I get USB sticks with logo printing in full colour?

Full colour print is not available on the UK models. Your logo is added with a 1 to 4 colour print or laser engraving, which works best for clear logos in fixed brand colours rather than photos or colour gradients.

Do you also offer branded USB hubs?

Yes, a 4-port USB hub sits alongside the branded USB memory sticks on this page, together with the Incognito privacy kit. Both make useful additions to a desk set or welcome pack for new starters, remote teams or loyal clients.

What does the Rotate swivel design do?

Rotate sticks have a swivel cover that turns around the body to protect the USB connector when it is not in use. With no separate cap to lose, these custom USB sticks are practical giveaways at busy trade fairs and conferences.
