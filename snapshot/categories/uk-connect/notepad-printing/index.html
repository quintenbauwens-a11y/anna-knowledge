Which Branded Notepads Fit Your Use?

Glued notepads are the widest range. You choose from 12 sizes, up to three PMS colours or full colour, and a printed cover if you want one. Covered notepads always come with a cover, in A6, A5 or A4, printed in full colour. Spiral-bound notebooks keep every page bound.

On a desk, a glued A5 or A4 pad holds to-do lists. At reception, a small square pad is handy for phone messages. Beside a keyboard, the long 62 x 148 mm pad fits a list. Glued notepads cover all three.

Under a cover, the sheets stay clean in a bag. Pick covered notepads for a meeting. They also work as a client gift, because your design is on the cover.

For a workbook that should stay in one piece, choose spiral-bound notebooks.
