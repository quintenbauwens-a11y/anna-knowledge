## Why You Need Personalised Notepads

Why branded notepads can benefit your business

Custom notepads can benefit your business in many ways. First, they serve as a practical marketing tool that can help to increase brand awareness and visibility. By placing your company's logo and contact information on paper notepads, you can reach more customers and remind your clients of your brand every time they use the printed notepads. Additionally, notepads can help to establish your company as a professional and reliable business, as they show your willingness to attention to detail and quality. They can also be used as a promotional item to attract new customers or as a giveaway to thank existing customers for their loyalty.

So Many Types of Personalised Notepads Available

  • Classic Notepads: You know them, you love them and you certainly can't go wrong with them! Not everything has to be complicated, sometimes simple is best. Just simply add your design or logo to the top of the page and these personalised notepads are ready to go.

  • Notepads with Covers: These covers not only serve as protection for your precious notes from errant coffee spillages, but also serve as a canvas for you to unleash your creativity.

  • Desk Pads: A calendar crossed with a printed notepad, perfect for any desk! Need to make notes quickly whilst in a call? Or quickly make arrangements in the coming weeks? Custom desk pads can do it all!

  • Recycled Notepads: Take notes, spread your brand and save the planet. Your custom printed notebooks could not only have your logo on the top of the page, but also be made from recycled paper.

Design tips for creating your custom notepads:

  1. Use your brand's colour scheme and fonts.

  2. Include your logo clearly on the cover.

  3. Consider adding a tagline or brand mission.

  4. Choose high-quality paper for a professional look and feel.

  5. Consider including helpful information such as a conversion chart or brainstorming prompts.

  6. Add your contact details and opening times so your customers know what times they can get through to you.
