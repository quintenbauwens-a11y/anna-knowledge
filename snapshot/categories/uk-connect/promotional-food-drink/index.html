Why Giving Away Food and Drinks is a Great Strategy

You may think, "How am I tending to my business by offering chocolate bars?" but we can assure you that food and drinks can be great promotional items and here are the reasons why:

They Maximise Visibility

Whether you are the owner of a bar & restaurant or just a small company, having your own personalised food & drinks to offer to your customers is a great way to enhance your brand's exposure.

By doing so, your brand is the first thing they see in the morning, but it's also perfect for other times of the day! Printed pringles box for the afternoon snack, microwave popcorn for the late night TV show, and mints for the whole day... And if you want to offer your own food and drinks, we have the perfect packaging to put your goods in the spotlight to perfection! They will be the frame to your painting and make sure everybody recognises the quality of your service, even when they take it home.

They Go Straight Into People's Heart

They say the way to a man's heart is through his stomach and it couldn't be more true. Everybody likes food, and everybody likes free gifts. Who wouldn't love free food and drinks? Furthermore, when you offer such products, you make sure your company's name will be out there and make people talk! Who wouldn't share how they were offered a nice chocolate bar from their favourite company? People can even share a picture on social media to show off how good they had it! Offering food and drinks is therefore a good way to gain people's trust and affection and be remembered for a long time!

They Are Cheap and Effective

Nowadays, advertising and marketing in general comes with great costs. Adverts in newspapers aren't as effective anymore, TV commercial are extremely costly and the same goes for online ads and social media. Giving away some free food and drinks therefore comes with a relatively low price but is sure to make people talk about your brand about how genuinely kind you were. Distributing free printed water bottles during a sports event for example is sure to make people notice you and remember you helped them by going the extra mile, and so will all the supporters!
