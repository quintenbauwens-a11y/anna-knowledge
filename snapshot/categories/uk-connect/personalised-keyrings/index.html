Custom Keyrings for Business: Choosing the Right One

Branded keyrings that actually get used

A keyring goes everywhere with the person who receives it, so your logo stays in sight long after the event is over. Branded keyrings work well as staff gifts, welcome packs for new customers, trade show giveaways or a thank-you in every order.

Pick a material that fits your brand

  • Wood, bamboo and cork for a natural look
  • Aluminium, zinc alloy and stainless steel for a sturdy, premium feel
  • Leather-look and PU keyrings for corporate gifts
  • Recycled aluminium, recycled plastic and RPET felt models

Personalised key rings with a second job

Useful promotional keychains get kept. Choose a keyring with a bottle opener, an LED torch, a carabiner, a trolley token or a tape measure, or go further with a key finder or power bank. Does your team also carry a badge? Pair your keyrings with personalised lanyards.

Printed or engraved

Printed keyrings show your logo in full colour, and some models are printed on both sides. Engraved keyrings, such as our leather keyring, get a laser-engraved logo for a subtle finish. Each product page shows the options for that model.

From one piece to bulk orders

Some models can be ordered from a single piece, such as the stainless steel photo keyring. Other models start from 5 pieces, and the price per piece drops as your quantity goes up.

Your Questions About Keyrings Answered

What is the minimum order for custom keyrings?

The minimum depends on the model. Some keyrings, such as the budget keyring and the stainless steel photo keyring, can be ordered from 1 piece. Others, like the leather keyring, start from 5 pieces. Each product page shows the quantities available.

Can I have my logo engraved on a keyring?

Yes, on selected models. Our leather keyring, for example, is personalised with laser engraving only, which gives a clean and subtle logo. Other keyrings are printed in full colour. The printing options on each product page show what is possible.

Which branded keyrings work best for promotion?

Keyrings with a second function tend to stay in use the longest. Bottle opener keyrings, LED torch keyrings and trolley token keyrings come in handy every day, so your logo is seen far more often than on a keyring that only holds keys.

Which materials can I choose from?

You can choose wood, bamboo, cork, aluminium, zinc alloy, stainless steel, PET, reflective PVC and leather-look PU. There are also models made with recycled aluminium, recycled plastic or RPET felt, so you can match the material to your brand and budget.

Can I put a photo on a keyring?

Yes. The rectangular stainless steel photo keyring holds your own photo, logo or image behind Plexiglas. You can order it from a single piece, which makes it a good choice for a personal gift or a small team present.

Are printed keyrings printed on both sides?

Some models are. The budget keyring, for example, is always printed in full colour on both sides, so your logo shows whichever way it hangs. Other keyrings have one print area. The printing option on each product page tells you which applies.

How do I add my design to a keyring?

Choose your model, then set the printing option and quantity in the configurator. Many keyrings can be designed online, or you can upload your own artwork. Approve your files before the upload deadline shown on the product page to keep your delivery date.

Are personalised keychains a good corporate gift?

Yes, especially when you choose a premium model. A wooden, leather-look or metal keychain with your logo feels valuable and gets used every day. Clients and staff see your brand each time they pick up their keys, long after they received the gift.

Which keyrings suit a trade show giveaway?

For large giveaways, choose a light and affordable model, such as a printed PET or budget keyring. Visitors are more likely to keep personalised keychains with a practical extra, such as a bottle opener or a trolley token.
