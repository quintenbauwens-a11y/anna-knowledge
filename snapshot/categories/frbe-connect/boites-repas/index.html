Are your personalised lunch boxes available in both plastic and glass materials?

Yes, HelloPrint Connect offers personalised lunch boxes made from durable plastic as well as premium glass. Both options can be customised with your logo or branding, ensuring your business finds the perfect match for your needs and sustainability values.

How do plastic and glass custom lunch boxes compare for daily business use?

Plastic branded lunchboxes are lightweight and shatter-resistant, ideal for everyday use and employee gifts. Glass lunchboxes with logo provide a sleek look and are preferred for executive environments, offering a high-end feel while keeping food fresh.

Why are branded lunchboxes beneficial for office environments?

Branded lunchboxes boost team morale, reinforce your company culture, and foster professionalism. Distributing custom lunch boxes with logo at your office encourages healthy eating habits and provides a practical, memorable gift to employees.

Can we bulk order personalised lunch boxes for corporate offices?

Absolutely. HelloPrint Connect specialises in bulk orders of personalised lunch boxes, allowing you to easily equip your entire staff or office event with high-quality, custom lunch boxes bearing your branding or logo.

How do custom lunch boxes enhance promotional campaigns at trade fairs?

Custom lunch boxes are standout giveaways at trade fairs, attracting attention to your booth and maximising your brand’s visibility. Practical and high-quality, lunchboxes with logo keep your brand top-of-mind long after the event ends.

Are branded lunchboxes suitable as corporate gifts during industry exhibitions?

Branded lunchboxes make excellent corporate gifts at exhibitions. Their practical use ensures your brand stays visible, while the ability to personalise them with your logo helps forge lasting connections with potential clients and partners.

Are personalised lunch boxes safe and convenient for school environments?

Yes, our personalised lunch boxes are designed with safety in mind, using BPA-free materials. They are convenient for schools, allowing students to keep their lunches fresh and easily identifiable with customised options.

Can schools use custom lunch boxes with logo for fundraising or student programs?

Custom lunch boxes with school logos are popular for fundraising and student initiatives. They foster school spirit, make great rewards for participation, and offer a useful product that parents and students will appreciate.
