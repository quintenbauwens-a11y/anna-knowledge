What techniques are used to print padded jackets?

Embroidery: This method involves sewing thread directly onto a garment to create your designs or logos. Embroidery is the most durable customization technique; it highlights your design, adds texture, and provides a high-quality appearance. The embroidered motif can include up to 6 colors.
