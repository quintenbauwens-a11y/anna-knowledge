Jackets and Parkas printing

Jackets and parkas are versatile, made from high-quality materials that can be customised to suit your brand's image. Whether you need reliable protection in cold and wet conditions or a stylish addition to your team's uniform, these garments offer a range of benefits.

Customising jackets and parkas for your team goes beyond keeping them comfortably warm; it's a powerful promotional tool at events, trade shows, and in the workplace. Enhance your brand identity and team spirit.

What techniques are used to print parka jackets?

Embroidery: This method involves sewing thread directly onto a garment to create your designs or logos. Embroidery is the most durable customisation technique; it highlights your design, adds texture, and provides a high-quality appearance. The embroidered motif can include up to 6 colours.
