    How can I easily remove stickers and adhesive residue?

    Removing stickers can sometimes be tricky, especially from surfaces like furniture, cars, windows, or walls. Start by gently peeling off one corner of the sticker. If needed, use a cloth with a bit of water or alcohol to loosen the adhesive. For larger stickers, place a clean cloth soaked in warm water over the sticker and let it sit for 5–10 minutes to soften the glue. For stubborn residue, dish soap or acetone can help achieve a clean finish.

    When ordering stickers, it’s important to consider how long they’ll be applied and where. Permanent stickers are harder to remove compared to temporary stickers designed for short-term promotions. If you need flexibility, choose Dot Foil material, which is easy to reposition and leaves no adhesive residue—perfect for quick application and hassle-free removal!
