## Bomber Jackets printing

Bomber jackets, also known as aviator jackets, are essential and timeless. Personalise them with your logo, text, or design for effective brand promotion and lasting impression. Gift promotional bomber jackets to your employees to strengthen team spirit and enhance visibility.

What techniques are used to print bomber jackets?

Embroidery: This method involves sewing thread directly onto a garment to create your designs or logos. Embroidery is the most durable customisation technique; it highlights your design, adds texture, and provides a high-quality appearance. The embroidered motif can include up to 6 colors.

Screen print: This is a popular method for clothing and textiles where ink is pushed through a mesh screen onto the fabric, creating vibrant and long-lasting prints. It's cost-effective, available from a quantity of 100 pieces, and allows for up to 4 colors.
