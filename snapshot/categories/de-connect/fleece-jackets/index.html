Fleece Jacket Printing

Stay warm all winter while promoting your business with a personalized fleece jacket! Fleece jackets are lightweight and made from fleece fabric, a type of synthetic polyester that retains heat while remaining breathable. An excellent choice for use as a mid-layer in cold conditions or as outerwear in milder weather.

Customizing a fleece jacket for your employees offers several benefits. In addition to keeping your team comfortably warm, it also serves as a promotional tool for your brand during events or trade shows and within the workplace. This helps strengthen not only your brand identity but also team spirit.

What techniques are used to print fleece jackets?

Embroidery: This method involves sewing thread directly onto a garment to create your designs or logos. Embroidery is the most durable customization technique; it highlights your design, adds texture, and provides a high-quality appearance. The embroidered motif can include up to 6 colors.
